## Section 1 When to Use

Use this script when: (a) the agent is making an outbound renewal reminder call to a Policyholder whose policy is approaching its expiry date; or (b) the agent receives an inbound enquiry from a Policyholder who is currently within the 30-day grace period following policy expiry and wishes to renew.

Before calling or responding, check the policy management system for: the policy number {policy_number}; the policy expiry date {expiry_date}; the renewal premium amount {renewal_premium}; whether the policy has already expired and, if so, the grace period end date {grace_period_end_date}; and whether any underwriting review is required at renewal.

If the policy has lapsed — meaning the grace period has ended without payment — do not use this script. Refer to SOP-RENEW-01 Section 4 for the lapsed policy process.

## Section 2 Customer Script

**Part A — Renewal reminder (policy not yet expired):**

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] calling from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. I am calling with reference to your health insurance policy number {policy_number}.

I wanted to let you know that your policy is due for renewal on {expiry_date}. The renewal premium for the coming year is ₹{renewal_premium}. To ensure your cover continues without a break, we would appreciate receiving your payment before the expiry date.

If you are unable to pay before {expiry_date}, please note that a grace period of 30 days is available after the expiry date during which you can still renew. However, no cover is available for any new hospitalisation that occurs during the grace period itself, so it is in your interest to renew promptly.

If you would like to change your sum insured option or add a rider at renewal, please let us know and we can arrange for the relevant team to assist you.

Would you like to make the payment today, or shall I note a preferred date for a follow-up call?"

---

**Part B — Customer calling during the grace period:**

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. I understand you are calling about policy number {policy_number}.

Your policy expired on {expiry_date}. The good news is that the 30-day grace period is still open. You can still renew and your Continuous Cover will be preserved, as though there was no break in your policy. However, please be aware that no cover is available for any hospitalisation that occurs during the grace period itself.

The renewal premium is ₹{renewal_premium}. The grace period ends on {grace_period_end_date}, after which the policy will lapse.

Would you like to proceed with the renewal payment today?"

## Section 3 Do and Do Not

DO: confirm the customer's identity before discussing policy details; check the system status before every call to ensure you are using the correct part of the script; clearly explain that no cover is available during the grace period for new events; offer to connect the customer with the underwriting team if they wish to change their sum insured or add a rider at renewal; note the call in the system.

DO NOT: imply that cover for new events is available during the grace period — this is a common customer misconception; offer to extend the grace period without supervisor approval and a confirmed underwriting review; quote renewal premium amounts or grace period end dates from memory — always check the system; promise renewal on the same terms if a health change has been disclosed or is pending review.
