## Section 1 When to Use

Use this script when a Policyholder or proposer contacts the Company to ask about transferring an existing health insurance policy from another registered insurer to Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. with portability credit. Use it also when explaining the portability process to a customer who is considering switching insurers and wants to understand how their waiting period history will be treated.

Before responding, check the system to see whether a portability application has already been submitted by this customer. If it has, check the application status in the underwriting system and confirm it to the customer using the follow-up part of this script. If the customer is making a first enquiry, use Part A.

## Section 2 Customer Script

**Part A — First enquiry about portability:**

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. I understand you are interested in transferring your existing health insurance policy to us. I am happy to explain how portability works.

Under the National Insurance Regulator's portability guidelines, if you have been with another registered health insurer and have maintained continuous cover without a lapse, you have the right to transfer to Aarogya Shield and receive credit for the waiting periods you have already served with your previous insurer. This means you do not have to restart your waiting period history from the beginning.

There is an important timing requirement: your portability application must be submitted to us at least 45 days before the renewal date of your existing policy with the other insurer. If we receive it less than 45 days before that date, we may not be able to process it in time, and you may lose the portability benefit.

To complete the application, you will need to provide: your existing policy documents and renewal certificates showing your cover history; and a completed proposal form from Aarogya Shield. Our underwriting team will then verify your prior cover and calculate the credit applicable to your waiting periods.

The portability credit applies to waiting period credits only. Your sum insured, premium and other terms will be set fresh under our policy. The specific credit applicable to each waiting period will be confirmed in writing once the underwriting review is complete.

Shall I send you the application form, or transfer you to our underwriting team to begin the process?

[Note the call and the customer's intent in the system.]"

---

**Part B — Follow-up on an existing portability application:**

"I can see that your portability application was received on [date from system]. Our underwriting team is currently reviewing your prior cover history. We will confirm the portability credit applicable to your waiting periods in writing once the review is complete. If you have any additional documents to support your application, please send them to [state channel] quoting your application reference number."

## Section 3 Do and Do Not

DO: explain the 45-day deadline clearly and prominently — this is the most common reason customers lose portability benefit; offer to send the application form or transfer the customer to the underwriting team; note the call in the system; direct detailed questions about specific waiting period credits to the underwriting team, not the service agent.

DO NOT: state specific waiting period month counts or promise a specific portability credit amount — the credit depends on the customer's individual cover history and the applicable policy wording version, and must be calculated and confirmed by the underwriting team after reviewing the documentation; confirm portability eligibility without the underwriting review being completed; accept a portability application verbally over the phone — a written application and documentary evidence of prior cover are required.
