## Section 1 When to Use

Use this script when an Insured Person or Policyholder contacts the Company to enquire about the current status of a submitted claim. This script applies to all claim types — cashless, reimbursement and Day Care — and to claims at any stage of the process.

Before taking the call or initiating the response, check the claims management system for the following: claim reference number {claim_id}; policy number {policy_number}; current claim status as shown in the system {claim_status_summary}; any outstanding action required from the customer; and whether the claim is under enhanced review. If the claim is under enhanced review, use only the holding statement in Section 2 Part D of this script and do not provide any other status detail.

If the claim status is a standard processing stage (documents pending, under assessment or settled), use the relevant part of the script below.

## Section 2 Customer Script

**Opening (all scenarios):**

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] calling from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. I am calling with reference to your health insurance claim number {claim_id} on policy number {policy_number}. Could you please confirm your date of birth so I can verify your identity?

[Confirm date of birth against system record. Proceed only after confirming identity.]

Thank you, {customer_name}. I can now provide an update on your claim."

---

**Part A — Documents pending:**

"Our team has reviewed the documents you have submitted so far for claim {claim_id}. Some documents are still outstanding before we can proceed with the assessment. I will read the list to you now.

[Read the list of outstanding documents from the claims management system.]

Could you please arrange to send these to us at your earliest convenience? Once we receive all the documents, we will begin the assessment and aim to complete it within the timelines required. You may submit them by [state available channels].

Is there anything on that list you need clarification on?"

---

**Part B — Under assessment:**

"Your claim {claim_id} is currently with our assessment team. All the documents we need have been received and our team is working through the assessment. We aim to complete assessments within the timelines required by the regulator. I will note your call and ensure the team is aware of your enquiry.

If you have not heard from us within a few days, please do not hesitate to call again. Is there anything else I can help you with?"

---

**Part C — Settled / payment made:**

"I am pleased to let you know that your claim {claim_id} has been settled. The eligible amount has been transferred to the bank account registered with us. The payment would typically appear in your account within [state standard bank processing time without quoting a specific day count].

A settlement letter confirming the amount paid and the basis for the settlement has been or will shortly be sent to your registered address or email.

If the amount is different from what you expected and you would like an explanation, I am happy to walk you through the settlement details, or I can arrange for our team to send you a written breakdown. Would you like that?"

---

**Part D — Under additional review (use this part ONLY when the system shows the claim is under enhanced review; do not add any other detail):**

"Your claim {claim_id} is currently under additional review. Our team will be in touch with you as soon as we have an update. If you have any questions in the meantime, please call our helpline and quote your claim reference {claim_id}.

Thank you for your patience."

---

**Closing (all scenarios except Part D):**

"Is there anything else I can help you with today, {customer_name}? Thank you for calling Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd."

## Section 3 Do and Do Not

DO: confirm the customer's identity before discussing any claim details; check the claims management system status before the call or at the start of the call; offer to send information in writing where the customer wants a record; note the call in the claims management system under the claim reference; use Part D for any claim showing an enhanced review status, without deviation.

DO NOT: use Part D and then add further explanation about why the claim is under review — the holding statement must be the complete response for that scenario; speculate about the likely outcome of an assessment that is still in progress; quote specific settlement amounts that have not yet been finalised in the system; promise a specific date by which the assessment will be completed; mention fraud, investigation or any specialist review team under any circumstances.
