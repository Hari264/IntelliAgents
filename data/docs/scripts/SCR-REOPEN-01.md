## Section 1 When to Use

Use this script when a Policyholder or Insured Person contacts the service team to request that a closed claim be reopened for reassessment. A claim may be closed because it was settled, partially settled or rejected. This script is used to explain the reopening process, take the customer's reason for the request, and pass the matter to the claims team for review.

Before responding, check the claims management system for: claim reference {claim_id}; policy number {policy_number}; the current status of the claim; the date of closure; and the reason for closure. The grounds on which a claim may be reopened are set out in SOP-REOPEN-01; review them before the call so you can give the customer accurate guidance.

This script does not apply to grievances. If the customer is expressing general dissatisfaction with the claim outcome and wishes to register a formal grievance, use SCR-GRIEV-01 instead.

## Section 2 Customer Script

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. I understand you are calling about your health insurance claim number {claim_id} on policy number {policy_number}, which was closed on [date from system]. I would like to understand your reason for requesting a reopening.

Could you please tell me why you believe the claim should be reviewed again?

[Listen carefully and note the full reason.]

Thank you. I have noted your reason as follows: [read back a brief summary]. Is that correct?

[If yes:] I will pass your request to our claims team for review. The team will assess whether your request meets the grounds for reopening under our claims process — for example, if there is new evidence that was not available when the claim was closed, or if an error was identified in the assessment.

We will contact you in writing once the team has reviewed your request and reached a decision. If the claim is reopened, a fresh assessment will be carried out and you will receive a new decision in writing.

Please note that a request to reopen is not the same as a grievance. If you would also like to register a formal grievance about the original decision, I can arrange that separately and in parallel. Would you like me to do that?

[Note the request in the claims management system under claim {claim_id} with the reason provided.]

Is there anything else I can help you with?"

## Section 3 Do and Do Not

DO: listen to the customer's full reason before reading back a summary; accurately record the stated reason in the claims management system; explain that the request will be reviewed against the grounds in the claims process; offer to register a parallel grievance if the customer wishes; note the call promptly in the system.

DO NOT: promise the customer that the claim will be reopened or that the outcome will change; explain the internal criteria for reopening in detail — this is an internal process document and should not be communicated verbatim to customers; tell the customer the claim will not be reopened without the claims team having reviewed the request; use this script where the customer's concern is best directed through the grievance route — if in doubt, offer both options.
