## Section 1 When to Use

Use this script when a Policyholder or Insured Person contacts the Company to register a formal grievance about a claim decision, a service interaction, a renewal matter, a document request or any other aspect of their policy or claim experience. This script applies to inbound calls, walk-in visits and written or email grievances received through the customer portal.

Before proceeding, check the claims management system for the policy number and, where applicable, the claim reference number. Confirm the customer's identity before discussing any grievance details. If the customer is calling to follow up on an existing grievance, check the grievance register for the current status and use the closing part of the script below.

## Section 2 Customer Script

**Registering a new grievance:**

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. I understand you would like to register a grievance regarding [policy number {policy_number} / claim reference {claim_id}]. I am sorry to hear you have had an unsatisfactory experience, and I want to ensure your concern is properly recorded and addressed.

May I ask you to describe the nature of your grievance so I can note it accurately?

[Listen carefully and note the full details of the customer's concern without interrupting.]

Thank you. I have noted your grievance as follows: [read back a brief summary]. Is that an accurate description of your concern?

[If yes:] I am registering this as a formal grievance now. Your grievance reference number is {grievance_ref}. Please keep this number for your records — you will need it in any future correspondence.

Under our regulatory obligations, we will acknowledge your grievance in writing within 3 working days of today. We will then work to resolve it within 14 days of receipt. If we need additional time, we will inform you in writing with the reason for the extension.

If, after receiving our response, you are not satisfied with our resolution, you have the right to escalate the matter to the Insurance Ombudsman. I can provide those contact details now or include them in our written acknowledgement. Would you prefer to receive them now?

[Provide Ombudsman contact details if requested.]

Is there anything else I can assist you with today?"

---

**Following up on an existing grievance:**

"I can see that your grievance with reference {grievance_ref} was registered on [date from system]. Our team is working on the resolution and you should receive a full response within 14 days of the registration date. If you would like to add any further information to your grievance, I can note it now and pass it to the team. Would you like to do that?"

## Section 3 Do and Do Not

DO: listen to the full grievance before reading back a summary; always provide the grievance reference number before ending the call; clearly state the 3-working-day acknowledgement and 14-day resolution timelines; note the call in the claims management system under the grievance reference; inform the customer of their right to escalate to the Ombudsman.

DO NOT: tell the customer whether the grievance will be upheld or what the resolution is likely to be; dismiss any part of the customer's concern as invalid or unworthy of a formal response; offer to resolve the matter informally to avoid a formal registration — all expressed dissatisfaction must be formally logged; promise an outcome or apologise for a decision on behalf of the team before the Grievance Redressal Officer has reviewed the case.
