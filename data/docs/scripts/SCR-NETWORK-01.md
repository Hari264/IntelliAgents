## Section 1 When to Use

Use this script when a Policyholder or Insured Person contacts the service team to: ask how to find a Network Hospital before a planned or emergency hospitalisation; check whether a specific hospital is on the Company's current Network Hospital list; or understand what to do if a preferred hospital is not on the network.

Before responding, check the claims management system for the current network list. If the customer has named a specific hospital, verify its network status in the system before confirming it to the customer. Network membership changes periodically and must not be confirmed from memory.

This script also applies to cashless enquiries from customers who have already been admitted and are calling to understand their options.

## Section 2 Customer Script

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. I understand you are looking for information about Network Hospitals under your policy number {policy_number}. I am happy to help.

Under your policy, the cashless facility is available only at hospitals that are on our current Network Hospital list. At a Network Hospital, we settle the eligible portion of your bill directly with the hospital after pre-authorisation, so you do not need to pay upfront. If you receive treatment at a hospital that is not on the network, you will need to pay the hospital directly and then submit a reimbursement claim to us afterwards.

The easiest way to find a Network Hospital near you is through our website, where you can search the full network list by city or area. You can also ask our helpline team to search for you while you are on the call.

[If the customer has named a specific hospital:]

You have asked about [name of hospital] in {city_or_area}. Let me check our current list. [Check system.]

[If on network:] That hospital is currently on our network. You can use the cashless facility there for covered treatments. For a planned admission, please notify us at least 48 hours in advance. For an emergency, please notify us within 24 hours of admission. I can walk you through the notification process if you would like.

[If not on network:] I am sorry, that hospital is not currently on our network. If you receive treatment there, you would pay the hospital and then submit a reimbursement claim to us within 30 days of discharge. I can explain the reimbursement process in detail if that would help.

Is there anything else I can assist you with?"

## Section 3 Do and Do Not

DO: always check the current network list in the claims management system before confirming network status — never confirm from memory; remind the customer of the 48-hour notification requirement for planned admissions and the 24-hour requirement for emergencies; offer to explain the reimbursement route if the customer's preferred hospital is not on the network; direct the customer to the website for the full searchable list.

DO NOT: confirm network status for a specific hospital without checking the system — membership can change; guarantee that a hospital will remain on the network for the entire duration of a planned course of treatment; discuss cashless eligibility for specific procedures without noting that waiting periods under the applicable Policy Wording may apply; quote the network list from an outdated or printed copy.
