## Section 1 When to Use

Use this script when: (a) an agent is making an outbound call to confirm to the Policyholder that documents submitted in support of a reimbursement claim have been received; or (b) a Policyholder contacts the service team to check whether their submitted documents have been received and what the next step is.

Before calling or responding, retrieve from the claims management system: the claim reference {claim_id}; the list of documents received {document_list}; the date those documents were received {received_date}; and whether any further documents are outstanding. If outstanding documents remain, prepare the list of missing items so you can communicate them clearly.

This script is for confirming receipt only. For the more detailed document deficiency process, refer to SOP-DOCS-01.

## Section 2 Customer Script

**Part A — All required documents received:**

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. I am calling with reference to your health insurance claim number {claim_id}.

I am pleased to confirm that we have received the following documents from you on {received_date}: [read document list from system].

All the documents we need for the assessment of your claim have now been received. Our assessment team will review them, and we aim to complete the assessment within 30 days of receiving the last document, in line with our regulatory obligations. We will be in touch with you once the assessment is complete.

If you have any questions in the meantime, please call our helpline and quote your claim reference {claim_id}."

---

**Part B — Some documents still outstanding:**

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. I am calling with reference to your health insurance claim number {claim_id}.

We have received the following documents: [read the received list from system].

However, the following items are still outstanding: [read the missing document list]. We will not be able to begin the assessment until all required documents are received.

Could you please arrange to send these to us at your earliest convenience? You may submit them by [state available channels]. Once we receive everything, the assessment will begin promptly.

Is there anything on that list you need clarification about, or any item you are having difficulty obtaining?"

## Section 3 Do and Do Not

DO: confirm the exact list of documents received against the claims management system record; read the outstanding items clearly and specifically if any are missing; offer to clarify what each document is and how it can be obtained or submitted; note the call in the system; use "30 days" only in reference to the regulatory assessment timeline, not as a promise of a specific resolution date.

DO NOT: tell the customer that the claim will be settled or approved once the documents are complete — the outcome depends on the assessment; commit to a specific settlement date; accept verbal confirmation that a document is "on its way" as a substitute for actually receiving it in the system; ignore an item on the outstanding list or describe it as optional without supervisor confirmation.
