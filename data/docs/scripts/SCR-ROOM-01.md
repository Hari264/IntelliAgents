## Section 1 When to Use

Use this script when a claim has been settled for less than the amount claimed because of a room rent limit under CL-305 (Room rent exceeds eligible limit) or a proportionate deduction under CL-306 (Proportionate deduction applied to associated expenses).

Before calling, retrieve from the claims management system: the claim reference {claim_id}; the applicable rejection code or codes {rejection_code}; the eligible room rent amount under the policy {eligible_room_rent}; the actual room rent charged by the hospital {actual_room_rent}; and the settlement breakdown showing the proportionate deduction calculation. The clause references are Clause 8.1 for room rent limit and Clause 8.2 for proportionate deduction.

Note: if both CL-305 and CL-306 apply to the same claim, address both in a single call.

## Section 2 Customer Script

"Good [morning / afternoon / evening]. May I please speak with {customer_name}?

Thank you. This is [your name] calling from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. regarding your health insurance claim number {claim_id}. I am calling to explain the settlement amount for your claim and how it was calculated.

I understand you may have questions about the difference between the amount you claimed and the amount we have paid. Let me explain.

Your health insurance policy includes a room rent limit under Clause 8.1 of your Policy Wording. The eligible room rent under your policy is ₹{eligible_room_rent} per day. During your hospitalisation, the room you occupied was charged at ₹{actual_room_rent} per day.

[For CL-305 only:] Because the room rent charged is above the eligible limit, we have applied rejection code {rejection_code}. The room rent amount above ₹{eligible_room_rent} per day is not payable under your policy.

[For CL-306 only, or when both codes apply:] When the room chosen is above the eligible limit, your policy also requires a proportionate adjustment to the associated medical expenses under Clause 8.2. This means that expenses such as surgeon fees, consultant charges, operation theatre charges and nursing charges are reduced in the same proportion as the room rent excess. Your pharmacy charges, implant costs and consumable costs are not affected by this deduction.

I can send you a detailed written breakdown of exactly how the deduction has been calculated if that would help. Would you like me to send that to your registered email address?

If you feel that this assessment is incorrect, you have the right to raise a written grievance with our Grievance Redressal Officer. I can give you those details now. The Grievance Redressal Officer will review your case independently.

Is there anything else I can help you with?"

## Section 3 Do and Do Not

DO: have the room rent calculation open in the system before calling; use the actual rupee figures {eligible_room_rent} and {actual_room_rent} to explain — do not quote percentages from the policy wording; offer to send the written calculation; provide the Grievance Redressal Officer contact details; document the call in the claims management system.

DO NOT: quote the room rent percentage from the policy wording — the eligible amount varies by policy version and sum insured, so always use the calculated rupee figure; tell the customer the deduction will be reversed; imply the hospital should have informed the customer about the limit; make any promise about a revised settlement; use the word "penalty" — the deduction is a policy term, not a penalty.
