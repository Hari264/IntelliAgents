## Section 1 Purpose

This guideline defines the age bands used for health insurance underwriting and premium rating at Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. and describes the principles that govern the application of age-based loadings. It applies to all individual and family health insurance proposals and renewals.

Age is one of the primary rating factors in health insurance. The risk of hospitalisation and the severity of health conditions generally increases with age. Age-based premium differentiation enables the Company to offer affordable premiums to younger proposers while ensuring that premiums for older insured persons are commensurate with the additional risk they represent. Underwriters must apply age-based loadings consistently and in accordance with the Company's rating manual.

## Section 2 Age Bands

The Company uses the following age bands for health insurance premium rating and underwriting. The age of the Insured Person on the date of policy inception (or renewal) is used to determine the applicable age band.

Age band 1: 18 to 35 years.
Age band 2: 36 to 45 years.
Age band 3: 46 to 55 years.
Age band 4: 56 to 65 years.
Age band 5: 66 years and above.

For family floater policies, the age band applicable to the eldest Insured Person covered under the floater governs the base premium for the floater. Individual members of a floater may carry their own age band for the purpose of medical test requirements.

Children and dependants below 18 years of age are rated in accordance with the Company's rating manual applicable to dependent children, which is a separate schedule not covered by this guideline.

## Section 3 Loading Principles

Age-based loadings are applied in addition to any health risk class loading determined under UW-RISK-01 and any occupational loading determined under UW-OCC-01. The loading for each age band is specified in the Company's rating manual. Underwriters must not deviate from the rating manual without the approval of the Senior Underwriter.

The following principles govern the application of age-based loadings.

3.1 Loading increases progressively with each age band. A proposer moving from one age band to the next at renewal will attract the loading applicable to the new age band, even if their health status remains unchanged.

3.2 For policies covering multiple Insured Persons on a family floater basis, the eldest member's age band determines the floater premium. Where the eldest member exits the policy at renewal, the premium is recalculated based on the age band of the new eldest member.

3.3 Age-based loadings are actuarially determined and are reviewed periodically by the actuarial team. Underwriters must use the most recent version of the rating manual. Where a loading in the rating manual has been superseded, the updated loading applies from the next renewal date.

3.4 Age is not, on its own, a ground for declining a proposal. A proposal from a proposer in age band 5 must be assessed on its merits under UW-RISK-01 and declined only if the health risk criteria for Class D are met.

## Section 4 Exceptions

The following exceptions apply to the general age-band loading principles.

4.1 Continuous renewal: a proposer who has maintained Continuous Cover with the Company since before age 66 and who renews without a break continues to attract the loading applicable to age band 5. Renewal must not be declined solely on the basis of age, provided the proposer has maintained Continuous Cover.

4.2 Portability: where an Insured Person transfers from another insurer under the NIR's portability framework, the age band applicable on the date of transfer governs the premium. Portability credit under Clause 9.2 of the applicable Policy Wording applies to waiting periods, not to age-band loadings. The loading is determined afresh based on the proposer's current age.

4.3 Mid-year birthdays on floater policies: where an Insured Person on a family floater crosses an age-band threshold during the policy year, the loading for the new age band applies at the next renewal, not mid-year.

4.4 Waiver of loading: an age-based loading may not be waived without the approval of the Senior Underwriter and a documented actuarial justification. Any waiver must be endorsed on the Policy Schedule.
