## Section 1 Purpose

This guideline sets out the checklist for onboarding a new group health insurance policy issued by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. Group policies cover a defined set of members under a single master policy held by the group holder, which is typically an employer, association or similar body. This guideline covers the documents required from the group holder, the checks to be performed on member data, and the steps required before the master policy and member certificates are issued. It applies to new group policies and to renewals where the member composition has materially changed.

## Section 2 Documents from the Group Holder

2.1 Before an underwriting decision can be made on a group policy, the group holder must submit the following: (a) a completed and signed master policy application form, authorised by a person with authority to bind the group holder; (b) a member data file in the format specified by the Company, listing all proposed Insured Persons with their names, dates of birth, gender, proposed sum insured option and their relationship to the group holder; (c) an authorisation letter on the group holder's letterhead confirming that the members listed have consented to being covered under the policy; and (d) payment instructions and confirmation of the total premium payable.

2.2 The underwriter must check that the application form is complete and signed before requesting the member data. Incomplete application forms must be returned to the group holder under the process in UW-FORM-01.

2.3 For group policies with members who have declared Pre-Existing Diseases, the relevant PED declarations and any supporting medical evidence must be submitted in accordance with UW-PED-01.

## Section 3 Member Data Checks

3.1 On receipt of the member data file, the underwriter must perform the following data quality checks before proceeding to issuance: (a) confirm that every member record contains a full name, date of birth, gender and the proposed sum insured option; (b) assign the correct age band under UW-AGE-01 to each member based on their date of birth; (c) check for duplicate entries — the same person may not be listed more than once on the same policy; (d) verify that the proposed sum insured option for each member falls within the available options for the product under which the group is to be insured.

3.2 For members aged 46 and above, the underwriter must flag the requirement for pre-policy medical tests in accordance with UW-MEDTEST-01. Where the group size makes individual testing impractical, the underwriter must seek guidance from the Senior Underwriter on the appropriate approach.

3.3 Where a member's declared health status requires underwriting review under UW-RISK-01, the underwriter must assess that member separately before confirming their inclusion in the group policy.

3.4 Any member whose data fails the checks in this Section must be queried with the group holder before the master policy is issued.

## Section 4 Issuance Steps

4.1 Once all member data has been verified and the underwriting review is complete, the underwriter must: (a) calculate the total premium payable, based on the sum insured options and age bands of all confirmed members; (b) obtain the requisite approval from the Senior Underwriter where the group size or risk profile requires senior sign-off; and (c) confirm the premium with the group holder before issuing the policy.

4.2 On receipt of the first premium, the underwriter must initiate the issuance of the master policy document and individual member certificates. Each member certificate must clearly state the member's name, sum insured, the policy period and any exclusions or loadings applicable to that individual.

4.3 A welcome communication must be sent to the group holder, including the master policy number, the network hospital access arrangements and the contact details for making claims and for the customer helpline.

4.4 All onboarding documents, member data files and underwriting decisions must be retained in the policy file in accordance with the Company's document retention policy.
