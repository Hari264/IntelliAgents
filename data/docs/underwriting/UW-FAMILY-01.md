## Section 1 Purpose

This guideline defines the composition rules for family floater health insurance policies issued by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. Under a family floater policy, all covered family members share a single sum insured. The premium is calculated based on the age band of the eldest Insured Person, as governed by UW-AGE-01. This guideline applies to Health Gold and Health Silver family floater policies and must be read alongside UW-RISK-01, UW-AGE-01 and UW-MEDTEST-01.

## Section 2 Who Can Be Covered

2.1 The following family members may be covered under a single family floater policy: (a) the principal Policyholder; (b) the Policyholder's legally recognised spouse; (c) dependent children who are unmarried and have not attained the maximum age for dependent cover as specified in the product guidelines; and (d) dependent parents of the Policyholder or the Policyholder's spouse.

2.2 Children may be covered from the minimum entry age specified in the product guidelines. Children who have attained age 18 at inception must be proposed as independent policyholders in their own right, not as dependants on the family floater.

2.3 Dependent parents may be covered on the same floater as the Policyholder. Where the age difference between the eldest parent and the youngest covered child results in a material premium impact, the underwriter must flag the composition for review by the Senior Underwriter before issue.

2.4 Each Insured Person named in a family floater must separately satisfy the health underwriting criteria in UW-RISK-01. Where one member of the proposed family floater is assessed at a loaded or sub-standard risk class, the loading or exclusion is applied to that member's portion of cover only, unless the Senior Underwriter determines otherwise.

## Section 3 Adding or Removing Members

3.1 New family members may be added to the family floater at renewal. Mid-term additions are limited to: (a) a newly married spouse, upon submission of a marriage certificate; (b) a newly born child, upon submission of a birth certificate; and (c) a family member who was previously excluded from cover and whose exclusion has been reviewed and lifted by the underwriting team.

3.2 To add a new member mid-term, the Policyholder must submit a written endorsement request with the supporting documents and a completed health declaration for the new member. A pro-rated additional premium is charged for the remaining Policy Year. The underwriter must assess the new member under UW-RISK-01 before confirming the addition.

3.3 Members may be removed from the family floater at renewal. Mid-term removal is not ordinarily permitted, except where the removed member takes out a separate individual policy with the Company or transfers to another insurer under the portability framework in SOP-PORT-01.

3.4 When the eldest member exits the family floater at renewal, the premium for the renewed policy is recalculated based on the age band of the new eldest Insured Person in accordance with UW-AGE-01.

## Section 4 Documentation

4.1 To add a member at inception or renewal, the following documents must be provided: a completed and signed proposal form or endorsement request form for the new member; a health declaration; and proof of relationship, such as a birth certificate, marriage certificate or adoption order, as applicable.

4.2 For additions of dependent children below the age specified in the product guidelines for mandatory medical tests, a health declaration from the parent or guardian is sufficient. For children who have attained the age requiring tests under UW-MEDTEST-01, a full test panel is required.

4.3 For additions of dependent parents, the age band of the parent at the date of addition governs whether medical tests are required under UW-MEDTEST-01.

4.4 All changes to the family floater composition must be reflected in an updated Policy Schedule issued to the Policyholder. The revised Schedule must clearly list all current Insured Persons, their dates of birth and any exclusions or loadings applicable to each.
