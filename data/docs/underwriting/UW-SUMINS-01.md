## Section 1 Purpose

This guideline governs applications to increase the sum insured on an existing health insurance policy issued by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. A sum insured enhancement is a request by the Policyholder to move from their current sum insured option to a higher option within the product's available range. The guideline applies to individual, family floater and group health policies and must be read alongside UW-RISK-01 for risk classification, UW-MEDTEST-01 for medical test requirements and UW-RENEW-01 for the renewal context in which enhancements are most commonly requested.

## Section 2 Eligibility for Enhancement

2.1 A Policyholder may apply for a sum insured enhancement at any renewal. Mid-term enhancements are not available; the application must be submitted and approved before the renewal date of the current policy.

2.2 The Policyholder must have maintained Continuous Cover without a lapse during the period preceding the enhancement application. A Policyholder whose policy has lapsed and been reinstated must complete a further period of uninterrupted cover, as determined by the Senior Underwriter, before an enhancement is considered.

2.3 Available sum insured options under Health Gold are ₹3,00,000; ₹5,00,000; ₹10,00,000; and ₹25,00,000. For Health Silver, the options are ₹2,00,000; ₹3,00,000; and ₹5,00,000. The Policyholder may apply to move to any higher option within the applicable product's range in a single step or incrementally.

2.4 An enhancement is treated as a fresh underwriting decision for the incremental increase in cover. The existing sum insured continues on its existing terms; only the additional cover increment is subject to fresh underwriting assessment and, where applicable, fresh waiting periods.

## Section 3 Evidence Required

3.1 The Policyholder must complete a declaration of good health at the time of the enhancement application. The declaration must confirm any changes in the health status of each Insured Person since the original policy or most recent renewal, and must address any new diagnoses, treatments or hospitalisations that have occurred.

3.2 For Insured Persons aged 46 and above, pre-policy medical tests are mandatory in accordance with UW-MEDTEST-01. Test results obtained within 6 months before the date of the enhancement application may be accepted without a fresh test; results older than 6 months require a new set of tests.

3.3 Where the enhancement results in a sum insured at the highest available option for the product, the standard medical test panel may be supplemented by additional tests at the discretion of the Senior Underwriter or the Company's Medical Officer.

3.4 Where the Policyholder has made claims during the current Policy Year, the underwriter must review those claims as part of the enhancement assessment, applying the claims history principles in UW-RENEW-01 Section 3.

## Section 4 Referral Rules

4.1 Enhancements must be referred to the Senior Underwriter where: (a) the Insured Person is in age band 56-65 or 66+; (b) the declaration of good health discloses a new significant health condition; (c) the claims history during the current policy term indicates a materially elevated risk profile; or (d) the requested enhancement would bring the sum insured to the highest available option and the Insured Person has had an in-patient hospitalisation claim in the current Policy Year.

4.2 An enhancement application may not be declined without written communication to the Policyholder explaining the basis for the decline.

4.3 All enhancement decisions must be documented in the policy file and reflected in a revised Policy Schedule issued to the Policyholder promptly after the decision is confirmed.
