## Section 1 Purpose

This guideline defines the underwriting review process for annual renewals of health insurance policies issued by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. At renewal, the underwriting team assesses whether the policy should be renewed on existing terms, renewed with modifications or referred for senior review. The guideline applies to individual, family floater and group health policies and must be read alongside UW-RISK-01 for general risk classification principles and UW-SUMINS-01 where the Policyholder requests a sum insured enhancement at renewal.

## Section 2 Renewal Review Scope

2.1 At each renewal, the underwriter must review: (a) the claims history for the Policy Year being closed; (b) any change in the Insured Person's health status disclosed by the Policyholder on the renewal proposal or health declaration; (c) any request for a sum insured enhancement; (d) any request to add or remove family members on a floater policy; and (e) whether any Insured Person has moved into a higher age band since the previous renewal.

2.2 A standard renewal for a Policy Year in which no claims were made and no health changes have been disclosed proceeds at standard terms without additional underwriting review, unless the Insured Person moves into a higher age band, in which case UW-AGE-01 applies.

2.3 Where claims have been made in the Policy Year, the underwriter must assess the nature, frequency and total amount of claims relative to the sum insured. No specific claims count triggers automatic adverse action; claims history is assessed holistically in accordance with Section 3.

2.4 Where the Policyholder has disclosed a new health condition since the last renewal, the underwriter must assess the condition under UW-RISK-01 and determine whether the policy should be renewed at standard terms, with a loading or exclusion, or referred to the Senior Underwriter.

## Section 3 Handling Claims History at Renewal

3.1 Claims history is a relevant factor in the renewal assessment but must not be the sole basis for a loading or renewal decline. The underwriter must consider the clinical nature of the claims, the sum insured consumed and the period of Continuous Cover maintained by the Policyholder.

3.2 A single large claim in the Policy Year does not automatically attract a loading at renewal. The underwriter must distinguish between claims arising from an acute or accidental event — which may not indicate an ongoing risk — and claims arising from a chronic or progressive condition, which may indicate a genuinely elevated ongoing risk.

3.3 Where claims history suggests an underlying condition that was not declared on the original proposal form, the underwriter must refer the matter to the team supervisor and, if appropriate, to UW-PED-01 and SOP-PEDVER-01 for a non-disclosure review before the renewal decision is finalised.

3.4 The underwriter must document the claims history assessment in the policy file, including the conclusion reached and the basis for it, so that the rationale is available for future renewals.

## Section 4 Referral Rules

4.1 Renewals must be referred to the Senior Underwriter in the following situations: (a) the Policyholder has disclosed a new serious health condition at renewal; (b) the claims history assessment indicates a risk profile that cannot be accommodated within standard terms; (c) an Insured Person moves into age band 66+ for the first time; and (d) the Policyholder has requested a sum insured enhancement and the conditions for referral in UW-SUMINS-01 Section 4 are met.

4.2 A renewal must not be declined without Senior Underwriter approval. The decline must be communicated to the Policyholder in writing, with the reason stated, and before the policy expiry date wherever possible.

4.3 All referral decisions and their outcomes must be recorded in the policy file and are subject to quality audit under SOP-QUALITY-01.
