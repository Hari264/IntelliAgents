## Section 1 Background

The National Insurance Regulator issued this circular to address deficiencies in renewal communication practices across the health insurance sector. The NIR found that policyholders were sometimes not receiving timely renewal notices, were not being clearly informed about changes to their policy terms at renewal, and were not receiving their policy documents in a form that allowed them to understand and exercise their rights.

This document is an internal summary of the NIR circular for the guidance of claims, service and underwriting staff at Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. For claim settlement timelines, refer to CIR-01. For rejection communication requirements, refer to CIR-02. For grievance timelines, refer to CIR-03. The Company's internal process for renewal notices and lapsed policies is set out in SOP-RENEW-01.

## Section 2 Renewal Notice Requirements

2.1 The NIR requires that health insurers issue renewal notices to policyholders sufficiently in advance of the policy expiry date to allow the policyholder to arrange payment or to seek cover elsewhere if they choose not to renew. The advance notice period is as stated in the applicable Policy Wording.

2.2 The renewal notice must state clearly: the renewal premium due; the policy expiry date; the grace period available after the expiry date; and any changes to the premium, terms, exclusions or sub-limits that will apply at renewal.

2.3 Changes to terms at renewal must be communicated with sufficient prominence so that the Policyholder is aware of them before deciding to renew. A renewal notice that does not clearly disclose premium changes or material term changes is treated as deficient by the NIR and may be the subject of a complaint or regulatory finding.

2.4 The NIR acknowledges that the Policyholder bears primary responsibility for timely renewal. However, the insurer must demonstrate that it fulfilled its obligation to issue a timely and complete renewal notice before treating a failure to renew as a lapse under the Policy Wording.

## Section 3 Policy Document Delivery

3.1 The NIR requires that insurers provide the full policy wording to the Policyholder at or before the time of inception of a new policy or renewal. Electronic delivery is permissible where the Policyholder has consented to receiving documents electronically.

3.2 Policyholders must be able to access the policy wording in a form they can read, retain and reference. Where the Policyholder requests a physical copy, the insurer must provide one without charge.

3.3 At renewal, if the policy terms have changed, the insurer must provide the updated policy wording or a written summary of the changes with sufficient clarity for a lay policyholder to understand what has changed. It is not sufficient to refer the Policyholder to the insurer's website without providing direct access to the relevant document or summary.

3.4 The welcome kit or renewal confirmation issued to the Policyholder must include the contact details of the insurer's customer helpline, the Grievance Redressal Officer and information about how to make a claim.

## Section 4 Changes to Terms at Renewal

4.1 The NIR requires that any change to the premium, sum insured, exclusions, sub-limits or waiting periods at renewal be communicated to the Policyholder in writing before the renewal takes effect.

4.2 Where a change results from a revision to the insurer's product wording — such as the modification of an existing benefit or the addition of a new sub-limit — the Policyholder must be given adequate notice to make an informed decision about whether to renew on the new terms.

4.3 Policyholders who raise a complaint about the content or timing of a renewal notice, or about changes to terms at renewal, must be directed to the Grievance Redressal Officer as described in Clause 10.1 of the applicable Policy Wording. The insurer must acknowledge all such grievances within 3 working days and resolve them within 14 days, as required by CIR-03.

4.4 Where a Policyholder chooses not to renew because the revised terms are not acceptable, the insurer must acknowledge the Policyholder's decision in writing and confirm the policy lapse date and any premium refund owing. The lapse must be recorded in the policy management system in accordance with SOP-RENEW-01.
