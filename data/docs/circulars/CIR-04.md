## Section 1 Background

The National Insurance Regulator (NIR) issued this circular to ensure that hospitals empanelled in the cashless networks of registered health insurers meet minimum quality and service standards. The circular was prompted by Policyholder complaints about cashless facilities being denied or delayed at network hospitals owing to administrative deficiencies, inadequate facilities or failure to adhere to agreed billing terms.

This document is an internal summary of the NIR circular for the guidance of claims, service and underwriting staff at Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. For claim settlement timelines, rejection communication requirements and grievance timelines, refer to the summaries in CIR-01, CIR-02 and CIR-03. The Company's internal process for empanelling and reviewing network hospitals is set out in SOP-NETWORK-01.

## Section 2 Standards for Network Hospitals

2.1 The NIR requires that health insurers empanel only hospitals that meet the statutory minimum requirements for a registered Hospital, as defined in the insurer's Policy Wording and applicable regulatory requirements. Hospitals must be duly registered with the appropriate state or central authority and must be equipped with the facilities, staff and infrastructure necessary to provide safe in-patient medical care.

2.2 Hospitals on the network must adhere to the tariff rates and billing standards agreed with the insurer under the provider agreement. The NIR has identified overbilling — particularly charges above agreed tariff rates — as a persistent source of Policyholder complaints. Insurers are required to have documented processes to monitor and address tariff deviations.

2.3 Network hospitals must maintain patient records in a manner that enables the insurer to verify claims. Failure to produce records upon reasonable request constitutes a deficiency in meeting empanelment criteria.

2.4 The NIR expects insurers to ensure that the cashless pre-authorisation and discharge authorisation process operates smoothly at network hospitals, with decisions communicated to the hospital promptly and in line with the timelines set out in CIR-01.

## Section 3 Transparency to Policyholders

3.1 The NIR requires that insurers make the network hospital list available to Policyholders in a readily accessible format. The list must be maintained on the insurer's website and available through the customer helpline.

3.2 Policyholders must be informed at the time of purchasing a policy how they can identify a network hospital in their area. This information must appear in the policy wording or in the welcome communication issued at inception.

3.3 Where a hospital is removed from the network, the insurer must update the publicly available network list promptly. Policyholders who enquire about network status must be given accurate and current information.

3.4 The insurer must communicate to Policyholders what steps they should take if cashless treatment at a network hospital is not available, including the reimbursement route available under the Policy Wording.

## Section 4 Supervisory Review

4.1 The NIR expects registered health insurers to conduct periodic reviews of the hospitals on their network to verify continued adherence to empanelment criteria. The regulator may request evidence of review activity during supervisory visits or in response to Policyholder complaints.

4.2 Where a network hospital is found to be non-compliant with empanelment criteria, the insurer must take prompt corrective action, including removal from the network where warranted. The insurer must document the basis for removal and retain those records.

4.3 Insurers must maintain records of empanelment decisions, periodic review findings and any action taken as a result. These records must be available for inspection by the NIR on request.

4.4 Policyholders who raise a complaint about the standard of care or the cashless process at a network hospital must be directed to the Grievance Redressal Officer as described in Clause 10.1 of the applicable Policy Wording and in CIR-03.
