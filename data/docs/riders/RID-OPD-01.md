## Clause 1 Rider Scope

1.1 This Rider, when endorsed on the Policy Schedule, extends cover under the Health Gold policy to include outpatient medical expenses as described in Clause 2 of this Rider. Without this Rider, outpatient consultations, diagnostic tests and medicines incurred outside a hospitalisation event are not covered under the base Health Gold policy.

1.2 This Rider provides supplementary cover for outpatient care only. In-patient Hospitalisation, Day Care Procedures, Domiciliary Hospitalisation and all other benefits under the base Health Gold Policy Wording continue to be governed by the applicable Policy Wording and are not affected by this Rider.

1.3 For the purposes of this Rider, outpatient care means consultations, diagnostic investigations and medicines obtained at a registered medical facility or pharmacy without an associated in-patient or day care admission on the same date.

1.4 Defined terms used in this Rider have the same meaning as in the applicable Health Gold Policy Wording unless otherwise stated.

## Clause 2 Benefit

2.1 Where this Rider is shown in the Policy Schedule, the Company will reimburse outpatient expenses incurred by the Insured Person for: (a) consultations with a qualified registered medical practitioner at a registered clinic or hospital for any covered condition; (b) diagnostic investigations (pathology, radiology and other tests) ordered by the treating physician; and (c) medicines prescribed by the treating physician for the same condition.

2.2 The outpatient benefit under this Rider is subject to the annual benefit limit stated in the Policy Schedule. The annual limit is the maximum aggregate amount payable under this Rider in a Policy Year, regardless of the number of outpatient visits or conditions treated.

2.3 The outpatient benefit is reimbursed on submission of original bills, prescriptions and consultation notes. Claims must be submitted to the Company within 30 days of the date of the outpatient visit.

2.4 The annual outpatient benefit limit does not increase the base health policy's sum insured. It is a separate benefit limit applicable only to outpatient expenses under this Rider.

## Clause 3 Conditions

3.1 Outpatient expenses are eligible for reimbursement only where the consultation, test or prescription is for a condition covered under the base Health Gold Policy Wording. Consultations for conditions permanently excluded under Clause 5.1 of the Policy Wording, or for conditions subject to an applicable specific exclusion, are not reimbursable.

3.2 Waiting periods applicable under the base Health Gold Policy Wording apply equally to outpatient claims under this Rider. A consultation or test for a condition that is within a waiting period under the base policy is not payable under this Rider.

3.3 Outpatient expenses must be supported by: original consultation receipt or invoice; original pharmacy bills where medicines are claimed; original test reports where investigations are claimed; and the treating physician's prescription linking the tests and medicines to the consultation.

3.4 This Rider is subject to an annual deductible or co-payment if stated in the Policy Schedule. Where a deductible or co-payment applies, it is applied per Policy Year before reimbursement commences.

## Clause 4 Exclusions

4.1 Outpatient consultations, investigations and medicines for conditions covered under in-patient Hospitalisation or Day Care Procedures in the same Policy Year are payable under the pre and post-hospitalisation provisions of the base policy (Clause 2.2) and not under this Rider, where they fall within the applicable pre and post-hospitalisation windows.

4.2 Vitamins, tonics, nutritional supplements and over-the-counter products not prescribed for the treatment of a specific diagnosed condition are excluded.

4.3 Consultations or procedures of a cosmetic or aesthetic nature, and dental consultations that do not arise from an accidental injury, are excluded under this Rider in the same manner as under the base Policy Wording.

4.4 Alternative medicine consultations and treatments (such as homeopathy, ayurveda, unani, siddha and naturopathy) are covered only where the base Health Gold Policy Wording includes cover for such treatments; this Rider does not expand the scope of cover for alternative medicine beyond the base policy.
