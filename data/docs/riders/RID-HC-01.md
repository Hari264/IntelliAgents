## Clause 1 Rider Scope

1.1 This Rider, when endorsed on the Policy Schedule, provides a fixed daily cash benefit to the Insured Person for each qualifying day of in-patient hospitalisation. The Hospital Cash benefit is a cash payment made directly to the Insured Person and is independent of the actual medical expenses incurred or reimbursed under the base Health Gold or Health Silver policy.

1.2 The Hospital Cash benefit is paid in addition to any amount settled under the base policy for the same hospitalisation event. It does not reduce the base policy's sum insured and does not affect the assessment of the in-patient hospitalisation claim.

1.3 Defined terms used in this Rider have the same meanings as in the applicable Policy Wording unless otherwise stated. In particular, the terms Hospital, In-patient Hospitalisation, Medically Necessary Treatment and Day Care Procedure have the meanings given in Clauses 1.1 and 1.2 of the applicable Policy Wording.

1.4 All terms and conditions of the applicable Policy Wording continue to apply except as modified by this Rider.

## Clause 2 Benefit

2.1 Where this Rider is shown in the Policy Schedule, the Company will pay a daily cash amount to the Insured Person for each calendar day of qualifying in-patient hospitalisation. The daily cash amount and the maximum number of qualifying days per Policy Year are as specified in the Policy Schedule.

2.2 The daily cash amount is payable from the first qualifying day of admission and continues for each subsequent calendar day of the same hospitalisation event, up to the maximum number of days specified in the Policy Schedule.

2.3 For hospitalisation events that span a partial day at admission and a partial day at discharge, each partial day is counted as one full qualifying day for the purpose of calculating the number of days payable.

2.4 The Hospital Cash benefit is paid by electronic fund transfer to the Policyholder's registered bank account after the hospitalisation claim documents are submitted and the period of in-patient stay is confirmed.

## Clause 3 Conditions

3.1 The Hospital Cash benefit is payable only where the Insured Person's admission qualifies as In-patient Hospitalisation under the applicable Policy Wording — that is, the Insured Person is admitted as a registered in-patient for a minimum continuous period of 24 hours for Medically Necessary Treatment.

3.2 The underlying hospitalisation must arise from a condition that is covered under the applicable base Policy Wording, subject to all applicable waiting periods and exclusions. If the base policy claim is not payable for a reason other than the daily cash limit, the Hospital Cash benefit is also not payable.

3.3 The same hospitalisation event cannot be claimed more than once under this Rider. Where a Policyholder is transferred from one Hospital to another during a single continuous episode of treatment, the entire episode is treated as one hospitalisation event for the purpose of this Rider.

3.4 The Hospital Cash benefit is subject to the same notification requirements as the base policy claim, as stated in Clause 6.1 of the applicable Policy Wording.

## Clause 4 Exclusions

4.1 The Hospital Cash benefit is not payable for Day Care Procedures completed in less than 24 hours, even if the procedure is listed on the Company's Day Care Procedure list and the base policy claim is payable. The 24-hour in-patient minimum is a condition of the Hospital Cash benefit.

4.2 The Hospital Cash benefit is not payable for Domiciliary Hospitalisation, as defined in Clause 2.4 of the applicable Policy Wording. The benefit applies only to formal in-patient admissions at a registered Hospital.

4.3 The Hospital Cash benefit is not payable for any period of hospitalisation that falls within an applicable waiting period under the base policy, or for any condition that is permanently excluded under Clause 5.1 of the applicable Policy Wording.

4.4 The Hospital Cash benefit is not payable for hospitalisations arising from intentional self-inflicted injury, substance abuse, or any of the specific exclusions in Clause 5.2 of the applicable Policy Wording.
