## Clause 1 Rider Scope

1.1 This Rider, when endorsed on the Policy Schedule, extends cover under the base Health Gold or Health Silver policy to include in-patient treatment at a registered AYUSH treatment facility. AYUSH refers to the systems of Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy as recognised under applicable Indian law and the Ministry of AYUSH.

1.2 Without this Rider, treatment at AYUSH facilities does not form part of the base policy cover. This Rider provides supplementary cover for qualifying in-patient AYUSH treatment only. All other benefits and terms of the base Policy Wording continue to apply.

1.3 Defined terms used in this Rider — including Insured Person, Policy Schedule, Medically Necessary Treatment and Policyholder — have the same meanings as in the applicable base Policy Wording.

1.4 Treatment under this Rider must be for a condition for which in-patient treatment at an AYUSH facility is clinically indicated. Treatment that is primarily for wellness, relaxation or general health maintenance is not covered under this Rider.

## Clause 2 Benefit

2.1 Where this Rider is shown in the Policy Schedule, the Company will reimburse expenses incurred by the Insured Person for in-patient treatment at a registered AYUSH facility, subject to the annual benefit limit stated in the Policy Schedule.

2.2 Covered expenses under this Rider include: (a) room charges at the AYUSH facility for the period of in-patient admission; (b) charges for the AYUSH treatment procedure or programme prescribed by the treating AYUSH practitioner; (c) medicines and therapeutic preparations prescribed by the treating AYUSH practitioner during the admission; and (d) diagnostic investigations ordered by the treating practitioner as part of the management of the condition.

2.3 The annual benefit limit stated in the Policy Schedule is the maximum amount payable under this Rider in a Policy Year. This limit is separate from and does not reduce the base policy's sum insured.

2.4 This Rider reimburses expenses at actuals up to the annual limit specified in the Policy Schedule. It does not operate as a cashless facility; the Insured Person pays the AYUSH facility directly and submits a reimbursement claim.

## Clause 3 Conditions

3.1 To qualify for reimbursement under this Rider, the following conditions must be met: (a) the treatment must be delivered at a registered AYUSH hospital or treatment facility that holds appropriate recognition from the central or relevant state government authority for providing in-patient treatment under the applicable AYUSH system; (b) the treating practitioner must be duly registered under the applicable law governing the relevant AYUSH system; and (c) the Insured Person must be formally admitted as an in-patient for a continuous period as specified in the product guidelines.

3.2 The treating AYUSH practitioner must provide a written treatment plan confirming the condition being treated, the AYUSH system and treatment modality applied, and the duration of in-patient stay.

3.3 The same notification requirements as those in Clause 6.1 of the applicable base Policy Wording apply to claims under this Rider.

3.4 This Rider is subject to any applicable waiting period under the base Policy Wording for the condition being treated, unless the waiting period has been completed or portability credit has been applied.

## Clause 4 Exclusions

4.1 Treatment that is primarily for general wellness, relaxation, preventive health programmes or personal enrichment — including yoga classes, lifestyle programmes and similar activities — is excluded from cover under this Rider, even if delivered at a registered AYUSH facility.

4.2 Outpatient AYUSH consultations, home visits by AYUSH practitioners and consultations conducted without in-person attendance at a registered facility are not covered under this Rider.

4.3 Treatment for conditions that are permanently excluded under Clause 5.1 of the applicable base Policy Wording, or that fall under any other specific exclusion in the base Policy Wording, is excluded from this Rider on the same basis.

4.4 Any AYUSH treatment modality not recognised by the central or relevant state government authority under the applicable AYUSH framework, or treatment at a facility without appropriate government recognition, is excluded.
