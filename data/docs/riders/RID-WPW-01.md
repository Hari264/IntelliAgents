## Clause 1 Rider Scope

1.1 This Rider, when endorsed on the Policy Schedule of a Health Gold policy, modifies the waiting period applicable to joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty) as described in Clause 2 of this Rider.

1.2 This Rider is issued as an endorsement to the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 (HG-WORD-2025) only. It has no application to any other policy wording or product.

1.3 The definitions in Clause 1.1 and Clause 1.2 of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 apply to this Rider unless the context requires otherwise. In particular, the term Arthroplasty in this Rider has the same meaning as in Clause 1.2.2 of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025.

1.4 All terms and conditions of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 continue to apply except as expressly modified by this Rider.

## Clause 2 Benefit

2.1 Where this Rider is shown in the Policy Schedule, the waiting period for joint replacement surgery under Clause 4.2.1 of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 is reduced to 0 months.

2.2 The effect of Clause 2.1 is that an Insured Person who is covered under this Rider may make a claim for joint replacement surgery under Clause 4.2 of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 from the first day of the Policy Period, without needing to satisfy any waiting period for that procedure under Clause 4.2.1.

2.3 This waiver applies to the waiting period for joint replacement surgery only. All other specific waiting periods under Clause 4.2 of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 — including those for cataract surgery (Clause 4.2.2), hernia surgery (Clause 4.2.3) and bariatric surgery (Clause 4.2.4) — continue to apply in full and are not affected by this Rider.

## Clause 3 Conditions

3.1 This Rider is available only at the inception of a new Health Gold policy issued under the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025. It may not be added at renewal to an existing policy, and it may not be added mid-term.

3.2 This Rider is available only for Insured Persons who are aged 45 years or below on the date of policy inception. The age condition is assessed separately for each Insured Person named in the Policy Schedule; the Rider applies only to those Insured Persons who satisfy the age criterion.

3.3 An additional premium is payable for this Rider. The applicable premium is as stated in the Policy Schedule.

3.4 This Rider does not affect the initial waiting period under Clause 4.1 of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025. The 1-month initial waiting period continues to apply to all illnesses, including joint replacement surgery claims, except for accidental injuries as provided in Clause 4.1.2 of the Policy Wording.

3.5 This Rider does not affect the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period under Clause 4.3 of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025. If the joint condition for which replacement surgery is required constitutes a Pre-Existing Disease, the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period applies regardless of this Rider.

3.6 All other terms, conditions, sub-limits and exclusions of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 continue to apply, including the joint replacement sub-limit under Clause 3.2.2 and the room rent provisions under Clauses 8.1 and 8.2.

## Clause 4 Exclusions

4.1 This Rider does not waive the waiting period for any condition other than joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty) as defined in Clause 1.2.2 of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025.

4.2 This Rider does not apply to Insured Persons aged above 45 years on the date of policy inception. Joint replacement surgery claims by such Insured Persons are subject to the waiting period under Clause 4.2.1 of the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 in the normal course.

4.3 The waiver of the joint replacement waiting period under this Rider does not override the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period in Clause 4.3. An Insured Person whose joint condition is a Pre-Existing Disease cannot use this Rider to bypass the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period.

4.4 If this Rider is not shown in the Policy Schedule, the standard waiting period under Clause 4.2.1 of the applicable Health Gold Policy Wording applies.
