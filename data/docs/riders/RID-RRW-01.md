## Clause 1 Rider Scope

1.1 This Rider, when endorsed on the Policy Schedule, modifies the room rent provisions of the base Health Gold policy by removing the room rent limit under Clause 8.1 and the proportionate deduction under Clause 8.2 for the Insured Person(s) named in the Policy Schedule as covered under this Rider.

1.2 Without this Rider, the base Health Gold Policy Wording applies a per-day room rent limit to standard in-patient room charges (Clause 8.1). Where the room category chosen exceeds this limit, a proportionate deduction is applied to associated medical expenses (Clause 8.2). This Rider removes both of those provisions for covered Insured Persons.

1.3 Defined terms in this Rider have the same meanings as in the applicable Health Gold Policy Wording unless otherwise stated.

1.4 This Rider does not increase the sum insured. All claims under the base policy continue to be subject to the sum insured, sub-limits (other than the room rent limit) and exclusions of the applicable Policy Wording.

## Clause 2 Benefit

2.1 Where this Rider is shown in the Policy Schedule, the room rent limit under Clause 8.1 of the applicable Health Gold Policy Wording does not apply to the Insured Person's hospitalisation. The Insured Person may occupy any room category at the treating Hospital and the room charges will be assessed at the actual rate charged, without reference to the per-day limit that would otherwise apply under Clause 8.1.

2.2 Where this Rider is shown in the Policy Schedule, the proportionate deduction under Clause 8.2 of the applicable Health Gold Policy Wording does not apply to the Insured Person's hospitalisation. Associated medical expenses — including surgeon and anaesthetist fees, consultant charges, nursing charges, operation theatre charges and diagnostic charges — are payable at actuals and are not reduced on account of the room category chosen.

2.3 ICU and ICCU charges continue to be covered at actuals under Clause 8.1.2 of the applicable Policy Wording, whether or not this Rider is in force. This Rider does not change the treatment of ICU charges, which are already covered at actuals under the base policy.

2.4 The benefit under this Rider applies per hospitalisation event. There is no annual limit on the number of hospitalisations to which the waiver applies, subject to the overall sum insured.

## Clause 3 Conditions

3.1 This Rider applies only to the Insured Persons listed in the Policy Schedule as covered under it. Where a family floater policy includes multiple Insured Persons and this Rider is endorsed for only some of them, the room rent waiver applies only to those specific Insured Persons. For Insured Persons not listed under this Rider, the base Policy Wording's room rent limit and proportionate deduction apply.

3.2 An additional premium is payable for this Rider. The applicable premium is as stated in the Policy Schedule.

3.3 This Rider does not override sub-limits set out in Clause 3.2 of the applicable Policy Wording. The joint replacement sub-limit, cataract sub-limit and other procedure-specific sub-limits continue to apply.

3.4 All other terms, conditions and exclusions of the base Health Gold Policy Wording continue in force. Room rent charges are payable at actuals under this Rider, but the overall sum insured limits remain unchanged.

## Clause 4 Exclusions

4.1 This Rider does not apply to Insured Persons not listed in the Policy Schedule as covered under this Rider. The standard room rent limit and proportionate deduction of the base Policy Wording apply to all uncovered Insured Persons.

4.2 This Rider does not affect any waiting period, sub-limit or exclusion of the base Health Gold Policy Wording other than the room rent limit (Clause 8.1) and the proportionate deduction (Clause 8.2).

4.3 Charges that are excluded under the base Policy Wording — including but not limited to personal convenience items, telephone charges, visitor meals and non-medical charges — remain excluded regardless of this Rider.

4.4 Where a claim is declined under the base Policy Wording for a reason unrelated to room rent — for example, on grounds of an applicable exclusion or a waiting period — this Rider does not reinstate the claim or modify the basis for decline.
