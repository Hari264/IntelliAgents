## Clause 1 Rider Scope

1.1 This Rider, when endorsed on the Policy Schedule, provides a lump sum benefit to the Insured Person upon first diagnosis of one of the covered critical illnesses listed in the Policy Schedule, subject to the terms and conditions set out in this Rider.

1.2 This Rider is a benefit rider and operates independently of the in-patient hospitalisation cover under the base Health Gold Policy Wording. The lump sum benefit payable under this Rider is in addition to any in-patient claim settled under the base policy for the same event and does not reduce the base policy's sum insured.

1.3 The lump sum benefit amount is as stated in the Policy Schedule for the applicable Insured Person. The Policy Schedule may set different benefit amounts for different covered critical illnesses.

1.4 Defined terms used in this Rider have the same meanings as in the applicable Health Gold Policy Wording unless otherwise stated in this Rider.

## Clause 2 Benefit

2.1 Where this Rider is shown in the Policy Schedule, the Company will pay a lump sum benefit to the Insured Person upon confirmed first diagnosis of a covered critical illness, provided the diagnosis is made in India by a specialist registered medical practitioner with the relevant qualification, and the survival condition in Clause 3.3 is satisfied.

2.2 The covered critical illnesses are those listed in the Policy Schedule for the Insured Person's cover. The list typically includes major conditions such as specified forms of cancer, heart attack meeting defined clinical criteria, stroke with permanent neurological deficit, coronary artery bypass surgery, kidney failure requiring regular dialysis, major major surgical intervention, and specified other conditions. The precise list and the clinical definitions for each condition are as set out in the Policy Schedule endorsement.

2.3 The lump sum benefit is payable only once per Insured Person per Policy under this Rider, regardless of the number of covered critical illnesses diagnosed or the number of Policy Years in force.

2.4 Payment of the lump sum benefit does not terminate the base Health Gold policy. The Insured Person's in-patient cover under the base policy continues in force for the remainder of the Policy Year, subject to all applicable terms and limits.

## Clause 3 Conditions

3.1 This Rider is subject to an initial waiting period, as specified in the Policy Schedule, from the date of inception of the Rider (or from the date the Rider is added to an existing policy). No lump sum benefit is payable where the covered critical illness is first diagnosed within this initial waiting period, except where the critical illness results directly from an accidental bodily injury.

3.2 The diagnosis must be confirmed by a specialist registered medical practitioner in the relevant field and supported by appropriate clinical evidence including, where applicable, histopathology reports, imaging, ECG findings, or other diagnostic evidence appropriate to the specific condition.

3.3 Survival condition: the Insured Person must survive for a period of 30 days from the date of first diagnosis of the covered critical illness before the lump sum benefit becomes payable. If the Insured Person does not survive this period, no benefit is payable under this Rider.

3.4 Where the Insured Person has a prior diagnosis of a covered critical illness that predates the Policy or the addition of this Rider, that condition is excluded from cover under this Rider as a Pre-Existing Disease. Any condition whose initial diagnosis is confirmed before the policy or Rider inception is not covered regardless of whether it was disclosed.

## Clause 4 Exclusions

4.1 Any critical illness first diagnosed before the inception of this Rider, or within the initial waiting period specified in Clause 3.1, is excluded unless caused directly by an accidental bodily injury.

4.2 Any condition arising from intentional self-inflicted injury, substance abuse, or the permanent exclusions in Clause 5.1 of the base Health Gold Policy Wording is not covered under this Rider.

4.3 Any condition that does not meet the specific clinical definition for the relevant covered critical illness as set out in the Policy Schedule endorsement is not covered, even if the Insured Person receives a diagnosis that uses the same or similar terminology.

4.4 A covered critical illness that has been diagnosed and claimed under a prior policy or under this Rider on a previous occasion is excluded. This Rider pays once per Insured Person per life of the policy, as stated in Clause 2.3.
