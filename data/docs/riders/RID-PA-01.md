## Clause 1 Rider Scope

1.1 This Rider, when endorsed on the Policy Schedule, provides defined benefits to the Insured Person or their nominee on the occurrence of an accidental bodily injury during the Policy Period. The Personal Accident Rider operates independently of the in-patient hospitalisation benefits in the base Health Gold or Health Silver policy. Benefits under this Rider are paid in addition to any amount settled under the base policy for the same event.

1.2 An accidental bodily injury for the purposes of this Rider means a sudden, unforeseen and external event causing bodily harm to the Insured Person, which is not attributable to any illness, disease, deliberate act, or any excluded cause listed in Clause 4 of this Rider.

1.3 The Rider benefit is paid as a lump sum or defined proportion of the sum assured as specified in the Policy Schedule, depending on the outcome category of the injury.

1.4 Defined terms in this Rider have the same meanings as in the applicable base Policy Wording unless otherwise stated.

## Clause 2 Benefit

2.1 Where this Rider is shown in the Policy Schedule, the Company will pay the benefit amount applicable to the outcome category as specified in the Policy Schedule on confirmation that the outcome meets the criteria for that category.

2.2 The benefit categories payable under this Rider, and the benefit amounts or proportions applicable to each, are as specified in the Policy Schedule. Categories typically include: (a) accidental death; (b) permanent total disability — meaning total and irrecoverable loss of the ability to engage in any employment or occupation; (c) permanent partial disability — meaning irrecoverable loss of a specified part or function, as defined in the Policy Schedule; and (d) temporary total disability — meaning temporary inability to engage in any employment or occupation for a continuous period following the injury.

2.3 Only one benefit category is payable per accident event. Where the same injury gives rise to a temporary disability that subsequently results in permanent disability, the higher applicable benefit as stated in the Policy Schedule is payable.

2.4 Payment is made by electronic fund transfer to the Policyholder or, in the event of accidental death, to the nominee registered in the Policy Schedule.

## Clause 3 Conditions

3.1 To qualify for a benefit under this Rider, the injury must: (a) be caused by a sudden, unforeseen and external event; (b) have occurred during the Policy Period while this Rider was in force; and (c) result in one of the outcome categories specified in the Policy Schedule within the period stated in the Policy Schedule from the date of the accident.

3.2 The claim must be supported by: a copy of a First Information Report or other official record of the accident where applicable; medical records confirming the nature and extent of the injury; a certified physician's report assessing the degree of disability or confirming the cause of death; and any other documentation specified in the claims process or requested by the Company.

3.3 The accidental death benefit is payable to the nominee registered in the Policy Schedule. If no nominee has been registered, the benefit is payable to the legal estate of the Insured Person in accordance with applicable law.

3.4 Notification of a claim under this Rider must be made promptly. For accidental death, notification must be made within 30 days of the event. For disability claims, notification must be made as soon as practicable.

## Clause 4 Exclusions

4.1 Intentional self-inflicted injury, suicide or attempted suicide, whether or not the Insured Person is of sound mind at the time, is excluded from cover under this Rider.

4.2 Accidental injury arising directly or indirectly from the use or misuse of alcohol or controlled substances as defined under applicable Indian law is excluded.

4.3 Accidental injury arising from participation in hazardous or adventure sports or activities listed in the Company's hazardous sports exclusion schedule, unless the Insured Person has paid an additional premium to cover such activities and this is noted in the Policy Schedule.

4.4 Accidental injury arising from war, invasion, hostilities (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, riot, revolution or any act of a foreign enemy, as well as injury arising from nuclear, radiological or chemical contamination, is excluded from cover under this Rider.

4.5 Any disability or death attributable primarily to an illness, disease or medical condition — rather than to an external accidental event — is excluded, even if the illness or condition was aggravated by an external event.
