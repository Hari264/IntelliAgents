## Clause 1.1 General Definitions

In this Policy, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them below. Where any term is not defined in this Clause, the ordinary meaning applicable in the context of Indian health insurance shall apply.

1.1.1 Hospital means a registered establishment that satisfies all of the following: (a) it maintains at least ten in-patient beds; (b) it has at least one qualified medical practitioner registered with an applicable Medical Council on duty at all times; (c) it maintains qualified nursing staff under the supervision of a registered nurse on duty at all times; (d) it has a fully equipped operation theatre of its own; and (e) it maintains daily records of patients and makes those records accessible to the Company on request.

1.1.2 Insured Person means each individual named in the Policy Schedule who is covered under this Policy during the Policy Period.

1.1.3 Policy Schedule means the schedule issued by the Company to the Policyholder, forming part of this Policy, which sets out the Insured Person or Persons, the sum insured, the Policy Period, the premium paid, and any riders or endorsements attached to this Policy.

1.1.4 Network Hospital means a hospital that has a valid and subsisting cashless arrangement with the Company or its designated third-party administrator at the time of the Insured Person's admission.

1.1.5 Continuous Cover means uninterrupted health insurance cover, whether under this Policy or under a prior registered insurer's policy, including portability credit applied under Clause 9.2 of this Policy.

1.1.6 Day Care Procedure means a surgical procedure or treatment that, owing to advances in medical technology, is completed within less than 24 hours of hospitalisation and is listed in the Company's Day Care Procedure list applicable at the date of the procedure.

## Clause 1.2 Medical Definitions

The following medical terms have the meanings set out below for the purposes of this Policy.

1.2.1 Pre-Existing Disease means any condition, ailment, injury or disease that has been diagnosed, or for which the Insured Person received medical advice or treatment, at any time during the 48-month period immediately before the date on which this Policy first incepted for that Insured Person. Whether or not the Insured Person was subjectively aware of the condition at inception is not determinative.

1.2.2 Arthroplasty means a surgical procedure in which a diseased or damaged joint is replaced in whole or in part with a prosthetic implant. For the purposes of this Policy, the terms Arthroplasty and joint replacement surgery are equivalent and describe the same class of procedure regardless of the joint involved.

1.2.3 Medically Necessary Treatment means treatment, procedure or supply that is: (a) required for the diagnosis or direct treatment of a condition suffered by the Insured Person; (b) in accordance with generally accepted medical standards in India; (c) not primarily for the convenience of the Insured Person, treating physician or any other person; and (d) not of a nature where a less intensive or less costly service could be provided without materially affecting the outcome.

1.2.4 In-patient Hospitalisation means formal admission to a Hospital as a registered in-patient for a minimum continuous period of 24 hours for the purpose of receiving Medically Necessary Treatment for a covered condition.

## Clause 2.1 In-patient Hospitalisation

Under this Policy, the Company will indemnify the Insured Person for reasonable and customary expenses incurred for In-patient Hospitalisation, subject to all terms, conditions, sub-limits and exclusions of this Policy.

2.1.1 For a claim to qualify as In-patient Hospitalisation, the Insured Person must be formally admitted as a registered in-patient in a Hospital for a continuous period of at least 24 hours. Admissions lasting less than 24 hours do not qualify under this Clause unless the procedure is a Day Care Procedure listed under Clause 2.3.

2.1.2 The treatment must be Medically Necessary as defined in Clause 1.2.3. The Company reserves the right to seek an independent medical opinion to verify medical necessity, at its own expense.

2.1.3 Expenses covered under this Clause include room charges (subject to Clause 8.1), ICU charges, surgeon and anaesthetist fees, nursing charges, operation theatre charges, diagnostic tests ordered during the admission, medicines prescribed during the admission, and internal prosthetic devices required during surgery.

2.1.4 Claims arising directly from an accidental bodily injury are not subject to the initial waiting period under Clause 4.1 and are covered from the first day of the Policy Period, provided the injury was caused by an unforeseen, sudden and external event and is not attributable to any condition excluded under Clause 5 of this Policy.

## Clause 2.2 Pre and Post Hospitalisation

Under this Policy, the Company will reimburse medical expenses directly associated with a covered In-patient Hospitalisation event, incurred within the time periods specified in this Clause.

2.2.1 Pre-hospitalisation expenses are reimbursable for up to 30 days before the date of admission, provided the expenses are directly related to the illness or injury for which the Insured Person was subsequently admitted to hospital.

2.2.2 Post-hospitalisation expenses are reimbursable for up to 60 days from the date of discharge, provided the expenses are directly related to the condition for which the Insured Person was admitted.

2.2.3 Pre and post-hospitalisation expenses must be submitted as part of the main hospitalisation reimbursement claim in accordance with Clause 6.2. Separate standalone claims for pre or post-hospitalisation expenses, lodged independently of the main hospitalisation claim, will not be entertained.

2.2.4 Pre and post-hospitalisation reimbursement is subject to the overall sum insured and any applicable sub-limits. Where the sum insured has been exhausted by the main hospitalisation expenses, no further pre or post-hospitalisation amount will be payable.

2.2.5 Expenses within the pre or post-hospitalisation period that are not directly related to the covered hospitalisation event are not payable under this Clause. Routine medical check-ups, chronic disease management visits unrelated to the admitted condition and medication for unrelated conditions are excluded.

## Clause 2.3 Day Care Procedures

Under this Policy, the Company will indemnify expenses incurred by the Insured Person for procedures listed in the Company's Day Care Procedure list, even where the duration of hospitalisation is less than 24 hours.

2.3.1 To qualify under this Clause, the Day Care Procedure must be performed at a Hospital or at a day care centre equipped with an appropriate procedure room or operation theatre, under the supervision of a qualified registered medical practitioner.

2.3.2 The Company's Day Care Procedure list may be updated from time to time. The version of the list applicable at the date of the procedure shall govern eligibility. Policyholders may request the current list from the Company at any time.

2.3.3 A procedure does not qualify under this Clause merely because the Insured Person or treating physician chose to perform it on a day-care basis. The procedure must appear on the Company's Day Care list. If the procedure is not on the list and the admission lasts less than 24 hours, the claim is not payable under this Clause or under Clause 2.1.

2.3.4 All waiting periods, sub-limits and exclusions applicable to the underlying condition apply equally to Day Care claims. For example, cataract surgery is subject to the specific waiting period in Clause 4.2.2 and the sub-limit in Clause 3.2.1, whether performed as a Day Care Procedure or as a standard in-patient admission.

## Clause 2.4 Domiciliary Hospitalisation

Under this Policy, the Company will reimburse medical expenses incurred by the Insured Person for treatment received at the Insured Person's home where the conditions of this Clause are met.

2.4.1 For a claim to qualify as Domiciliary Hospitalisation, the period of continuous home-based treatment must exceed 3 consecutive days. Treatment that lasts 3 days or fewer does not qualify under this Clause.

2.4.2 The treating physician must certify in writing that: (a) the Insured Person's condition required treatment of a nature and intensity equivalent to in-patient hospitalisation; and (b) hospitalisation was not possible or was medically inadvisable owing to the Insured Person's condition.

2.4.3 Domiciliary Hospitalisation does not include outpatient consultations, routine nursing visits, chronic disease management visits or physiotherapy sessions that do not meet the criteria in this Clause.

2.4.4 Claims under this Clause must be supported by daily treatment records, prescriptions, nursing notes where applicable, and the written certification of the treating physician as described in Clause 2.4.2. All documents must be submitted within the time limit prescribed in Clause 6.2.

2.4.5 Domiciliary Hospitalisation is subject to all applicable sum insured limits, waiting periods and exclusions, including but not limited to those in Clauses 4 and 5 of this Policy.

## Clause 2.5 Road Ambulance

Under this Policy, the Company will reimburse charges incurred for the use of a road ambulance to transport the Insured Person to a Hospital in connection with a covered In-patient Hospitalisation event.

2.5.1 The reimbursement under this Clause is limited to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation event, regardless of the actual charge incurred or the number of ambulance journeys made in connection with that hospitalisation.

2.5.2 The ambulance must be a registered emergency medical services vehicle operating as a designated road ambulance. Charges for private cars, taxis, auto-rickshaws, app-based cab services or any other conveyance that is not a registered road ambulance are not reimbursable under this Clause.

2.5.3 The road ambulance reimbursement is payable in addition to the in-patient hospitalisation expenses and does not form part of any sub-limit set out in Clause 3.2. However, the road ambulance amount is included within and subject to the overall sum insured.

2.5.4 The Insured Person must submit an original receipt or invoice from the ambulance service provider, clearly stating the date of travel, the origin and destination addresses, and the amount charged. This document must be submitted together with the main hospitalisation claim documents as required by Clause 6.2.

## Clause 2.6 Maternity

2.6.1 Maternity benefits are not covered under this Policy. Expenses related to childbirth, delivery, Caesarean section, miscarriage, pre-natal care, post-natal care and newborn care are excluded from cover under this Policy.

2.6.2 Complications arising directly from pregnancy or childbirth are not covered to the extent they are causally connected to the maternity event. A condition that arises independently and is not caused by or directly related to pregnancy may be assessed for coverage under the applicable in-patient or other clauses of this Policy, subject to all other terms and conditions.

2.6.3 A newborn child is not automatically covered under this Policy. Coverage for a newborn child is available only upon the child being separately enrolled as an Insured Person under this Policy, subject to all underwriting requirements and applicable waiting periods.

2.6.4 Lawful medical termination of pregnancy where the procedure is Medically Necessary and results in formal in-patient admission for at least 24 hours may be assessed under Clause 2.1 on its merits. Such expenses are not payable as a maternity benefit; they are separately assessed under Clause 2.1, subject to all other terms of this Policy.

## Clause 3.1 Sum Insured

Under this Policy, the sum insured is the maximum aggregate amount the Company will pay in respect of all covered claims made by or on behalf of an Insured Person in a Policy Year, subject to the terms of this Clause.

3.1.1 The following sum insured options are available under this Policy: ₹2,00,000; ₹3,00,000; and ₹5,00,000. The sum insured applicable to each Insured Person is as stated in the Policy Schedule.

3.1.2 For a family floater Policy, the sum insured is shared collectively among all Insured Persons named in the Policy Schedule. Once the aggregate sum insured is exhausted by claims made by any combination of Insured Persons in a Policy Year, no further claims are payable in that year.

3.1.3 Where separate individual covers are in force for multiple Insured Persons, each person's sum insured is independent. Utilisation by one Insured Person does not reduce the sum insured available to another.

3.1.4 The sum insured resets to its full value at the start of each new Policy Year upon timely renewal of the Policy.

3.1.5 Sub-limits specified in Clause 3.2 operate within the sum insured. Payment under a sub-limit reduces the available balance of the sum insured by the amount paid.

## Clause 3.2 Sub-limits

Notwithstanding the available sum insured, the monetary sub-limits specified in this Clause apply to certain procedures and benefits. All sub-limits are the maximum amount payable per event or per applicable unit as specified.

3.2.1 Cataract surgery: the Company will pay a maximum of ₹25,000 per eye for all expenses related to cataract surgery in a Policy Year, including the cost of an intra-ocular lens implant. Where both eyes require cataract surgery in the same Policy Year, the sub-limit of ₹25,000 applies separately to each eye.

3.2.2 Joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty): the Company will pay a maximum of ₹1,50,000 per joint for all expenses related to joint replacement surgery. Where more than one joint is replaced during a single hospitalisation, the sub-limit applies separately to each joint replaced.

3.2.3 Road ambulance: the maximum payable per hospitalisation event is ₹2,000, as set out in Clause 2.5.1.

3.2.4 Sub-limits apply within the overall sum insured and are not additive to it. Where the actual claim for a sub-limited procedure exceeds the sub-limit, the balance above the sub-limit is not payable from any other benefit or sum insured available under this Policy.

3.2.5 Sub-limits are not altered by riders unless the rider endorsement on the Policy Schedule expressly provides for a change to a specific sub-limit stated in this Clause.

## Clause 4.1 Initial Waiting Period

Under this Policy, no benefit is payable for any illness or disease that first manifests or is first treated during the initial waiting period specified in this Clause, except as provided for accidental injuries.

4.1.1 The initial waiting period is 1 month commencing on the policy start date stated in the Policy Schedule. During this period, claims arising from any illness or disease — whether or not the Insured Person was previously aware of it — will not be entertained, except as provided in Clause 4.1.2.

4.1.2 Claims arising directly from an accidental bodily injury are not subject to the initial waiting period and are covered from the first day of the Policy Period. To qualify for this exception, the injury must have been caused by an unforeseen, sudden and external event and must not be attributable to any pre-existing condition, self-infliction or conduct excluded under Clause 5 of this Policy.

4.1.3 For the purposes of this Clause, the relevant date is the date on which the Insured Person first sought or received medical attention for the condition that led to the hospitalisation, not solely the date of admission. Where treating physician records indicate that the condition commenced during the initial waiting period, the claim will not be payable.

4.1.4 The initial waiting period applies from the date of commencement of this Policy as stated in the Policy Schedule.

## Clause 4.2 Specific Waiting Periods

Certain conditions and procedures are subject to the specific waiting periods set out in this Clause, commencing on the policy start date, regardless of when the Insured Person first became aware of the condition.

4.2.1 Joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty): no benefit is payable under this Policy for joint replacement surgery unless 36 months of Continuous Cover have elapsed from the policy start date. Where the date of loss falls before the expiry of 36 months from the policy start date, the claim is not payable under this Clause.

4.2.2 Cataract surgery: no benefit is payable for cataract surgery unless 24 months of Continuous Cover have elapsed from the policy start date.

4.2.3 Hernia surgery: no benefit is payable for the surgical correction of a hernia unless 24 months of Continuous Cover have elapsed from the policy start date.

4.2.4 Bariatric surgery: no benefit is payable for bariatric surgery, being surgical intervention for the treatment of clinically severe obesity, unless 36 months of Continuous Cover have elapsed from the policy start date.

4.2.5 Portability credit: where an Insured Person transfers to this Policy from a prior insurer with Continuous Cover, waiting periods already completed under the prior policy will be credited toward the specific waiting periods in this Clause, subject to the conditions and documentation requirements of Clause 9.2.

4.2.6 The specific waiting periods in this Clause apply in addition to, and not in substitution for, the initial waiting period in Clause 4.1 and the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period in Clause 4.3.

## Clause 4.3 Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period

Under this Policy, no benefit is payable for treatment of a Pre-Existing Disease, or for any condition arising directly from or as a complication of a Pre-Existing Disease, until the waiting period specified in this Clause has been completed.

4.3.1 The Pre-Existing Disease waiting period under this Policy is 48 months of Continuous Cover commencing from the policy start date. No claim relating to a Pre-Existing Disease, its complications, or any condition whose primary cause is a Pre-Existing Disease, shall be payable unless the Insured Person has maintained Continuous Cover for not less than 48 months.

4.3.2 A condition is treated as a Pre-Existing Disease for the purposes of this Clause if, as of the policy start date, the Insured Person had received any medical advice, diagnosis or treatment for that condition during the 48-month period immediately preceding the policy start date, as defined in Clause 1.2.1. The Insured Person's lack of awareness of the condition does not exclude its classification as a Pre-Existing Disease.

4.3.3 Where the treating physician determines that the primary cause of a claimed condition is a Pre-Existing Disease, the claim will be assessed under this Clause, even if the immediate precipitating event appears to be a new illness or complication.

4.3.4 Portability credit under Clause 9.2 applies to the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period. An Insured Person may claim credit for the period already served under a prior continuous health insurance policy, subject to the documentation requirements of Clause 9.2.

## Clause 5.1 Permanent Exclusions

The following are permanently excluded from cover under this Policy and shall not become payable regardless of the duration of cover or any waiting periods served.

5.1.1 Any condition or injury arising from or in connection with war, invasion, acts of foreign enemies, hostilities (whether war is declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection or military or usurped power.

5.1.2 Any condition arising from nuclear, radiological, biological or chemical contamination or attack.

5.1.3 Intentional self-inflicted injury, suicide or attempted suicide, whether the Insured Person is mentally sound or otherwise at the time.

5.1.4 Injuries sustained while participating in hazardous or adventure sports activities listed in the Company's hazardous sports exclusion schedule in force at the date of injury, unless the Insured Person has paid an additional premium to cover such activities and this is noted in the Policy Schedule.

5.1.5 Treatment for any condition arising from or directly related to the non-prescription use of narcotics or controlled substances as defined under applicable Indian law.

5.1.6 Any procedure performed for purposes other than the treatment of a diagnosed illness or injury, including prophylactic surgical procedures undertaken without a specific medical indication.

5.1.7 Expenses for treatment obtained outside India from a practitioner not registered with an applicable Medical Council or equivalent body.

## Clause 5.2 Specific Exclusions

In addition to the permanent exclusions in Clause 5.1, the following specific exclusions apply under this Policy.

5.2.1 Treatment for conditions arising directly from substance abuse, being the abuse of alcohol or drugs listed under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, or any amendment thereto. This includes hospitalisation for detoxification or rehabilitation in connection with substance abuse.

5.2.2 Non-surgical treatment for obesity, including supervised dietary programmes, appetite suppressants and weight-loss supplements, that does not constitute the bariatric surgery described in Clause 4.2.4.

5.2.3 Spectacles, contact lenses, hearing aids and similar assistive devices, except where required as an immediate and direct consequence of a covered surgical procedure.

5.2.4 Dental treatment that does not qualify for cover under Clause 5.4 of this Policy.

5.2.5 Congenital external diseases and defects, and developmental abnormalities present before the policy start date, except as specifically covered under an applicable rider noted in the Policy Schedule.

5.2.6 Rest cures, sanitarium stays, spa treatments or any residential stay whose primary purpose is not the active medical treatment of a diagnosed condition.

5.2.7 Vitamins, tonics, supplements and over-the-counter medications that are not part of a treatment plan for a specific diagnosed illness being treated during a covered hospitalisation.

## Clause 5.3 Experimental and Unproven Treatment

Under this Policy, the Company will not pay for any treatment, procedure, drug or device that is experimental or unproven as described in this Clause.

5.3.1 A treatment is experimental or unproven for the purposes of this Policy if it: (a) is not recognised as a standard treatment for the relevant condition by the Medical Council of India or an equivalent authoritative medical body; (b) has not received approval from the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation for the relevant indication; or (c) is available only through a clinical trial or research protocol.

5.3.2 This exclusion applies regardless of whether the treating physician recommends the treatment and regardless of the outcome for the Insured Person. The test is whether the treatment meets the criteria in Clause 5.3.1, not whether it produced a beneficial result.

5.3.3 Examples of treatment categories that may be excluded under this Clause include stem cell therapy for indications not approved by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation, gene therapy for non-approved indications, and off-label pharmaceutical prescriptions for conditions for which the drug has not received applicable regulatory approval.

5.3.4 Where there is a dispute as to whether a treatment is experimental, the Company may refer the matter to an independent medical panel. The determination of such a panel will be taken into account in the Company's claims assessment and, if it confirms the experimental nature of the treatment, the exclusion applies.

## Clause 5.4 Dental and Cosmetic Treatment

Under this Policy, dental and cosmetic treatment are excluded except as expressly described in this Clause.

5.4.1 Dental treatment is covered under this Policy only where it is required as a direct and immediate consequence of an accidental bodily injury that itself necessitates in-patient hospitalisation under Clause 2.1. Only the dental treatment directly caused by and forming part of that same hospitalisation event is eligible. Routine dental care, preventive dentistry, orthodontic treatment, dental prosthetics and any elective dental procedure are excluded from cover.

5.4.2 Cosmetic and aesthetic procedures are excluded from cover under this Policy. A cosmetic or aesthetic procedure is one whose primary purpose is to alter or improve the appearance of the Insured Person rather than to diagnose or treat a diagnosed disease or injury. This includes, but is not limited to, skin resurfacing procedures, augmentation procedures, hair restoration treatments and any surgical or non-surgical intervention performed for aesthetic rather than therapeutic purposes.

5.4.3 Reconstructive surgery required as a direct consequence of an accidental injury covered under Clause 2.1, or as a necessary consequence of the treatment of a malignant condition, is not considered cosmetic for the purposes of this Clause and may be covered as In-patient Hospitalisation under Clause 2.1, subject to all other terms and conditions of this Policy.

## Clause 6.1 Claim Notification

The Insured Person, or if the Insured Person is unable to do so, the Policyholder or their authorised representative, must notify the Company of any hospitalisation within the time limits specified in this Clause. Failure to notify within the required time may result in the claim being declined or reduced, unless the Company is satisfied that sufficient cause exists for the delay.

6.1.1 For planned hospitalisation, the Company or its designated third-party administrator must be notified at least 48 hours before the date of admission. This advance notification is required to allow the Company to process a pre-authorisation request for the cashless facility where applicable.

6.1.2 For emergency hospitalisation, the Company or its designated third-party administrator must be notified within 24 hours of admission. Where the severity of the Insured Person's condition prevents timely notification, the treating hospital or a family member of the Insured Person may notify on their behalf.

6.1.3 Notification must be made through the helpline number, email address, online portal or other channel specified in the Policy Schedule or in communications from the Company. A claim reference number will be issued upon receipt of the notification.

6.1.4 The claim reference number issued at the time of notification must be retained and quoted in all subsequent correspondence and documents relating to that claim.

## Clause 6.2 Claim Documents

All documents required to support a claim must be submitted to the Company within the time limits and in the manner specified in this Clause.

6.2.1 All reimbursement claim documents must be submitted within 30 days of the date of discharge from the Hospital. Where the Insured Person is physically unable to submit within this period owing to the nature of the illness or injury, the Company may condone a reasonable delay upon receipt of a satisfactory written explanation.

6.2.2 The following documents are mandatory for all reimbursement claims: (a) completed and signed claim form as issued by the Company; (b) original discharge summary or discharge certificate from the treating Hospital; (c) original itemised final bill from the Hospital; (d) all original bills, receipts and payment proofs for all expenses claimed; (e) all original diagnostic test reports and prescriptions relating to the hospitalisation; (f) a copy of a valid photo identity document of the Insured Person; and (g) a cancelled cheque or bank account details of the Policyholder for electronic fund transfer.

6.2.3 For surgical claims, the post-operative notes and, where applicable, the implant invoice with the implant sticker must also be submitted.

6.2.4 The Company may, in its reasonable discretion, request additional documents as required to assess a specific claim. The Insured Person must submit any additionally requested documents within 15 days of the Company's request.

## Clause 7.1 Cashless Facility

The Company offers a cashless facility that allows the Insured Person to receive covered treatment at a Network Hospital without making an upfront payment for eligible expenses, subject to pre-authorisation and the conditions in this Clause.

7.1.1 The cashless facility is available only at hospitals listed on the Company's Network Hospital list at the time of the Insured Person's admission. The Insured Person is responsible for confirming network status before or at the time of admission. The current network list may be checked through the Company's helpline or website.

7.1.2 For planned admissions, the pre-authorisation request must be submitted to the Company or its third-party administrator at least 48 hours before the intended date of admission, in accordance with Clause 6.1.1.

7.1.3 For emergency admissions, the pre-authorisation request must be submitted within 24 hours of admission, in accordance with Clause 6.1.2.

7.1.4 Pre-authorisation approval is not a guarantee of final claim settlement. Final settlement remains subject to submission of complete supporting documents, verification of the terms of this Policy, and any applicable sub-limits or exclusions.

7.1.5 Any expenses not approved under the cashless facility may be lodged as a reimbursement claim under Clause 7.2. The cashless facility is not available at non-network hospitals; all expenses incurred at a non-network hospital must be claimed as reimbursement under Clause 7.2.

## Clause 7.2 Reimbursement Claims

Where the Insured Person has incurred expenses at a non-network hospital, or where the cashless facility was not availed for any reason, the Insured Person may submit a reimbursement claim under this Clause.

7.2.1 To make a reimbursement claim, the Insured Person must submit all documents listed in Clause 6.2 within 30 days of the date of discharge from the Hospital.

7.2.2 Upon receipt of complete documents, the Company will assess the claim and pay the eligible amount directly to the Policyholder's bank account by electronic fund transfer. Payment by cheque may be arranged upon written request.

7.2.3 The amount reimbursed will be the lesser of: (a) the actual expenses incurred as evidenced by original bills and receipts; or (b) the amount that would have been payable had the treatment been received at a Network Hospital under the cashless facility, subject to all applicable limits, sub-limits and deductions under this Policy.

7.2.4 Original bills and receipts are required for all reimbursement claims. Where originals have been lost, the Insured Person must submit a written explanation together with a self-declaration and, where available, certified duplicate bills from the treating facility.

7.2.5 All reimbursement claims are subject to the sum insured, sub-limits, exclusions and other terms applicable to the underlying condition as set out in this Policy.

## Clause 8.1 Room Rent Limit

The Company will pay room charges incurred during the Insured Person's hospitalisation subject to the eligibility limits specified in this Clause.

8.1.1 For a standard in-patient room — whether a single private room, semi-private room or general ward — the eligible room rent is limited to 1% of the sum insured per day. The sum insured applicable for this purpose is the sum insured stated in the Policy Schedule for the Insured Person.

8.1.2 ICU and ICCU charges are payable at actuals with no monetary cap per day, provided the ICU or ICCU admission is Medically Necessary and confirmed as such by the treating physician.

8.1.3 Where the Insured Person occupies a room at a category whose cost exceeds the eligible room rent limit, a proportionate deduction as described in Clause 8.2 will be applied to the associated medical expenses of that hospitalisation.

8.1.4 The room rent limit is applied on a per-day basis for each day of hospitalisation. A partial day of admission and a partial day of discharge are each treated as one full day for the purpose of computing eligible room charges.

8.1.5 The room rent limit applies only to room accommodation charges. Charges for medical treatment, surgery, consultations, diagnostic services and other clinical services are governed by the overall sum insured and any applicable sub-limits, and are not restricted by this Clause.

## Clause 8.2 Proportionate Deduction

Where the Insured Person occupies a room at a category that exceeds the eligible room rent limit under Clause 8.1.1, a proportionate deduction will be applied to the associated medical expenses of that hospitalisation, as described in this Clause.

8.2.1 The proportion of deduction equals the ratio of the eligible room rent under Clause 8.1.1 to the actual room rent charged for the period of hospitalisation. All associated medical expenses for that hospitalisation (as defined in Clause 8.2.2) are reduced by this same proportion. For example, for an Insured Person with a sum insured of ₹5,00,000, the eligible room rent is ₹5,000 per day (being 1% of ₹5,00,000). If the actual room rent charged is ₹10,000 per day, the proportion of deduction is 50% and all associated medical expenses are reduced accordingly.

8.2.2 Associated medical expenses subject to proportionate deduction are all medical expenses incurred during the hospitalisation other than pharmacy charges, implant costs and consumables. Specifically, the following are subject to deduction: surgeon and anaesthetist fees, consultant charges, nursing charges, operation theatre charges and diagnostic charges.

8.2.3 Pharmacy charges, implant costs and consumables are not subject to the proportionate deduction and are payable at actuals, subject to medical necessity and the overall sum insured.

8.2.4 The proportionate deduction may be waived only where the Insured Person holds the Room Rent Waiver Rider endorsed on the Policy Schedule, as described in that rider.

## Clause 9.1 Renewal

This Policy is renewable annually. The Insured Person's right to renewal is subject to the conditions of this Clause and the Company's underwriting guidelines in force at the time of renewal.

9.1.1 The Company will issue a renewal notice to the Policyholder at least 30 days before the expiry of the Policy Period. The Policyholder bears primary responsibility for timely renewal and may not rely solely on receipt of the Company's renewal notice.

9.1.2 A grace period of 30 days is available after the Policy expiry date for payment of the renewal premium. The Policy continues in force during the grace period for administrative continuity purposes, but no claim arising from a loss that occurs during the grace period will be payable by the Company.

9.1.3 If the renewal premium is not received by the Company within the 30-day grace period, the Policy lapses. A lapsed Policy may be reinstated at the Company's discretion upon payment of arrears and a satisfactory declaration of continued good health. Waiting periods will recommence from the date of reinstatement.

9.1.4 At renewal, the Policyholder may change the sum insured or add or remove rider benefits, subject to the Company's underwriting requirements at the time of renewal. Changes to sum insured may be subject to fresh underwriting review.

## Clause 9.2 Portability and Continuity

The Insured Person has the right to transfer this Policy to another registered health insurer while retaining credit for waiting periods already served, in accordance with the National Insurance Regulator's portability guidelines.

9.2.1 An Insured Person who has maintained Continuous Cover under a prior registered health insurance policy may apply for portability credit at the time of transferring to this Policy. The applicable waiting periods under Clauses 4.2 and 4.3 will be reduced by the number of months of Continuous Cover already completed under the prior policy, to the extent evidenced by documentary proof.

9.2.2 To avail of portability credit, the Policyholder must submit a portability application to the Company at least 45 days before the renewal date of the prior policy. Applications submitted less than 45 days before the prior policy's renewal date may be declined at the Company's discretion.

9.2.3 Portability credit will be calculated based on the prior insurer's renewal certificate, policy documents and any records available through the Insurance Information Bureau. The Company may verify information directly with the prior insurer.

9.2.4 Portability applies to waiting period credits only. It does not transfer or enhance the sum insured, premiums, riders or any other benefit from the prior insurer. All terms, conditions and benefits under this Policy are governed solely by this Policy document.

9.2.5 Where an Insured Person's prior cover lapses before transfer to this Policy, Continuous Cover is broken and the full waiting periods under Clauses 4.2 and 4.3 apply afresh from the commencement of this Policy.

## Clause 10.1 Grievance Redressal

The Company is committed to resolving grievances of Insured Persons and Policyholders in a fair, transparent and timely manner, consistent with the National Insurance Regulator's grievance redressal guidelines.

10.1.1 Any Insured Person or Policyholder who is dissatisfied with a claim decision, service interaction or any other matter relating to this Policy may submit a written grievance to the Company's Grievance Redressal Officer at the address or email specified in the Policy Schedule or in communications from the Company.

10.1.2 The Company will acknowledge receipt of the grievance within 3 working days of receipt. The Company will endeavour to resolve the grievance within 14 days of receipt. Where the grievance requires extended investigation, the Company will inform the Policyholder in writing of the expected revised timeline and the reasons for the extension.

10.1.3 If the Insured Person or Policyholder is not satisfied with the resolution provided, they may escalate to the Insurance Ombudsman having jurisdiction over the Policyholder's place of residence, in accordance with applicable Consumer Protection rules.

10.1.4 The Company will not charge any fee for processing a grievance. All grievance submissions must be made in writing — by letter, email or through the Company's online portal — to maintain a clear and auditable record. The Company will maintain a register of all grievances received and their resolution status as required by the National Insurance Regulator.
