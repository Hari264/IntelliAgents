## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP governs the quality audit programme for health insurance claim files processed by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. The programme is designed to ensure that claims are assessed consistently, in accordance with the applicable Policy Wording, the Company's SOPs and the NIR's requirements. Regular audits identify systemic errors, training needs and opportunities for process improvement before they affect a large number of claims. This SOP applies to all claims adjusters and their supervisors, and is administered by the Quality Assurance function in coordination with the Head of Claims.

## Section 2 Sampling and Scope

2.1 The Quality Assurance function conducts periodic audits of both closed and open claim files. Files are selected through a combination of random sampling and targeted selection.

2.2 Targeted selection may include: (a) all files closed within a specified period that were the subject of a Policyholder grievance or complaint; (b) files from adjusters who have recently joined the team or who are in a structured performance improvement programme; (c) files for specific claim types or document types that have shown elevated error rates in prior audit cycles; and (d) files associated with specific hospitals where billing concerns or repeated deficiency notices have been noted.

2.3 The scope of each audit cycle and the number of files to be reviewed are determined by the Head of Quality Assurance in consultation with the Head of Claims. Audit cycles may be monthly, quarterly or triggered by a specific event.

## Section 3 Audit Checklist

3.1 For each file selected, the auditor must verify: (a) that the correct Policy Wording version was identified and applied, based on the date of loss; (b) that all mandatory documents required by SOP-DOCS-01 were received before the financial assessment was finalised; (c) that any applicable waiting period was correctly identified and calculated using the policy start date and any portability credit; (d) that sub-limits were applied in accordance with the applicable Policy Wording clause; (e) that any rejection or partial disallowance was communicated to the Policyholder in writing within the required timeframe, as defined in CIR-02; (f) that all customer communications were handled using the applicable customer script from the SCR series; and (g) that all milestones, documents and decisions were recorded in the claims management system in a timely manner.

3.2 The auditor must also verify that no superseded rule was applied to the claim. For example, where a date-of-loss falls after a product change took effect, the new product terms must have been used rather than a prior version.

## Section 4 Feedback to Teams

4.1 On completion of each audit cycle, the Quality Assurance function must prepare a written summary report for the Head of Claims. The report must list each file audited, the specific findings for each file, whether each finding was a critical error or an administrative shortcoming, and whether any systemic trend was identified across multiple files.

4.2 Individual adjuster findings are shared with the adjuster's direct supervisor promptly after the audit is completed. The supervisor must discuss the findings with the adjuster and agree on a documented corrective action plan where required.

4.3 Systemic issues identified in the audit report must be escalated to the owner of the relevant SOP for review and, where necessary, amendment. The SOP owner must respond to the Quality Assurance function within a reasonable period with the proposed corrective action.

4.4 Audit reports are retained by the Quality Assurance function in accordance with the Company's document retention policy and are made available to senior management, the Compliance function and the NIR on request.
