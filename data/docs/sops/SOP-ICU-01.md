## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP governs the assessment and processing of claims involving treatment in an Intensive Care Unit (ICU) or Intensive Coronary Care Unit (ICCU) under health insurance policies issued by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. Under the applicable Policy Wording (Clause 8.1), ICU and ICCU charges are covered at actuals without any monetary cap per day, subject to the admission being Medically Necessary and confirmed as such by the treating physician. This SOP applies to both cashless and reimbursement claims across Health Gold and Health Silver policies and must be read alongside SOP-BILLREV-01 for the review of ICU line items in the hospital bill.

## Section 2 Verifying ICU Admission

2.1 For ICU charges to be assessed under the applicable Policy Wording, the admission to the ICU or ICCU must be Medically Necessary as defined in Clause 1.2.3 of the Policy Wording. The adjuster must confirm that medical necessity is documented by the treating physician.

2.2 The adjuster must verify: (a) that the hospital's records confirm the Insured Person was admitted to a designated ICU or ICCU, as opposed to a high-dependency unit or general ward with enhanced monitoring; (b) that the treating physician has documented the specific clinical reasons for the ICU or ICCU admission; and (c) that the number of days attributed to ICU or ICCU care in the bill corresponds to the period confirmed in the clinical records.

2.3 If the ICU admission notation on the discharge summary is unclear or generic, the adjuster must request clarification from the hospital in the form of daily nursing observations, monitoring charts or a physician's written statement confirming the nature and duration of ICU care provided.

2.4 The adjuster must also check that the ICU stay duration is consistent with the stated diagnosis and the clinical course documented throughout the discharge summary.

## Section 3 Reviewing ICU Charges

3.1 ICU charges typically include: the daily ICU or ICCU bed charge; monitoring and life-support equipment charges; nursing charges specific to intensive care; and specialist physician visit charges for the ICU period. Each of these categories must be identified separately in the itemised bill.

3.2 The adjuster must verify that the charges attributed to the ICU period in the itemised bill correspond to the number of ICU days confirmed in the clinical records. Any discrepancy between the number of days billed and the number confirmed in the clinical records must be queried with the hospital before the assessment is finalised.

3.3 Charges for medicines, blood and blood products, diagnostic tests and medical devices during the ICU stay are assessed for medical necessity and are subject to the applicable policy terms. These are covered as part of the in-patient hospitalisation expenses, not as a separate ICU-specific benefit.

3.4 Standard ward room charges and ICU charges may not both be billed for the same calendar day. The adjuster must check for such overlaps in the itemised bill and raise a written query to the hospital for any day on which both categories of charge appear.

## Section 4 Documentation

4.1 ICU claims must be supported by: the discharge summary confirming the period of ICU or ICCU admission and the clinical reasons for the admission; the daily ICU observation or monitoring charts for the period of ICU stay, or a physician's written confirmation where monitoring charts are not available; the original itemised final bill showing ICU and ICCU charges as separate line items; and all other mandatory documents set out in SOP-DOCS-01.

4.2 Where the hospital is unable to provide ICU monitoring charts because they form part of internal records not routinely supplied to patients, the adjuster may accept a physician's written statement confirming the ICU admission dates and the clinical justification for the admission, provided the statement is consistent with the discharge summary.

4.3 All findings from the ICU admission verification and charge review must be recorded in the claims management system before the financial assessment is finalised. Any query raised during the review and the hospital's response must be retained as part of the claim file.
