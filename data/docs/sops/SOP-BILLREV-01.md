## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP governs the review of itemised hospital bills submitted in support of health insurance claims under policies issued by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. The itemised bill is the primary document used to assess the financial quantum of a claim. An itemised bill that has not been carefully reviewed can lead to inaccurate settlements, overbillings or underpayments that harm both the Insured Person and the Company. This SOP applies to all claims involving an itemised hospital bill and must be followed by all claims adjusters as part of the standard assessment process.

## Section 2 Reading an Itemised Bill

2.1 An itemised bill from a hospital should break down charges by category. The adjuster must identify and separately verify the following key categories: (a) room charges, including the room type and the daily rate for each day of admission; (b) ICU or ICCU charges, where the Insured Person was admitted to intensive care; (c) surgeon, anaesthetist and consultant fees; (d) nursing charges; (e) operation theatre charges for surgical cases; (f) diagnostic tests and investigations ordered during the admission; (g) medicines and consumables; and (h) any separately itemised charges for medical equipment or devices used during the procedure or admission.

2.2 The total of all individual line items in the bill must reconcile to the final bill total. Any discrepancy between the sum of the individual items and the stated total must be queried with the hospital before the assessment is finalised.

2.3 For bills that span a period including both general ward and ICU days, the adjuster must verify that the room charges and ICU charges are attributed to the correct days and do not overlap.

## Section 3 Items Needing Clarification

3.1 The adjuster must raise a written query to the hospital for any of the following: (a) a line item that does not correspond to anything recorded in the clinical notes, discharge summary or operation notes; (b) a line item described only by a code, number or abbreviation that cannot be identified from standard medical billing references; (c) a line item that appears more than once without a clinical justification for the repetition; (d) a charge for a medicine or medical device that does not appear in the treating physician's prescription or operation notes; and (e) a room charge category that differs from what the Insured Person stated at the time of pre-authorisation.

3.2 Queries must be submitted to the hospital in writing and must specify the exact line items in question. The hospital's response must be received and reviewed before the assessment proceeds to the financial determination stage.

3.3 Where a line item is queried and the hospital confirms it is correct, the adjuster must note the confirmation and the basis for it in the claims management system. Where the hospital confirms the item was billed in error, the corrected figure is used for the assessment, and the hospital should be asked to issue a revised bill.

## Section 4 Recording Findings

4.1 On completing the bill review, the adjuster must record in the claims management system: (a) a summary of each major category reviewed and whether it was accepted, queried or disallowed; (b) the reason for any query or disallowance, with the applicable policy clause where relevant; and (c) the total eligible amount after any adjustments arising from the review.

4.2 Where a bill is accepted in full without any queries or disallowances, the adjuster must record a brief confirmation in the claims management system that the bill review was completed and no items required clarification or adjustment.

4.3 All queries raised during the bill review and all responses received form part of the claim file and must be retained in accordance with the Company's document retention policy. These records are subject to review during quality audits under SOP-QUALITY-01.

4.4 Bill review findings feed directly into the financial assessment. The adjuster must not confirm the final settlement amount until the bill review is fully complete, all queries have been resolved, and the findings have been recorded.
