## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP defines the steps to be followed by service and claims staff when receiving and processing cashless hospitalisation pre-authorisation requests under Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. health insurance policies. It covers both planned admissions and emergency admissions at Network Hospitals.

The cashless facility allows an Insured Person to receive covered treatment at a Network Hospital without paying upfront, subject to pre-authorisation under Clause 7.1 of the applicable Policy Wording. This SOP ensures pre-authorisation requests are processed promptly, eligibility is verified correctly, and decisions are issued within the mandated turnaround times.

This SOP applies to all health insurance products administered by the Company. Where product-specific rules differ, the adjuster must refer to the applicable policy wording version determined by the date of loss.

## Section 2 Pre-authorisation Steps

2.1 Receive the pre-authorisation request from the hospital or Insured Person through the designated channel (helpline, portal or third-party administrator system). Log the date and time of receipt and generate a unique pre-auth reference number in the claims management system.

2.2 Verify that the hospital is listed in the Company's current Network Hospital list. If the hospital is not on the network, advise the hospital and Insured Person that the cashless facility is unavailable and direct them to the reimbursement route under Clause 7.2. Log rejection code CL-501.

2.3 Verify that the Policy is in force on the proposed admission date: confirm the policy number, policy period, policyholder identity and that no lapse has occurred. If the policy is not in force, log CL-505 and decline the pre-auth.

2.4 Confirm that notification was received within the required time: at least 48 hours before the planned admission date (Clause 6.1.1) or within 24 hours of admission for emergency hospitalisation (Clause 6.1.2). Record the notification timestamp.

2.5 Obtain clinical information from the hospital: provisional diagnosis, proposed treatment or procedure, estimated length of stay and estimated cost. Check whether the procedure appears on the Company's current Day Care list.

2.6 Review clinical details against applicable waiting periods as per the policy wording version applicable (Clause 4.2), and against permanent and specific exclusions in Clauses 5.1 to 5.4. Issue the pre-authorisation decision specifying the approved amount, any applicable sub-limits and any conditions.

## Section 3 Turnaround and Escalation

The pre-authorisation decision must be communicated to the hospital within 1 hour of receiving the complete pre-authorisation request. For final discharge authorisation, the decision must be issued within 3 hours of receipt of the hospital's final bill.

3.1 If the decision cannot be issued within 1 hour, the adjuster must immediately escalate to the team supervisor, noting the pre-auth reference number and the reason for delay. The supervisor must review the case within 30 minutes of escalation.

3.2 Where clinical information is insufficient to make a decision, the adjuster must contact the hospital immediately to request the missing details and document the date and time of the request. The turnaround clock is paused from the time the information request is logged until the information is received.

3.3 Where a pre-authorisation cannot be approved owing to a waiting period or exclusion, a written decline notice must be issued to the hospital and Insured Person, citing the specific policy clause.

3.4 All pre-authorisation decisions, escalations and communications must be recorded in the claims management system with timestamps to create a complete audit trail.

## Section 4 Common Errors

The following errors occur frequently during cashless pre-authorisation processing. Adjusters must check for these proactively before issuing a decision.

4.1 Non-network hospital: the requesting hospital does not appear on the current network list. Do not issue a pre-authorisation. Advise the reimbursement route and log CL-501.

4.2 Policy not in force: the Policy has lapsed or the admission date falls outside the policy period. Do not issue a pre-authorisation. Advise the Policyholder to contact customer service and log CL-505.

4.3 Identity mismatch: the Insured Person named in the pre-auth request does not match the Policy Schedule. Request identity verification from the hospital before proceeding. Do not issue a pre-authorisation until identity is confirmed.

4.4 Incomplete clinical details: the pre-auth form lacks a diagnosis, proposed treatment or estimated cost. Contact the hospital for the missing information promptly; the 1-hour turnaround clock is paused until details are provided.

4.5 Late notification: the notification was not received within the time required by Clause 6.1. Document the late notification; do not decline solely on this basis without first giving the Insured Person an opportunity to explain, and escalate for a condonation decision if warranted.
