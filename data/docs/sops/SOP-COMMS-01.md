## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP establishes the standards for all customer-facing communications issued by claims adjusters and service staff at Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. Clear, respectful and accurate communication with Insured Persons and Policyholders is both a regulatory requirement and a core service obligation. It reduces complaints, helps Policyholders exercise their rights and ensures that claim decisions are understood. This SOP applies to all written and verbal communications with customers relating to health insurance policies and claims. It must be read alongside the customer scripts in the SCR series and the rejection communication process in SOP-REJCOMM-01.

## Section 2 Tone and Language

2.1 All customer communications must be in formal Indian English. The tone must be courteous, professional and empathetic. The agent must acknowledge the customer's situation before delivering a decision or making a document request.

2.2 Plain language must be used at all times. Technical insurance terms must be explained in everyday language. If a policy clause must be referenced, the agent must also describe what that clause means to the customer in practical terms.

2.3 The agent must address the customer by name and must confirm the customer's identity before discussing any claim or policy details. The claim reference number and policy number must be quoted in every substantive communication.

2.4 The agent must not make promises about outcomes that have not been confirmed in the claims management system, speculate about how a pending assessment will conclude, or volunteer information about other Insured Persons or claims on the same policy.

## Section 3 Written Communication

3.1 All written communications — whether by letter, email or customer portal message — must: state the claim reference number and policy number; clearly identify the purpose of the communication; cite the specific policy clause where a decision is based on a policy term; include the contact details for the customer helpline or the responsible agent; and inform the Policyholder of the grievance route available under Clause 10.1 of the applicable Policy Wording.

3.2 Rejection and partial disallowance letters must be prepared in accordance with SOP-REJCOMM-01 and must meet the NIR's requirements as set out in CIR-02. The letter must cite the specific clause, state the rejection code and explain the reason in plain language.

3.3 Document request communications must list each outstanding document clearly and specifically. A single, consolidated deficiency notice must be issued for all missing documents, rather than multiple separate requests at different times, in accordance with the NIR's requirements summarised in CIR-05.

3.4 Draft written communications prepared by a new adjuster, or for a case that is likely to be contentious, must be reviewed by the team supervisor before despatch. The supervisor's review must be documented in the claims management system.

## Section 4 Recording Customer Contact

4.1 Every customer contact — whether outbound call, inbound call, email, letter or portal message — must be recorded in the claims management system under the relevant claim or policy reference. The record must include the date and time, the method of contact, the name of the agent and a brief summary of what was discussed and any action committed to.

4.2 Where a customer calls to dispute a decision or express dissatisfaction, the agent must note the nature of the dispute, explain the grievance process to the customer, and inform the team supervisor. The supervisor must be involved where the dispute escalates during the call.

4.3 Call recordings, where made, must be retained in accordance with the Company's document retention policy and are subject to quality review under SOP-QUALITY-01. Agents must conduct themselves during calls as if the call is being recorded at all times.

4.4 Customer contact records must not be edited or deleted once created. Any correction to a contact record must be entered as a new entry that references the original, with a note explaining the reason for the correction.
