## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP covers the handling of queries raised by Network Hospitals concerning an existing pre-authorisation decision and of requests from hospitals for an enhancement of an already-issued pre-authorisation amount. The initial pre-authorisation process is governed by SOP-CASHLESS-01. This SOP applies where the initial pre-authorisation has been issued and the hospital requires clarification, correction or an increase in the approved amount.

Under NIR guidelines (CIR-01), the Company must respond to cashless requests promptly. Queries and enhancement requests are part of the cashless communication cycle and must be handled with the same level of urgency as initial pre-authorisation requests. All interactions with the hospital must be recorded in the claims management system under the original pre-authorisation reference.

## Section 2 Handling Queries from the Hospital

2.1 A Network Hospital may raise a query about a pre-authorisation decision for the following reasons: the approved amount is below the hospital's expected tariff rate; the approval is conditional on submission of additional documents; or specific items or procedures have been excluded from the pre-authorisation.

2.2 On receipt of a query, the adjuster must: (a) retrieve the original pre-authorisation record from the claims management system; (b) review the basis for the original decision, including the clinical details submitted by the hospital and the applicable Policy Wording terms; (c) determine whether the query relates to a clinical or administrative matter; and (d) respond to the hospital within the same pre-authorisation turnaround timeline specified in SOP-CASHLESS-01 and CIR-01.

2.3 If the review reveals that an error was made in the original decision, the adjuster must prepare a correction, obtain supervisor approval where required by the claims authority matrix, and issue a revised pre-authorisation promptly.

2.4 If the original decision was correct, the adjuster must communicate the reasons to the hospital clearly, citing the applicable policy clause. Internal decision rationale beyond what is appropriate for external communication must not be disclosed.

## Section 3 Requests for Enhancement

3.1 An enhancement request arises when the hospital's actual or anticipated costs for the admitted patient exceed the amount pre-authorised. This may occur because the clinical condition has changed, additional procedures are required, or the length of stay has extended beyond the original estimate.

3.2 On receipt of an enhancement request, the adjuster must: (a) obtain updated clinical information from the hospital explaining the reason for the additional costs; (b) review the information against the applicable policy terms and any procedure-specific sub-limits stated in Clause 3.2 of the Policy Wording; (c) determine the additional eligible amount; and (d) issue an enhanced approval, a partial approval with reasons, or a written decline with reasons and clause reference.

3.3 Enhancement requests must be treated with the same urgency as initial pre-authorisation requests. The 1-hour decision timeline specified in CIR-01 applies to enhancement decisions as well.

3.4 Where an enhancement is declined, the hospital must be informed in writing of the reason and the applicable policy clause. The Insured Person may be informed at the hospital's discretion; the adjuster must not contact the Insured Person directly about an enhancement decline without supervisor approval.

## Section 4 Recording Decisions

4.1 Every query, every enhancement request and every decision — including the basis for the decision — must be recorded in the claims management system under the original pre-authorisation reference number.

4.2 Where an enhancement is approved, the system record must clearly show: the original approved amount; the additional amount approved; the revised total; and the clinical justification provided by the hospital.

4.3 Where a query or enhancement request is declined, the record must include the reason for the decline and the specific policy clause applied. The decline must also be communicated to the hospital in writing.

4.4 All records relating to pre-authorisation queries and enhancements form part of the overall claim file and are retained in accordance with the Company's document retention policy. These records are accessible during quality audits under SOP-QUALITY-01.
