## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP defines the process for receiving, registering and assessing reimbursement claims submitted under Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. health insurance policies. It applies to all claims where the Insured Person has obtained treatment at a non-network hospital, or where the cashless facility was not availed for any reason.

Under reimbursement, the Insured Person pays the hospital directly and submits a claim to the Company for reimbursement of eligible expenses. The process must ensure that claims are registered promptly, eligibility is assessed accurately and settlement is issued within the timelines prescribed by the National Insurance Regulator.

This SOP should be read in conjunction with SOP-DOCS-01 (document requirements) and SOP-REJCOMM-01 (rejection communications).

## Section 2 Intake and Registration

2.1 Receive the reimbursement claim through the designated channel (post, email, walk-in or online portal) and record the exact date of receipt in the claims management system.

2.2 Check whether the claim documents were submitted within 30 days of the date of discharge as required by Clause 6.2.1. If documents are received after this period, log the late submission. Do not reject solely on grounds of late submission without first requesting a written explanation from the claimant; refer the condonation decision to the team supervisor if warranted.

2.3 Conduct a document completeness check using the checklist in SOP-DOCS-01. If any mandatory document is missing, issue a written deficiency notice to the claimant within 7 working days of receipt, listing each missing item. Log the claim as pending on rejection code CL-104 until documents are received.

2.4 Once all mandatory documents have been received, register the claim as complete, generate a unique claim reference number and communicate it to the claimant. Record the date of receipt of the last necessary document in the system, as this date triggers the settlement clock.

## Section 3 Assessment Steps

3.1 Verify that the Policy was in force on the date of loss: confirm the policy period, renewal status and premium payment record.

3.2 Confirm the identity of the Insured Person by matching the KYC document submitted with the name, date of birth and other details on the Policy Schedule.

3.3 Determine the applicable policy wording version by matching the date of loss to the effective period of the relevant wording document. All waiting period and benefit assessments must be made using the wording version applicable to the date of loss.

3.4 Review the date of loss against the policy start date and assess the initial waiting period (Clause 4.1) and any applicable specific waiting periods as per the policy wording version applicable (Clause 4.2). Where a specific waiting period applies, record the reason and the applicable wording version.

3.5 Review the diagnosis and treatment against the permanent exclusions (Clause 5.1), specific exclusions (Clause 5.2), experimental treatment exclusion (Clause 5.3) and dental and cosmetic treatment provisions (Clause 5.4), all as per the applicable Policy Wording.

3.6 Review the room category against the room rent limit as per the policy wording version applicable (Clause 8.1). If the room category exceeds the eligible limit, calculate the proportionate deduction under Clause 8.2 and document the calculation.

3.7 Apply applicable sub-limits from Clause 3.2. For procedures subject to a sub-limit, the payable amount is capped at the sub-limit value.

3.8 Where medical necessity is in doubt, seek an independent medical opinion at the Company's expense before finalising the assessment.

## Section 4 Payment and Closure

4.1 Issue the settlement decision within 30 days of receipt of the last necessary document. Where investigation is required, settlement must be completed within 45 days of receipt of the last necessary document.

4.2 For approved claims, initiate the electronic fund transfer to the Policyholder's bank account as stated in the claim documents. Issue a written settlement confirmation letter specifying the amount paid, the components of the settlement and any items disallowed.

4.3 For partial settlements, the letter must clearly itemise: the amount approved; each amount disallowed; and the specific policy clause or reason for each disallowance. Partial settlements are not considered final until the claimant has acknowledged receipt or the grievance period has elapsed.

4.4 For rejections, issue the rejection communication in accordance with SOP-REJCOMM-01 within 15 days of receipt of the last necessary document, citing the specific policy clause and the applicable rejection code from the rejection codes reference table.

4.5 Close the claim in the claims management system, recording the outcome (approved, partial, rejected), the settlement or rejection date, the amount paid and all applicable clause references. Retain the claim file in accordance with the Company's document retention policy.
