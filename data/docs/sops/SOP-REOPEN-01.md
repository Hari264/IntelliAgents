## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP defines the process for reopening a health insurance claim that has been closed — whether settled, partially settled or rejected — under Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. policies. A claim may be reopened only on the grounds specified in Section 2. Reopening is not automatic and requires approval as specified in Section 4.

The purpose of this SOP is to ensure that genuine errors and new evidence are addressed in a fair and timely manner, while preventing the routine reversal of claim decisions without adequate justification. Claim reopening is not a substitute for the grievance process under Clause 10.1 of the applicable Policy Wording; however, a grievance upheld by the Grievance Redressal Officer or the Insurance Ombudsman is itself a valid ground for reopening under Section 2.

## Section 2 Grounds for Reopening

A closed claim may be reopened only where at least one of the following grounds is established.

2.1 New or additional medical evidence: the Policyholder or Insured Person submits medical evidence that was not available at the time of the original assessment and that materially affects the eligibility or quantum of the claim.

2.2 Administrative error: an error in the original assessment is identified, such as an incorrect waiting period calculation, an incorrect sub-limit applied, an arithmetic error in the settlement calculation, or the wrong policy wording version used to assess the claim.

2.3 Document processing error: a mandatory document was submitted by the claimant within the required period but was not included in the claim file due to an internal processing error, resulting in an incorrect rejection under CL-104.

2.4 Grievance decision: the Company's Grievance Redressal Officer or the Insurance Ombudsman has directed that the claim be reconsidered or re-assessed on specified grounds.

2.5 Legal or regulatory direction: a court order or regulatory direction requires the Company to re-assess the claim.

2.6 Policy correction: an error in the Policy Schedule (such as a wrong policy start date or incorrect sum insured) has been corrected through an endorsement, and the correction materially affects the claim outcome.

## Section 3 Steps

3.1 Application: the request to reopen a claim may be initiated by the claimant (in writing, citing the ground for reopening) or by the adjuster or supervisor who identifies an internal error. All requests must be documented in the claims management system under the original claim reference number.

3.2 Preliminary review: the team supervisor must review the reopening request within 5 working days of receipt. The supervisor must determine whether a valid ground under Section 2 has been established and record their finding.

3.3 If a valid ground is established, the supervisor must notify the claimant that the claim is being reopened for reassessment and communicate the expected timeline for the revised decision. The claim status in the system must be updated to "reopened" and the original settlement or rejection must not be reversed until the reassessment is complete.

3.4 Reassessment: the claim is reassessed by a different adjuster from the one who handled the original assessment, where operationally possible, to ensure objectivity. The reassessment must address the specific ground for reopening and must consider all evidence in the claim file including the new material.

3.5 Revised decision: the reassessed claim must be decided within 30 days of the reopening approval date. The revised settlement or rejection must be communicated to the claimant in writing, citing the grounds for the revision and the specific policy clauses applied.

## Section 4 Approvals Required

All claim reopening decisions require formal approval before the claim status is changed in the claims management system.

4.1 Minor amendments — corrections to arithmetic errors, administrative errors or document processing errors where the change in payable amount is within the financial authority limit defined in the Company's claims authority matrix — require approval from the team supervisor.

4.2 Significant changes — including any reassessment that reverses a rejection, increases the payable amount beyond the supervisor's financial authority limit, or involves a change in the wording version applied — require approval from the Senior Claims Manager or equivalent.

4.3 Grievance-directed reopenings — where the reopening follows a direction from the Grievance Redressal Officer or the Insurance Ombudsman — require acknowledgement from the Grievance Redressal Officer and notification to the Compliance function.

4.4 Legally or regulatorily directed reopenings require review by the Legal or Compliance function before approval is granted.

4.5 All approvals must be documented in the claims management system with the approver's name, designation and the date of approval. No claim may be settled on the revised basis without a recorded approval.
