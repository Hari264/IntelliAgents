## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP defines the escalation and grievance handling process for Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. health insurance claims and service interactions. It applies to all complaints, disputes and requests for reconsideration arising from claim decisions, service interactions, policy servicing or any other matter relating to health policies administered by the Company.

This SOP ensures that grievances are acknowledged, investigated and resolved within the timelines required by the National Insurance Regulator and as set out in Clause 10.1 of the applicable Policy Wording. It must be followed by all claims adjusters and service staff when a Policyholder or Insured Person expresses dissatisfaction with any decision or service. All escalation actions must be documented in the claims management system with timestamps and the name of the staff member handling each step.

## Section 2 Escalation Levels

Escalations follow a four-level structure. Each level must be exhausted before escalating to the next, unless the nature of the complaint or a specific directive requires immediate elevation.

Level 1 — Claims Adjuster or Service Agent: the first point of contact for all grievances. The agent must attempt to resolve the grievance immediately where possible. If the issue cannot be resolved at this level within the same working day, it must be escalated to Level 2.

Level 2 — Team Supervisor: reviews the complaint and the underlying claim or service interaction. The supervisor has authority to overturn decisions within defined financial limits, direct a re-assessment of the claim and communicate a revised position to the Policyholder.

Level 3 — Grievance Redressal Officer: the designated officer responsible for formal grievance resolution in accordance with Clause 10.1 of the Policy Wording and NIR guidelines. The Policyholder must be directed in writing to submit their grievance to the Grievance Redressal Officer if Level 2 does not resolve the matter.

Level 4 — Insurance Ombudsman: an external escalation available to the Policyholder if the Company's internal resolution is unsatisfactory or if the grievance remains unresolved within the mandated timeline. The Company must inform the Policyholder of their right to approach the Ombudsman in writing.

## Section 3 Grievance Timelines

The following timelines are mandatory and must be tracked in the claims management system for every grievance received.

3.1 Acknowledgement: every written grievance must be acknowledged within 3 working days of receipt. The acknowledgement must confirm receipt, state the grievance reference number, name the team or officer handling the matter and give an indicative timeline for resolution.

3.2 Resolution: every grievance must be resolved and a written resolution communicated to the Policyholder within 14 days of receipt. Where the grievance cannot be resolved within 14 days owing to the complexity of the investigation required, the Policyholder must be informed in writing of the extended timeline and the reason for the extension before the 14-day period expires.

3.3 Ombudsman escalation: if the Policyholder is not satisfied with the resolution provided, or if the grievance remains unresolved beyond the 14-day period, the Policyholder has the right to escalate to the Insurance Ombudsman having jurisdiction over their place of residence. The Company must provide the relevant Ombudsman contact details upon request.

3.4 All timelines run from the date the written grievance is received by the Company, regardless of the channel through which it was submitted.

## Section 4 Records

The Company is required to maintain a complete and auditable record of all grievances received, the actions taken and the outcomes, in accordance with NIR requirements.

4.1 All grievances must be logged in the grievance register maintained by the Grievance Redressal Officer. The register must capture: the date of receipt; the Policyholder name and policy number; the nature of the grievance; the claim reference number (if applicable); the date of acknowledgement; the date of resolution; the resolution outcome; and, where applicable, whether the case was escalated to the Ombudsman.

4.2 The claims management system must be updated at each stage of the grievance process. Staff must record the date and nature of every communication with the Policyholder and every internal decision taken.

4.3 Grievance records must be retained in accordance with the Company's document retention policy. The grievance register is subject to periodic review by the Compliance function and may be requested by the NIR during regulatory inspections.

4.4 Aggregate grievance data must be compiled monthly and submitted to the Management Review Committee to identify systemic issues and drive process improvements.
