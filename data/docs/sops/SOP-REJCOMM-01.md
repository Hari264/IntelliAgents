## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP defines the process for communicating claim rejections and partial disallowances to Insured Persons and Policyholders under Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. health insurance policies. It applies to all rejections, including full rejections, partial disallowances and cases where a sub-limit or exclusion reduces the payable amount below the claimed amount.

Under the National Insurance Regulator's guidelines (summarised in CIR-02), the Company is required to communicate the reasons for a rejection in writing, citing the specific policy clause, within 15 days of receipt of the last necessary document. Failure to meet this requirement may expose the Company to complaints, regulatory findings and interest liability.

This SOP applies to all health insurance claims. It must be read in conjunction with SOP-REIMB-01 (claim assessment) and the rejection codes reference table.

## Section 2 Writing the Rejection Letter

Every rejection or partial disallowance must be communicated to the Policyholder in a written rejection letter. The rejection letter must:

2.1 State the claim reference number, policy number, Insured Person's name, date of loss and the hospital name, so that the Policyholder can identify the specific claim.

2.2 Clearly state the ground or grounds for rejection. Each ground must cite the specific clause of the applicable Policy Wording, for example: "Your claim has been assessed and the expenses are not payable because the date of loss falls within the specific waiting period under Clause 4.2 of the Health Gold Policy Wording applicable to your policy."

2.3 Where the rejection is based on a waiting period or an exclusion, reference the clause number and the heading of the relevant clause. Do not use only the rejection code; the full clause reference must appear in the body of the letter.

2.4 Inform the Policyholder of their right to lodge a grievance with the Company's Grievance Redressal Officer within the specified timeframe, and of the further right to escalate to the Insurance Ombudsman as described in Clause 10.1.

2.5 Use plain language that is accessible to a lay reader. Avoid internal terminology, abbreviations or references that would not be understood by the Policyholder.

2.6 The rejection letter must be issued within 15 days of receipt of the last necessary document, as required by CIR-02.

## Section 3 Using Rejection Codes

Rejection codes are internal codes that classify the reason for a rejection or disallowance. They are used for system logging, management reporting and as a reference when preparing customer communications. The codes are set out in the Company's rejection codes reference table.

3.1 Select the rejection code that most accurately describes the primary reason for rejection. Where multiple grounds apply, select the primary code for system logging and list all applicable codes in the assessment notes.

3.2 The rejection code alone must not be used as the sole communication to the Policyholder. The rejection letter must state the reason in full (see Section 2). The code is an internal identifier only.

3.3 Key rejection codes for common scenarios include:
CL-101: illness within the initial 1-month waiting period (Clause 4.1).
CL-201: treatment within a specific disease waiting period (Clause 4.2).
CL-205: joint replacement within the applicable waiting period (Clause 4.2).
CL-217: Pre-Existing Disease within the applicable PED waiting period (Clause 4.3).
CL-220: Pre-Existing Disease not disclosed at proposal (Clause 4.3).
CL-305: room rent exceeds eligible limit (Clause 8.1) — this is a partial disallowance, not a full rejection.
CL-401 to CL-420: various exclusion-based rejections (Clauses 5.1 to 5.4).

3.4 For cases under additional review, log rejection code CL-601 as the interim status. The customer must not be informed of the specific reason for additional review at this stage; use the script for CL-601 from SCR-GEN-01.

## Section 4 Customer Call Guidance

After the rejection letter has been issued, the service team must attempt to contact the Policyholder by telephone to explain the rejection. The call must be made within 2 working days of issuing the rejection letter.

4.1 Before making the call, the service agent must review the rejection letter, the applicable rejection codes and the customer script for the relevant rejection reason. Scripts for specific rejection categories are available as follows: SCR-PED-01 for PED-related rejections (CL-217, CL-220); SCR-WAIT-01 for waiting period rejections (CL-101, CL-201, CL-205, CL-510); SCR-ROOM-01 for room rent rejections (CL-305, CL-306); SCR-EXCL-01 for exclusion-based rejections (CL-401 to CL-420); and SCR-GEN-01 for other categories.

4.2 During the call, the agent must: introduce themselves and confirm the identity of the caller; reference the claim number and the rejection letter; explain the rejection reason in plain language; and advise the Policyholder of the grievance route available under Clause 10.1.

4.3 The agent must not make any statement that constitutes an admission of liability or a promise to reverse the rejection. If the Policyholder disputes the rejection, advise them to submit a written grievance to the Grievance Redressal Officer.

4.4 Where the claim has been logged under CL-601 (claim under additional review), the agent must use only the SCR-GEN-01 script for CL-601 and must not disclose the nature of the additional review or the specific reason the claim has been referred.

4.5 Document the call in the claims management system: record the date, time, the identity of the caller and a brief summary of the conversation.
