## Section 1 Purpose and Scope

This SOP defines the process for assessing and settling claims for joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty) under Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. health insurance policies. Joint replacement claims require additional eligibility checks and documentation beyond the standard reimbursement process and must be handled in accordance with this SOP.

Arthroplasty is defined in the applicable Policy Wording as a surgical procedure in which a diseased or damaged joint is replaced in whole or in part with a prosthetic implant (Clause 1.2.2). The term covers all joint replacement procedures, including but not limited to total knee replacement, total hip replacement and partial joint replacement, regardless of the joint involved.

This SOP applies to all joint replacement claims submitted under Company health insurance policies and must be used in conjunction with SOP-REIMB-01 and SOP-DOCS-01. The adjuster must determine the applicable policy wording version by matching the date of loss to the effective period of the relevant wording document, before applying any eligibility rule.

## Section 2 Eligibility Checks

Before proceeding to financial assessment, the adjuster must complete the following eligibility checks for every joint replacement claim.

2.1 Confirm the policy is in force on the date of loss and that the Insured Person is covered under the Policy Schedule.

2.2 Determine the applicable policy wording version by reference to the date of loss. Check Clause 4.2 of the applicable wording version by date of loss to determine the specific waiting period applicable to joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty). Calculate whether the waiting period had been satisfied as at the date of loss by comparing the date of loss to the policy start date (and any portability credit applied under Clause 9.2).

2.3 Check the Policy Schedule for the presence of the Waiting Period Waiver Rider (RID-WPW-01). If this rider is shown in the Policy Schedule, refer to RID-WPW-01 to determine its effect on the joint replacement waiting period for the applicable wording version.

2.4 Where portability credit has been applied at inception or renewal, verify the credit granted against the prior insurer's renewal certificate and the portability credit confirmation in the policy file. Adjust the effective waiting period start date accordingly.

2.5 Check whether the joint condition may constitute a Pre-Existing Disease as per Clause 4.3 of the applicable Policy Wording. Review the proposal form and any prior medical records for disclosure of joint-related conditions.

## Section 3 Documents Checklist

The following documents are required for all joint replacement claims, in addition to the mandatory documents set out in SOP-DOCS-01 Section 2.

3.1 Original discharge summary from the treating Hospital, confirming the diagnosis, the joint or joints treated, the procedure performed (specifying whether total or partial replacement), the implant type and the dates of admission and discharge.

3.2 Original implant invoice with the manufacturer's sticker affixed. The sticker must show the implant serial number, make, model and batch number. Claims without the implant sticker cannot be settled for implant costs.

3.3 Pre-operative X-ray or MRI report demonstrating the joint condition that necessitated the surgery. The report must be dated before the date of surgery and must identify the affected joint.

3.4 Written advice or referral letter from the treating orthopaedic surgeon recommending joint replacement surgery, with reasons. The letter must identify the joint to be replaced and the clinical basis for surgery.

3.5 Original itemised final bill from the Hospital, with room charges, surgeon fees, implant costs and all other components separately itemised.

3.6 Copy of the Insured Person's valid photo identity document (KYC).

3.7 Cancelled cheque or bank account details of the Policyholder for electronic fund transfer.

## Section 4 Assessment Steps

4.1 Confirm waiting period eligibility as determined in Section 2. If the waiting period as per the applicable wording version had not been satisfied on the date of loss, and the Waiting Period Waiver Rider does not apply, apply rejection code CL-205 and proceed to SOP-REJCOMM-01.

4.2 Confirm that the claim is for a surgical joint replacement procedure. Conservative management of joint conditions (physiotherapy, injections, bracing) does not qualify as Arthroplasty and is not payable under the joint replacement provision.

4.3 Apply the joint replacement sub-limit from Clause 3.2 of the applicable Policy Wording to the eligible expenses. Where more than one joint was replaced in the same hospitalisation, the sub-limit applies separately per joint. Record the sub-limit calculation in the assessment notes.

4.4 Review the room category against the room rent limit as per the policy wording version applicable (Clause 8.1). If the room category exceeds the eligible limit, calculate and apply the proportionate deduction under Clause 8.2.

4.5 Assess whether the joint condition may be linked to a Pre-Existing Disease under Clause 4.3. If a linkage is identified, refer to SOP-PEDVER-01 for verification steps before finalising the assessment.

4.6 Confirm the implant invoice and sticker are present and that the implant cost is supported. Implant costs are excluded from proportionate deduction under Clause 8.2.3.

## Section 5 Exceptions to Check

The following matters require particular attention in joint replacement claims and must be specifically documented in the assessment notes before the claim is finalised.

5.1 Joint replacement sub-limit (Clause 3.2): confirm the applicable sub-limit per joint under the applicable Policy Wording and apply it to each joint replaced. Where the claimed amount exceeds the sub-limit, the excess is not payable under any other benefit.

5.2 Room rent limit (Clause 8.1): confirm the eligible room rent as per the policy wording version applicable (Clause 8.1). If the Insured Person occupied a higher room category, calculate the proportionate deduction in accordance with Clause 8.2 and document the calculation fully.

5.3 Pre-Existing Disease linkage (Clause 4.3): check whether the joint condition, or any contributing diagnosis such as osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis or avascular necrosis, was present or treated within the Pre-Existing Disease lookback period as defined in Clause 1.2.1. If a PED linkage is identified, assess whether the PED waiting period as per the applicable Policy Wording (Clause 4.3) had been satisfied on the date of loss.

5.4 Waiting Period Waiver Rider (RID-WPW-01): check the Policy Schedule for the presence of this rider. If the rider is endorsed, refer to RID-WPW-01 for its specific terms and conditions before applying any joint replacement waiting period.

5.5 Portability credit (Clause 9.2): if portability credit was granted at inception or renewal, verify that the credit has been correctly applied in the policy file. Confirm the effective waiting period commencement date and whether it reduces the outstanding waiting period to zero as at the date of loss.
