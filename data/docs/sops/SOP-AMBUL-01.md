## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP governs the processing of road ambulance claims under Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. health insurance policies. Under the applicable Policy Wording (Clause 2.5), the Company reimburses charges for a registered road ambulance used to transport the Insured Person to a Hospital in connection with a covered hospitalisation event. This SOP applies to both cashless and reimbursement claims. The road ambulance benefit is a supplementary benefit that sits alongside the main hospitalisation cover; it is subject to its own limit and must be processed as a distinct line item within the overall claim record.

## Section 2 Eligibility

2.1 To qualify for road ambulance reimbursement, all of the following must be satisfied: (a) the vehicle must be a registered emergency medical services vehicle operating as a designated road ambulance; (b) the ambulance must have been used to transport the Insured Person to a Hospital in connection with a hospitalisation that is itself covered under the Policy; and (c) the hospitalisation must have resulted in a covered in-patient admission or a covered Day Care Procedure.

2.2 Private cars, taxis, auto-rickshaws, app-based cab services and any other conveyance that is not a registered road ambulance are not eligible for reimbursement under this benefit, regardless of the circumstances of the journey.

2.3 Where the underlying hospitalisation is assessed as not payable under the Policy, the road ambulance claim is also not payable.

2.4 The road ambulance reimbursement is limited to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation event, as stated in the applicable Policy Wording (Clause 2.5). This limit applies regardless of the number of ambulance journeys associated with the event or the actual amount charged.

## Section 3 Documents Required

3.1 The claim must be supported by: an original receipt or invoice from the ambulance service provider, clearly showing the date of the journey, the origin and destination, the name of the patient transported and the amount charged; and confirmation that the destination was a registered Hospital.

3.2 If the ambulance invoice does not clearly identify the vehicle as a registered road ambulance, the adjuster must request the vehicle registration details or a letter from the ambulance service provider confirming its registration status and the nature of the service provided.

3.3 Road ambulance claim documents must be submitted together with the main hospitalisation claim documents, within 30 days of the date of discharge from the Hospital.

## Section 4 Settlement Notes

4.1 The road ambulance reimbursement is paid in addition to the in-patient hospitalisation expenses and does not form part of any procedure sub-limit listed in Clause 3.2 of the applicable Policy Wording.

4.2 The ₹2,000 road ambulance reimbursement forms part of the overall claims expenditure against the Insured Person's sum insured for the Policy Year and reduces the available sum insured balance accordingly.

4.3 Where the ambulance invoice amount is ₹2,000 or less, the full invoice amount is payable, subject to submission of original documents and satisfaction of the eligibility conditions in Section 2.

4.4 Where the invoice amount exceeds ₹2,000, the Company pays ₹2,000 and the balance above that amount is not payable under this benefit. The settlement letter must state the amount paid, the applicable clause reference and the limit applied.

4.5 Road ambulance claims are entered in the claims management system as a separate line item within the overall hospitalisation claim record so that road ambulance reimbursements are tracked separately from hospitalisation expenses for quality and reporting purposes.
