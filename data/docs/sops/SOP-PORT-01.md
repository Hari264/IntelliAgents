## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP governs the processing of portability requests under health insurance policies issued by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. Under the applicable Policy Wording (Clause 9.2), an Insured Person who has maintained Continuous Cover under a prior registered health insurer's policy may apply to transfer to Aarogya Shield with credit for the waiting periods already served under the prior policy. This SOP is to be followed by the underwriting and policy administration teams when receiving and processing portability requests. The purpose is to ensure consistent, accurate and timely application of portability credits in accordance with NIR portability guidelines.

## Section 2 Receiving the Request

2.1 To avail of portability credit, the Policyholder must submit a portability application to the Company at least 45 days before the renewal date of the prior policy, as required by Clause 9.2.2 of the applicable Policy Wording.

2.2 On receipt of a portability application, the administrator must: (a) record the date of receipt in the system; (b) confirm that the application was received at least 45 days before the renewal date of the prior policy; and (c) issue a written acknowledgement to the Policyholder promptly.

2.3 Applications received less than 45 days before the prior policy's renewal date may be declined at the Company's discretion in accordance with the Policy Wording. The reason for any such decline must be communicated to the Policyholder in writing.

2.4 The portability application must be supported by copies of the prior insurer's policy documents, the renewal certificate confirming the renewal history, and any prior portability credit confirmation received in the event of earlier transfers from other insurers.

## Section 3 Verifying Prior Cover

3.1 The Company must verify that the Insured Person maintained Continuous Cover under the prior policy without a break. Continuous Cover is defined in Clause 1.1.5 of the applicable Policy Wording as uninterrupted health insurance cover, whether under the current policy or a prior insurer's policy.

3.2 To verify prior cover, the administrator must: (a) review the prior insurer's renewal certificates to confirm the period and continuity of cover; (b) check for any lapse period between the prior policy's expiry and the proposed inception date of the new policy; and (c) confirm the prior policy's product type, sum insured and inception date.

3.3 Where the prior cover history is unclear, the Company may verify information through the Insurance Information Bureau or through direct communication with the prior insurer, as permitted by applicable data-sharing frameworks.

3.4 If a lapse is identified in the prior cover history, the portability credit is calculated only for the period of continuous cover completed before the lapse. Any period after the lapse is treated as a fresh commencement of cover for waiting period purposes.

## Section 4 Recording Credit for Waiting Periods Served

4.1 Once prior cover is verified, the administrator must calculate the portability credit applicable to the waiting periods specified in the applicable Policy Wording. The credit equals the number of months of Continuous Cover already completed under the prior policy, up to the applicable waiting period for each condition.

4.2 The portability credit is documented in the policy file and is reflected in the Policy Schedule issued to the Policyholder. The Policy Schedule must clearly show the effective waiting period commencement date for each condition for which credit has been applied.

4.3 Portability credit applies to waiting period credits only. It does not transfer any sum insured, premium, riders, benefit amounts or other terms from the prior insurer. All benefits under the new policy are governed solely by the applicable Policy Wording, as stated in Clause 9.2.4.

4.4 If the portability application is approved with modifications — for example, with partial credit owing to a gap in continuous cover — the administrator must communicate the outcome in writing, stating the specific credit applied to each waiting period and the reason for any partial credit.
