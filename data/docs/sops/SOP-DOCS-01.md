## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP defines the mandatory and procedure-specific document requirements for health insurance claims submitted under Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. policies. It is intended for use by claims adjusters and service staff when assessing document completeness at the intake stage, before any financial assessment begins.

No reimbursement claim should proceed to financial assessment until all mandatory documents have been received. For cashless claims, supporting documents must also be provided to the Company after discharge in accordance with the applicable Policy Wording.

The document requirements in this SOP are drawn from Clause 6.2 of the applicable Policy Wording. Where the applicable wording version affects document requirements (for example, maternity claims), the adjuster must determine the correct wording version by reference to the date of loss before applying this SOP.

## Section 2 Mandatory Documents

The following documents are required for all claim types, regardless of the condition, procedure or hospitalisation type. Each item must be verified for completeness and authenticity before the claim is registered as complete.

2.1 Completed and signed claim form, using the version issued by the Company for the applicable policy.

2.2 Original discharge summary or discharge certificate from the treating Hospital, signed by the treating physician, confirming the principal diagnosis, treatment rendered, and dates of admission and discharge.

2.3 Original itemised final bill from the Hospital showing a line-by-line breakdown of charges, including room charges, ICU charges (if applicable), surgeon and anaesthetist fees, nursing charges, operation theatre charges, medicines, consumables and any other charges.

2.4 All original bills, receipts and payment proofs for all expenses being claimed, including separate bills for pre and post-hospitalisation expenses where applicable.

2.5 All original diagnostic test reports and prescriptions relating directly to the hospitalisation, including pre-operative investigations where surgery has been performed.

2.6 A copy of a valid photo identity document (KYC) of the Insured Person. Acceptable documents are Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport or driving licence.

2.7 A cancelled cheque or written bank account details (account number, IFSC code and bank name) of the Policyholder for electronic fund transfer of the settlement amount.

## Section 3 Procedure-specific Documents

In addition to the mandatory documents in Section 2, the following procedure-specific documents are required for the claim types listed below.

3.1 All surgical claims: post-operative notes signed by the operating surgeon, confirming the procedure performed, anaesthesia used and any intra-operative complications; original invoice for any internal prosthetic device or implant used.

3.2 Joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty): original implant invoice with the manufacturer's sticker affixed (sticker is essential for implant verification); pre-operative X-ray or MRI report demonstrating the joint condition necessitating surgery; written referral or advice letter from the treating orthopaedic surgeon recommending the surgery. Refer to SOP-JOINT-01 for the full checklist and assessment process for joint replacement claims.

3.3 Cataract surgery: operation notes confirming the type of procedure and the lens used; original invoice for the intra-ocular lens implant.

3.4 Domiciliary Hospitalisation: written certification from the treating physician confirming that hospitalisation was not possible or was medically inadvisable and stating the period of home-based treatment; daily treatment records, prescriptions and nursing notes for the entire period.

3.5 Maternity claims (where covered under the applicable policy version): delivery notes or Caesarean section operation notes; paediatrician's assessment where a newborn's expenses are being claimed; confirmation of policy inception date to verify the waiting period as per the policy wording version applicable (Clause 2.6).

3.6 Road ambulance: original receipt or invoice from the registered ambulance service provider clearly stating the date, origin, destination and amount charged.

## Section 4 Handling Missing Documents

4.1 Upon receipt of a claim, complete a document completeness check within 2 working days using the applicable checklist from Sections 2 and 3. If any document is missing or appears incomplete, raise a written deficiency notice to the claimant.

4.2 The deficiency notice must: list each missing or incomplete document by name; state the consequence of non-submission (the claim cannot be assessed until all documents are received); and allow a reasonable time for submission of not less than 15 working days from the date of the notice.

4.3 Log the claim as pending under rejection code CL-104 in the claims management system. The settlement timeline is paused from the date the deficiency notice is issued until the date the outstanding documents are received.

4.4 If the claimant fails to submit the required documents within the specified period, issue a reminder notice. If documents remain outstanding after the reminder period, proceed to formal rejection under CL-104, following the rejection communication process in SOP-REJCOMM-01.

4.5 Where originals have been lost, the claimant must submit a written explanation and a self-declaration. The adjuster may, at their discretion, accept certified duplicate bills from the treating facility. Document the decision to accept duplicates in the claim file with justification.
