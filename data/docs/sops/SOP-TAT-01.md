## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP defines the mandatory turnaround time (TAT) requirements for all health insurance claim types under Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd., in accordance with the National Insurance Regulator's guidelines as summarised in CIR-01. It applies to cashless pre-authorisation, discharge authorisation, reimbursement settlements and rejection communications.

Compliance with TAT requirements is a regulatory obligation. Failure to settle claims within the prescribed periods may result in interest liability, regulatory action and customer complaints. All claims adjusters and service staff are responsible for monitoring TAT at every stage of the claim lifecycle and escalating cases at risk of breach in accordance with Section 4 of this SOP.

TAT clocks are tracked in the claims management system. Staff must update claim milestones in real time to ensure accurate TAT monitoring.

## Section 2 Cashless Timelines

The following timelines apply to cashless pre-authorisation and discharge authorisation at Network Hospitals.

2.1 Pre-authorisation decision: the Company must communicate the pre-authorisation decision to the treating hospital within 1 hour of receiving the complete pre-authorisation request. Where clinical information is missing, the TAT clock is paused from the time the information request is logged until the information is received. Refer to SOP-CASHLESS-01 for the full pre-authorisation process.

2.2 Final discharge authorisation: the Company must issue the final discharge authorisation to the treating hospital within 3 hours of receiving the hospital's final bill. This applies to all cashless admissions, including planned and emergency admissions.

2.3 Where the pre-authorisation decision or discharge authorisation cannot be issued within the prescribed period, the adjuster must immediately escalate to the team supervisor as described in SOP-ESCAL-01. The reason for the delay must be documented in the claims management system.

2.4 If a cashless pre-authorisation request is received outside business hours, the TAT clock commences from the time the request is logged in the system. The on-call adjuster or duty supervisor must be notified for urgent cases.

## Section 3 Reimbursement Timelines

The following timelines apply to reimbursement claims from the date of receipt of the last necessary document.

3.1 Settlement: all reimbursement claims must be settled within 30 days of receipt of the last necessary document. The last necessary document date is recorded in the claims management system at the point when all mandatory and requested documents have been received.

3.2 Settlement with investigation: where an investigation is required before a settlement decision can be made, the claim must be settled within 45 days of receipt of the last necessary document. The decision to treat a claim as requiring investigation must be recorded in the system with the reason.

3.3 Rejection communication: where a claim is to be rejected or partially disallowed, the written rejection communication must be issued within 15 days of receipt of the last necessary document. Refer to SOP-REJCOMM-01 for the rejection communication process.

3.4 Document deficiency notice: if mandatory documents are missing at the time of intake, the deficiency notice must be issued to the claimant within 7 working days of the initial receipt of the claim. The TAT clock for settlement does not commence until the last necessary document is received.

3.5 Claims pending due to document deficiencies must be monitored weekly. If outstanding documents are not received within the period stated in the deficiency notice, follow the process in SOP-DOCS-01 Section 4.

## Section 4 Breach Handling

A TAT breach occurs when any deadline specified in this SOP is missed. Breaches must be identified, escalated and remediated promptly.

4.1 The claims management system generates daily TAT breach alerts for all claims approaching or exceeding their TAT deadline. The team supervisor must review the breach alert report each morning and assign remediation actions.

4.2 Where a settlement TAT breach results in a delayed payment to the Policyholder, the Company is liable to pay interest at 2% above the prevailing bank rate for the period of delay, as required by NIR guidelines (CIR-01). The finance team must be notified to calculate and include the interest amount in the settlement.

4.3 Each TAT breach must be documented in the claims management system with the reason for the breach, the date it was identified and the remediation action taken. Systemic or repeated TAT breaches must be escalated to the Head of Claims and reported in the monthly Management Review Committee meeting.

4.4 TAT compliance metrics are monitored by the Compliance function and reported to senior management monthly. Individual and team-level TAT performance data is used in performance reviews.
