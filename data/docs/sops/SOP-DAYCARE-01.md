## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP governs the assessment and processing of claims for Day Care Procedures under Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. health insurance policies. Under the applicable Policy Wording (Clause 2.3), a Day Care Procedure is one listed in the Company's Day Care Procedure list that, owing to advances in medical technology, can be completed within less than 24 hours without the need for a full in-patient admission. The 24-hour minimum admission requirement applicable to in-patient hospitalisation does not apply to Day Care Procedures. This SOP applies to all Day Care claims whether submitted as cashless or as reimbursement, and across Health Gold and Health Silver policies.

## Section 2 Identifying Day Care Procedures

2.1 The first step in assessing any Day Care claim is to verify that the procedure performed appears on the Company's current Day Care Procedure list.

2.2 The Day Care Procedure list is maintained by the Company's medical and clinical team and is accessible in the claims management system. The adjuster must consult the version of the list that was applicable on the date of the procedure, not the date the claim was submitted.

2.3 A procedure is not eligible under the Day Care provision merely because it was completed in less than 24 hours. The procedure must appear on the Company's list. If the procedure is not on the list and the admission lasted less than 24 hours, the adjuster must assess whether any other provision of the applicable Policy Wording applies and, where none applies, must follow the process in SOP-REJCOMM-01.

2.4 Where the treating physician has described the procedure using a non-standard name or a local alternative term, the adjuster must determine whether the described procedure maps to a listed Day Care Procedure. If the mapping is uncertain, the adjuster must seek guidance from the Company's medical panel before finalising the assessment.

## Section 3 Documents and Checks

3.1 In addition to the mandatory documents set out in SOP-DOCS-01, Day Care claims must be supported by: operation notes or procedure notes confirming the procedure performed and the duration; the admission and discharge record showing the admission and discharge times on the same date; and, for procedures using implants or medical devices, the original invoice and the device identification sticker.

3.2 The adjuster must confirm that the procedure was performed at a Hospital or at a day care centre equipped with an appropriate procedure room, under the supervision of a qualified registered medical practitioner.

3.3 Any applicable waiting period under the applicable Policy Wording version must be checked before financial assessment begins. A Day Care Procedure claim is subject to the same waiting period provisions as an equivalent in-patient claim for the same condition. For specific waiting periods, refer to the applicable Policy Wording rather than to this SOP.

3.4 Sub-limits applicable to the procedure must be identified and applied. For specific sub-limit amounts, refer to Clause 3.2 of the applicable Policy Wording.

## Section 4 Common Issues

4.1 Procedure not on the Day Care list: the most common reason for a Day Care claim being assessed as not payable is that the procedure performed is not listed. Before concluding the assessment, the adjuster must confirm that the most current version of the Day Care list has been consulted and that the procedure has been correctly identified.

4.2 Admission duration exceeds 24 hours: if the Insured Person was admitted for 24 hours or more, the claim qualifies as an in-patient claim rather than a Day Care claim and must be processed under SOP-REIMB-01 or SOP-CASHLESS-01 as appropriate.

4.3 Inadequate procedure notes: if the hospital's clinical notes do not confirm the specific procedure performed, the adjuster must raise a written deficiency notice requesting the specific documentation. Assessment must not proceed until the notes are received.

4.4 Waiting period not completed: where a Day Care Procedure is subject to a waiting period under the Policy Wording, the adjuster must calculate the eligible date using the policy start date and any applicable portability credit before determining whether the claim proceeds to financial assessment.
