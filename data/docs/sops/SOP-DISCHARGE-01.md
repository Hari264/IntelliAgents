## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP governs the review of discharge summaries submitted in support of health insurance claims under policies issued by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. The discharge summary is a critical clinical document confirming the diagnosis, the period of admission, the treatment provided and the treating physician's findings at discharge. An incomplete or inconsistent discharge summary can delay or undermine the claim assessment. This SOP applies to all claims requiring a discharge summary and must be followed by all claims adjusters regardless of the type of claim or the hospital involved.

## Section 2 Required Content

2.1 A discharge summary submitted with a health insurance claim must contain at minimum: (a) the full name of the patient, their date of birth or age, and the admission and discharge dates; (b) the principal diagnosis with a disease classification code where the hospital provides one; (c) the name and registration number of the treating physician or surgeon; (d) a description of the clinical history that led to the admission; (e) the treatment or surgical procedure performed, including the type of anaesthesia used for surgical cases; (f) the condition of the patient at the time of discharge; and (g) any prescribed follow-up treatment, medication or review appointments.

2.2 For surgical claims, the discharge summary must also confirm the name of the operating surgeon, the type of procedure performed and that the procedure was carried out under the stated form of anaesthesia.

2.3 For Day Care Procedure claims, the discharge summary or day care procedure note must confirm that the admission and discharge occurred on the same day, and must clearly name the procedure performed.

## Section 3 Consistency Checks

3.1 On receipt of the discharge summary, the adjuster must check for internal consistency and consistency with the other documents in the claim file: (a) confirm that the patient name, date of birth and admission dates in the summary match those on the hospital bill and the claim form; (b) confirm that the diagnosis in the summary is consistent with the diagnostic test reports submitted; (c) confirm that the treatment or procedure described in the summary is consistent with the operation notes and any implant invoice; and (d) confirm that the discharge date matches the billing period in the final itemised bill.

3.2 Where the principal diagnosis in the discharge summary differs from the diagnosis stated on the claim form, the adjuster must note the discrepancy, seek clarification from the treating physician if needed, and document the finding before finalising the assessment.

3.3 The adjuster must check that the number of in-patient days stated in the discharge summary is consistent with the room charges in the itemised bill. Any day-count discrepancy must be queried in accordance with SOP-BILLREV-01.

## Section 4 Incomplete Summaries

4.1 Where the discharge summary is missing one or more of the elements listed in Section 2, the adjuster must raise a written deficiency notice requesting the specific missing information. The claim assessment must not proceed until a complete summary or an acceptable supplementary document is received.

4.2 Where the hospital provides a supplementary document addressing the missing elements — such as a physician's clarification letter or an addendum to the discharge summary — the adjuster must verify that the supplementary document is consistent with the original summary before accepting it as satisfying the requirement.

4.3 Where the treating physician is unable to provide a full discharge summary owing to the nature of the admission — for example, in a Domiciliary Hospitalisation case — the adjuster may accept a detailed physician's letter that covers the same elements listed in Section 2, in accordance with SOP-DOMICIL-01.

4.4 All deficiency notices raised and all responses received must be recorded in the claims management system under the claim reference, together with the date of each communication and the name of the staff member who sent or received it.
