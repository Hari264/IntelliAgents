## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP governs the process for empanelling hospitals to the Company's Network Hospital list and for conducting periodic reviews of network membership. Under the applicable Policy Wording (Clause 7.1), cashless treatment is available only at Network Hospitals — those with a valid cashless arrangement with the Company or its designated third-party administrator. The Provider Management team is responsible for maintaining the network, with support from the claims function in monitoring hospital performance. This SOP is to be read alongside SOP-CASHLESS-01 for the cashless claims process and CIR-04 for the regulator's standards on network hospital quality.

The network is an operational asset. Accurate and timely updates to the network list directly affect the cashless experience of Insured Persons and the integrity of the pre-authorisation process. All changes to the network list must be reflected in the claims management system without delay.

## Section 2 Empanelment Criteria

2.1 To be considered for empanelment, a hospital must satisfy the definition of a Hospital as set out in Clause 1.1.1 of the applicable Policy Wording, including requirements regarding in-patient bed capacity, qualified medical and nursing staff, operation theatre and daily patient records.

2.2 In addition to satisfying the Hospital definition, the applying facility must demonstrate: (a) adequate infrastructure for cashless claim processing, including the ability to submit claims electronically; (b) a willingness to operate under agreed tariff rates; and (c) adequate staffing of the billing and medical records departments to support timely and complete claim submissions.

2.3 The hospital must execute a provider agreement with the Company setting out tariff rates, billing standards, claim processing obligations, the Company's right to conduct billing audits, and the grounds for removal from the network.

2.4 The Company reserves the right to conduct a site visit before empanelment to verify the facilities and documentation processes. The findings of any site visit are recorded in the Provider Management file for that hospital.

## Section 3 Periodic Review

3.1 The Provider Management team conducts periodic reviews of all Network Hospitals at frequencies determined by the team and approved by the Head of Provider Management. High-volume and higher-risk providers are reviewed more frequently than others.

3.2 Each review assesses: (a) billing accuracy and adherence to agreed tariff rates; (b) the quality and completeness of claim documentation submitted by the hospital; (c) response time to pre-authorisation requests and enhancement requests; (d) complaints received from Insured Persons or service staff about the hospital; and (e) any findings from site visits or billing audits conducted since the previous review.

3.3 Hospitals whose performance consistently meets required standards are retained without change. Hospitals with performance below the required standard are placed on a formal review notice period and monitored at closer intervals.

3.4 Review findings and decisions are documented in the Provider Management file for each hospital and are accessible to the claims leadership as required.

## Section 4 Removal and Communication

4.1 A hospital may be removed from the network where: (a) the provider agreement is terminated by either party; (b) the hospital no longer satisfies the empanelment criteria; (c) persistent billing or documentation issues are not remedied following a formal review notice; or (d) the hospital's registration is suspended or cancelled by a competent authority.

4.2 The Provider Management team must update the network list in the claims management system on the same day a hospital is removed.

4.3 For planned removals, the Company will endeavour to give the hospital advance written notice. For immediate removal arising from a serious concern, notice may be given simultaneously with the update to the network list.

4.4 Policyholders are informed of significant network changes through the Company's website and customer communication channels. Claims that were pre-authorised before the removal date must be honoured in accordance with SOP-CASHLESS-01 and the terms of the existing pre-authorisation.
