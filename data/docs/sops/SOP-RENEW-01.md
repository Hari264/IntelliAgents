## Section 1 Purpose

This SOP governs the process for handling annual renewals and grace period situations under health insurance policies issued by Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. Under the applicable Policy Wording (Clause 9.1), the policy is renewable annually and the Policyholder bears primary responsibility for ensuring timely renewal. This SOP applies to all Health Gold and Health Silver individual and family floater policies and is to be followed by the policy administration and service teams. It should be read alongside CIR-06 for the NIR's requirements regarding renewal notices.

## Section 2 Renewal Notices

2.1 The Company will issue a renewal notice to the Policyholder at least 30 days before the policy expiry date, as required by Clause 9.1.1 of the applicable Policy Wording.

2.2 The renewal notice must include: the renewal premium amount due; the policy expiry date; the grace period available after expiry; any change in premium from the previous year with a brief explanation; and the contact details and payment methods available for making the renewal payment.

2.3 Where the Policyholder wishes to change the sum insured option, add or remove a rider, or make any other material change at renewal, the request must be received and processed before the policy expiry date. All changes are subject to underwriting review and may require submission of medical evidence in accordance with the applicable underwriting guidelines.

2.4 The service team must follow up with the Policyholder if the renewal premium has not been received in a reasonable period following issuance of the renewal notice. The follow-up may be by telephone, email or written reminder, and must be recorded in the policy management system.

## Section 3 Grace Period Handling

3.1 A grace period of 30 days is available after the policy expiry date for payment of the renewal premium, as stated in Clause 9.1.2 of the applicable Policy Wording.

3.2 During the 30-day grace period, the policy continues in force for administrative continuity purposes, but no cover is available for new losses occurring during the grace period. This must be communicated clearly and proactively to any Policyholder who contacts the service team during the grace period with a claim or treatment enquiry.

3.3 If the renewal premium is received within the grace period, the policy is renewed with Continuous Cover preserved. The effective renewal date is the day after the original expiry date, ensuring there is no break in cover for waiting period purposes.

3.4 Service staff must not create a new policy number for a grace period renewal. The original policy number continues. Any error in this process must be corrected by the supervisor before the renewal record is finalised in the policy management system.

## Section 4 Lapsed Policies

4.1 If the renewal premium is not received within the 30-day grace period, the policy lapses. Following lapse, no cover is available, and the Insured Person may not submit claims for events occurring after the expiry date.

4.2 A lapsed policy may be reinstated at the Company's discretion. The Policyholder must pay the outstanding premium and submit a satisfactory declaration of continued good health. The underwriting team must review the declaration before reinstatement is confirmed.

4.3 On reinstatement, all waiting periods recommence from the date of reinstatement as a fresh commencement. Any portability credit that was previously applied does not carry forward to a reinstated policy automatically; the Policyholder must reapply and satisfy the portability criteria in SOP-PORT-01 if applicable.

4.4 Service staff must clearly explain to the Policyholder that reinstatement is not guaranteed, that the gap in cover cannot be bridged retroactively, and that claims for events during the lapse period cannot be entertained. All lapse and reinstatement decisions must be documented in the policy management system.
