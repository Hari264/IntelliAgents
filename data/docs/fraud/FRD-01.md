## Section 1 Purpose

This document forms part of the Fraud Playbook, a series of Restricted documents for the exclusive use of claims adjusters and Special Investigations Unit (SIU) staff. It defines the mandatory thresholds at which a health insurance claim must be referred to the SIU and the red-flag scoring bands used to route claims for the appropriate level of review.

The Fraud Playbook and all information contained in it are strictly confidential. Nothing in this document may be communicated to Insured Persons, Policyholders, hospitals, brokers or any other external party. When communicating with a claimant about a claim that is under enhanced review, staff must use only the CL-601 script in SCR-GEN-01.

Staff accessing this document must have been granted access through the SIU module of the claims management system. Unauthorised access or disclosure is subject to disciplinary action.

## Section 2 Mandatory Referral Thresholds

The following thresholds require a mandatory SIU referral, regardless of the red-flag score. When any of these thresholds is met, the adjuster must submit a referral through the SIU module immediately, using the process described in SOP-FRAUDREF-01.

2.1 Threshold FT-01 — High-value early claim: the claimed amount exceeds ₹5,00,000 AND the date of loss falls within 90 days of the policy start date. Both conditions must be present simultaneously for this threshold to apply. A large claim amount alone does not trigger FT-01; the 90-day condition must also be met.

2.2 Threshold FT-02 — Claim frequency: the same Insured Person has submitted 3 or more claims within any rolling 12-month period ending on the current date of loss. The adjuster must query the claims management system for all prior claims by the Insured Person to establish the 12-month claim count before assessing this threshold.

2.3 Threshold FT-03 — Billing anomaly: the billed amount for a procedure exceeds 150% of the Company's applicable network tariff for that procedure at the time of the claim. The comparison is made against the current network tariff schedule. Where the billed amount exceeds the tariff threshold, a billing audit must be initiated alongside the SIU referral.

2.4 Threshold FT-04 — Watchlisted provider: the treating hospital or medical provider appears on the Company's internal provider watchlist at the date of the cashless pre-authorisation request or the date of reimbursement claim receipt. For cashless claims at a watchlisted provider, a field visit must be conducted and the outcome recorded before cashless approval is issued.

## Section 3 Scoring Bands

In addition to the mandatory referral thresholds in Section 2, the Company uses a red-flag scoring model to assess the overall risk profile of each claim. The scoring model assigns numerical values to individual risk factors identified during claim assessment. The aggregate score determines the processing pathway.

3.1 Scoring band thresholds and processing actions (Threshold FT-05):

Red-flag score 70 and above: the claim is referred to the SIU without delay. Settlement and payment are suspended pending the SIU's assessment and recommendation.

Red-flag score 40 to 69: the claim is referred to a senior adjuster for enhanced desk review. The senior adjuster must document their assessment findings in the SIU module and may escalate to a full SIU referral if the review identifies additional concerns.

Red-flag score below 40: the claim proceeds through normal processing channels. No enhanced review is required solely on the basis of the score.

3.2 The individual risk factors, their point weightings and the scoring tool are maintained by the SIU and are accessed through the claims management system. The specific factor list and weightings are not reproduced in this document.

3.3 A mandatory referral threshold under Section 2 overrides the score and triggers referral regardless of the score band.

## Section 4 Confidentiality

The thresholds, scores and referral criteria in this Fraud Playbook are commercially and operationally sensitive. Disclosure of this information could enable fraudulent actors to design claims that fall below referral thresholds.

4.1 No threshold value, score band, referral reason or investigation detail may be communicated to an Insured Person, Policyholder, hospital, broker or any representative of any of these parties at any point before, during or after an investigation.

4.2 When a claim has been referred and the claimant enquires about the status, the agent must respond using only the CL-601 script from SCR-GEN-01: the customer is informed only that the claim is under additional review. No other explanation is given.

4.3 All SIU referral records, scoring outputs and investigation notes must be stored in the Restricted SIU folder in the claims management system. These records are not accessible to standard claims processing staff and must not be included in any correspondence with the claimant.

4.4 Any suspected breach of confidentiality relating to Fraud Playbook contents must be reported to the Head of SIU and the Compliance function immediately.
