## Section 1 Purpose

This document forms part of the Fraud Playbook series and is Restricted to claims adjusters and SIU staff. It defines the standards for building, maintaining and protecting the documentation and evidence gathered during an enhanced review or SIU investigation. Sound case documentation ensures that decisions are defensible, that evidence is preserved for any subsequent legal or regulatory proceedings, and that the investigation can be reviewed and audited internally. This document supplements FRD-01 (referral thresholds), FRD-02 (billing red flags) and FRD-03 (investigation procedure).

## Section 2 Building the Case File

2.1 A dedicated case file must be opened in the restricted SIU module of the claims management system at the moment a claim is referred for enhanced review — whether because a threshold defined in FRD-01 has been met, a red-flag score falls in an enhanced review band, a watchlist provider is involved, or a concern is referred under SOP-FRAUDREF-01.

2.2 The case file must contain, as a minimum: (a) the referral notice, stating the basis for the referral with the specific threshold or concern; (b) a copy of all claim documents received from the Policyholder, hospital and treating physician; (c) the claims management system record for the original claim, including pre-authorisation records if applicable; (d) the red-flag scoring output from the claims management system at the time of referral; and (e) a log of all contacts made during the investigation, in accordance with FRD-04 Section 4.

2.3 As the investigation progresses, additional documents obtained from the hospital, the Insurance Information Bureau or through field visits must be added to the case file promptly, with the date and source of each document noted.

2.4 Each document added to the case file must be labelled with the claim reference, the date of receipt and the source. Unlabelled documents are not to be retained in the case file.

## Section 3 Preserving Evidence

3.1 All original documents obtained during the investigation must be retained in their original form. Physical originals must be held securely by the SIU; digital copies must be stored in the restricted SIU module and must not be altered, compressed or moved to a general claims file.

3.2 Where a document has been obtained from a third party — for example, a hospital record or a copy of an admission register obtained during a field visit — the SIU officer must note: the date and method of collection; the name and designation of the person who provided the document; and whether the document is an original, a certified copy or a photograph.

3.3 Electronic records — including email correspondence, portal submissions and digital images — must be preserved in a format that cannot be retrospectively altered. The SIU module's audit trail capability should be relied upon for this purpose.

3.4 If the SIU officer has reason to believe that any document in the standard claim file may have been altered, the unaltered version must be preserved separately in the case file and the fact of the potential alteration must be noted, without any communication to the Insured Person or hospital as required by FRD-04.

## Section 4 Access Control

4.1 The SIU case file is Restricted. Access is limited to: the SIU officer assigned to the case; the Head of SIU; the Head of Claims for the purpose of management oversight; the Legal function where legal advice is sought; and the Compliance function for audit purposes.

4.2 Standard claims processing staff — including adjusters who are not assigned to the specific case — must not have access to the SIU case file. The adjuster who originally processed the claim retains access to the standard claim file but must not have access to the SIU case file unless specifically assigned to the investigation.

4.3 Access to the SIU module requires a separate login and authorisation. Any request for access by a person not in the categories listed in Clause 4.1 must be approved in writing by the Head of SIU.

4.4 On conclusion of the investigation and closure of the case, the case file must be archived in the restricted SIU records repository and retained in accordance with the Company's document retention policy. The retention period for SIU case files must be no shorter than that required for standard claim files, given the possibility of future legal or regulatory proceedings.
