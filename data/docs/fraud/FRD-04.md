## Section 1 Purpose

This document forms part of the Fraud Playbook series and is Restricted to claims adjusters and SIU staff. It defines the communication restrictions that apply to all customer-facing interactions while a claim is under enhanced review or has been referred to the SIU. It supplements FRD-01, FRD-02 and FRD-03, which define the referral thresholds, billing red flags and investigation procedure respectively. The core rule governing all communications under this Playbook is that the customer must never be told that a fraud review is in progress.

This restriction applies from the moment a claim is flagged for enhanced review — whether because a mandatory referral threshold in FRD-01 has been met, the red-flag score falls in an enhanced review band, a watchlist provider is involved, or the adjuster raises a concern under SOP-FRAUDREF-01.

## Section 2 What Must Not Be Said

2.1 Staff must never use any of the following words or concepts in any communication with the Insured Person, Policyholder, hospital, broker or any representative during or after an enhanced review: "fraud", "investigation", "SIU", "Special Investigations Unit", "suspicious", "irregularity", "threshold", "referral for review", "red flag" or any synonym or equivalent that would indicate to the recipient that the claim is under a fraud-related assessment.

2.2 Staff must not reveal that the claim has been referred to any specialist team, that documents or records are being checked for authenticity, or that the hospital or any medical professional associated with the claim is being reviewed.

2.3 Staff must not tell the customer the specific reason for any delay that is attributable to the enhanced review. Where the customer asks why the claim is taking longer than expected, staff must use only the approved holding statement in Section 3.

2.4 These restrictions apply regardless of whether the customer is polite, persistent, upset or legally represented. The restriction is not a matter of discretion; it is a mandatory rule. Any breach must be reported to the Head of SIU immediately.

## Section 3 Approved Holding Statements

3.1 The only approved statement for a claim under enhanced review or referred to the SIU is: "Your claim is currently under additional review. Our team will be in touch with you as soon as we have an update. If you have any questions, please call our helpline and quote your claim reference {claim_id}." This statement is reproduced in SCR-GEN-01 and must be used without modification.

3.2 For cashless claims where the treating hospital contacts the Company to query a delayed discharge authorisation, the approved response is: "We are completing our assessment. We will issue our decision as soon as possible." The hospital must not be told the reason for the delay.

3.3 If the customer or hospital escalates the matter and demands a written explanation for the delay, the written response must state only: "Your claim is under review and we will communicate our decision in writing as soon as the assessment is complete." No further detail is to be provided.

3.4 All uses of the approved holding statement must be recorded in the claims management system under the original claim reference, in accordance with Section 4.

## Section 4 Recording Contact

4.1 All customer contacts — inbound and outbound, by telephone, email or any written medium — that occur while a claim is under enhanced review or referred to the SIU must be recorded in the restricted SIU module of the claims management system, not in the standard customer contact log.

4.2 The SIU module record must include the date and time of the contact, the method of contact, the name of the staff member involved, what the customer asked or said, and exactly what holding statement was used in response.

4.3 Standard customer contact records that are accessible to non-fraud staff must contain only: the date and time; the claim reference; and a note that the claim is under additional review. The substance of the conversation must not be visible outside the restricted SIU module.

4.4 Any breach of the communication restrictions in Section 2 — including any inadvertent disclosure — must be reported to the Head of SIU and the Compliance function immediately, with a full account of what was said, to whom, and in what context.
