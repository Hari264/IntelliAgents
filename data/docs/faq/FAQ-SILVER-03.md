## Section 1 How to Submit a Claim Under Health Silver

Q: How do I notify the Company when I am admitted to hospital?

A: The notification method depends on whether the admission is planned or an emergency (Clause 6.1).

For planned hospitalisation: notify the Company or its designated third-party administrator at least 48 hours before the date of admission. This allows time to process a cashless pre-authorisation request if required.

For emergency hospitalisation: notify within 24 hours of admission. If the Insured Person cannot notify due to the severity of the condition, a family member or the treating hospital may do so on their behalf.

Notification can be made through the helpline, email, online portal or other channel specified in the Policy Schedule. A claim reference number will be issued on receipt of notification and must be quoted in all subsequent correspondence.

Q: What documents must I submit for a reimbursement claim?

A: All reimbursement claim documents must be submitted within 30 days of the date of discharge (Clause 6.2.1). The mandatory documents are (Clause 6.2.2): (a) completed and signed claim form; (b) original discharge summary from the Hospital; (c) original itemised final bill; (d) all original bills, receipts and payment proofs; (e) all diagnostic test reports and prescriptions relating to the hospitalisation; (f) a copy of the Insured Person's valid photo identity document; and (g) a cancelled cheque or bank account details for electronic fund transfer. For surgical claims, the post-operative notes and the implant invoice with sticker must also be included (Clause 6.2.3).

Q: What happens if I cannot submit documents within 30 days?

A: The Company may condone a reasonable delay if the Insured Person provides a satisfactory written explanation for the delay. Contact the Company promptly if you anticipate a late submission.

## Section 2 Room Rent Limit Under Health Silver

Q: Is there a limit on room rent under Health Silver?

A: Yes. Under Health Silver, the eligible room rent for a standard in-patient room (single private, semi-private or general ward) is limited to 1% of the sum insured per day (Clause 8.1.1). For an Insured Person with a sum insured of ₹5,00,000, the eligible room rent is ₹5,000 per day.

Q: Is there a room rent limit for ICU admissions?

A: No. ICU and ICCU charges are payable at actuals with no monetary cap per day, provided the ICU admission is Medically Necessary (Clause 8.1.2).

Q: What happens if I choose a room that costs more than the eligible limit?

A: If the Insured Person occupies a room category whose cost exceeds the eligible room rent limit, a proportionate deduction is applied to the associated medical expenses of the entire hospitalisation (Clause 8.2).

The proportion of deduction equals the ratio of the eligible room rent to the actual room rent charged. For example, for a sum insured of ₹5,00,000, the eligible room rent is ₹5,000 per day. If the actual room rent charged is ₹10,000 per day, the proportion is 50% and all associated medical expenses — such as surgeon fees, consultant charges, nursing charges, operation theatre charges and diagnostic charges — will be reduced by 50% (Clause 8.2.1).

Pharmacy charges, implant costs and consumables are not subject to proportionate deduction (Clause 8.2.3).

Q: Can the proportionate deduction be avoided?

A: The proportionate deduction can be waived only if the Insured Person holds the Room Rent Waiver Rider and it is endorsed on the Policy Schedule (Clause 8.2.4).

## Section 3 What Sub-limits Apply Under Health Silver

Q: Are there monetary caps on specific procedures under Health Silver?

A: Yes. The following sub-limits apply under Health Silver, regardless of the actual expenses incurred (Clause 3.2):

Cataract surgery: ₹25,000 per eye per Policy Year. If both eyes are treated in the same year, the sub-limit of ₹25,000 applies separately to each eye (Clause 3.2.1).

Joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty): ₹1,50,000 per joint per event. If more than one joint is replaced in a single hospitalisation, the sub-limit applies separately to each joint (Clause 3.2.2).

Road ambulance: ₹2,000 per hospitalisation event (Clause 3.2.3).

Q: What if the actual procedure cost exceeds the sub-limit?

A: If the actual expense exceeds the applicable sub-limit, the Company will pay up to the sub-limit. The balance above the sub-limit is not payable from any other benefit or sum insured under the Policy (Clause 3.2.4).

Q: Do sub-limits reduce the overall sum insured?

A: Yes. Amounts paid under a sub-limit are deducted from the available sum insured balance for that Policy Year (Clause 3.1.5).

## Section 4 Can I Use the Cashless Facility Under Health Silver

Q: Can I use a cashless facility under Health Silver?

A: Yes, the cashless facility is available at Network Hospitals — hospitals that have a valid cashless arrangement with the Company or its designated third-party administrator (Clause 7.1.1). Before admission, the Insured Person should confirm that the hospital is on the Company's current network list through the helpline or the Company's website.

Q: What is the process for cashless treatment?

A: For planned admissions, the pre-authorisation request must be submitted at least 48 hours before the intended date of admission. For emergency admissions, it must be submitted within 24 hours of admission (Clauses 7.1.2 and 7.1.3). Pre-authorisation approval does not guarantee final claim settlement; settlement remains subject to submission of complete documents and policy verification (Clause 7.1.4).

Q: What if the hospital is not on the network?

A: Cashless treatment is not available at non-network hospitals. All expenses incurred at a non-network hospital must be submitted as a reimbursement claim under Clause 7.2. The reimbursement amount will be the lesser of the actual expenses or the amount that would have been payable at a Network Hospital, subject to all applicable limits and deductions.

Q: Within what time must a reimbursement claim be submitted?

A: All reimbursement documents must be submitted within 30 days of the date of discharge (Clause 7.2.1). If original bills have been lost, the Insured Person must submit a written explanation together with a self-declaration and, where available, certified duplicate bills from the treating facility (Clause 7.2.4).
