## Section 1 What Documents Are Needed for a Reimbursement Claim

Q: What documents must I submit for a reimbursement claim under Health Gold?

A: Under Clause 6.2 of the applicable Policy Wording, the following are mandatory for all reimbursement claims: (a) a completed and signed claim form issued by the Company; (b) the original discharge summary or discharge certificate from the Hospital; (c) the original itemised final bill from the Hospital; (d) all original bills, receipts and payment proofs for all expenses claimed; (e) all original diagnostic test reports and prescriptions relating to the hospitalisation; (f) a copy of the Insured Person's valid photo identity document; and (g) a cancelled cheque or bank account details for electronic fund transfer.

Q: Are there additional documents for surgical claims?

A: Yes. For surgical claims, the post-operative notes and the original implant invoice with the implant sticker must also be submitted. The implant sticker is essential for verifying the device used. Without it, the implant cost cannot be settled.

Q: By when must I submit the documents?

A: All reimbursement claim documents must be submitted within 30 days of the date of discharge from the Hospital, as required by Clause 6.2.1 of the applicable Policy Wording. If you are unable to submit within this period for a genuine reason, contact the Company and explain the delay in writing. The Company may consider condoning the delay at its discretion.

## Section 2 What Documents Are Needed for a Cashless Claim

Q: What documents does the hospital need to submit for cashless authorisation?

A: For a cashless claim at a Network Hospital, the hospital typically submits the pre-authorisation request on the Insured Person's behalf. The request must include the provisional diagnosis, the proposed treatment or procedure, the estimated length of stay and the estimated cost. For planned admissions, this must be done at least 48 hours before the admission date. For emergency admissions, the request must be submitted within 24 hours of admission.

Q: Are original bills still required after a cashless settlement?

A: Yes. Even for cashless claims, the Hospital must provide the final itemised bill at the time of discharge. The Company needs the final bill to process the discharge authorisation and to confirm the actual treatment rendered against what was pre-authorised.

Q: What if there is a discrepancy between the pre-authorised amount and the final bill?

A: Where the final bill is higher than the pre-authorised amount, the hospital may submit an enhancement request. The process for enhancement requests is set out in SOP-PREAUTH-02. If any amount is not covered under the cashless facility, the Insured Person may pay that amount and claim it as reimbursement under Clause 7.2.

## Section 3 What Should I Do If I Cannot Provide a Document

Q: What if I have lost the original bills?

A: Where original documents have been lost, submit a written explanation together with a self-declaration. Where available, provide certified duplicate bills from the treating facility. The adjuster will assess whether the alternative evidence is sufficient to support the claim. Original documents are preferred and their absence may affect the ability to reimburse certain line items.

Q: What if the hospital cannot provide the discharge summary promptly?

A: Notify the Company and explain the reason for the delay. The assessment clock (which begins on receipt of the last necessary document) will not start until the discharge summary is received. Use this time to submit all other mandatory documents to avoid further delay once the summary is available.

Q: Can I submit photocopies instead of originals?

A: Original documents are required for all reimbursement claims. Photocopies alone are generally not accepted. Exceptions apply in limited circumstances — for example, where the original has been lost and an affidavit and certified copy are provided. Contact the Company to discuss your specific situation before submitting.

## Section 4 How Do I Submit Documents to the Company

Q: What are the available channels for submitting claim documents?

A: Claim documents may be submitted by: physical post to the Company's claims address; email to the Company's claims email address (original documents to follow by post where required); upload through the Company's online customer portal; or in person at a Company service centre. The preferred channel and submission details are stated in the Policy Schedule and in communications from the Company. The claim reference number must be quoted on all submissions.

Q: Can a family member submit documents on behalf of the Insured Person?

A: Yes. A family member or an authorised representative may submit documents on behalf of the Insured Person. If the settlement payment is to be made to a person other than the Policyholder, a written authorisation from the Policyholder is required.

Q: How will I know the documents have been received?

A: The Company will acknowledge receipt of documents and issue a deficiency notice for any missing items in accordance with CIR-05. If you have submitted documents and not received an acknowledgement within a reasonable period, contact the claims helpline and quote your claim reference number.
