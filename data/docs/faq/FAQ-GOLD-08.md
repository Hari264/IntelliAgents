## Section 1 How Do I Check the Status of My Claim

Q: How can I find out what is happening with my claim?

A: You may check the status of your claim at any time by calling the Company's customer helpline and quoting your claim reference number. You may also check through the Company's online customer portal, if you are registered. When you call, the agent will tell you whether your claim is pending documents, under assessment, settled or closed.

Q: What claim statuses may I be given?

A: Common claim statuses include: documents pending (some required documents have not yet been received); under assessment (all documents have been received and the team is reviewing the claim); settled (a payment has been made); and additional review (the claim requires further assessment before a decision is issued — no further detail is provided at this stage). If the claim is at the additional review stage, the agent will inform you that the team will be in touch once the review is complete.

Q: What should I do if I have not heard anything about my claim?

A: Under NIR guidelines, the Company must settle claims within 30 days of receiving the last necessary document. Where an investigation is required, settlement must be completed within 45 days. If you believe these timelines have passed without a decision, contact the helpline to enquire. If you remain unsatisfied, you may raise a formal grievance as described in Section 4 below.

## Section 2 How Long Does the Company Take to Settle a Claim

Q: What are the settlement timelines for a reimbursement claim?

A: The Company must settle a reimbursement claim within 30 days of receiving the last necessary document, in accordance with NIR guidelines (CIR-01). Where the Company requires an investigation before a decision can be made, the settlement must be completed within 45 days of receiving the last necessary document. If the Company fails to meet these timelines, it is liable to pay interest on the amount payable for the period of delay, as prescribed by the NIR. For the applicable rate, refer to CIR-01.

Q: What is the timeline for a cashless pre-authorisation decision?

A: The Company must issue a pre-authorisation decision within 1 hour of receiving a complete pre-authorisation request from the Network Hospital. For final discharge authorisation, the decision must be issued within 3 hours of receiving the hospital's final bill.

Q: What is the timeline for communicating a claim rejection?

A: If a claim is to be rejected, the Company must communicate the written rejection reason to the Policyholder within 15 days of receiving the last necessary document, citing the specific policy clause. The Policyholder then has the right to raise a grievance if they disagree.

## Section 3 What Should I Do If My Claim Is Not Fully Approved

Q: What if only part of my claim has been paid?

A: A partial settlement means that some expenses were approved and others were not. The settlement letter will explain each amount that was not approved, with the specific policy clause applied. If you disagree with any part of the settlement, you may raise a formal grievance with the Grievance Redressal Officer.

Q: What if my claim is fully rejected?

A: You will receive a written rejection letter explaining the reason for the rejection and the specific policy clause. You have the right to challenge this by raising a grievance. The process is described in Section 4.

Q: Can I submit additional evidence to support a rejected claim?

A: Yes. If you have new evidence that was not available at the time of the original assessment, you may contact the Company and request that the case be reviewed. Refer to SOP-REOPEN-01 for the grounds on which a closed claim may be reassessed.

## Section 4 How Do I Register and Escalate a Grievance

Q: How do I register a formal grievance?

A: You may register a grievance by writing to the Company's Grievance Redressal Officer at the address or email stated in the Policy Schedule. You may also call the customer helpline and use the script in SCR-GRIEV-01, or visit a service centre. Your grievance will be acknowledged within 3 working days of receipt. The Company will resolve the grievance within 14 days of receipt and communicate the outcome in writing.

Q: What if I am not satisfied with the Company's grievance response?

A: If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided by the Company, or if the grievance is not resolved within 14 days, you have the right to escalate to the Insurance Ombudsman having jurisdiction over your place of residence, in accordance with Clause 10.1 of the applicable Policy Wording and CIR-03.

Q: Is there a fee for raising a grievance?

A: No. The Company does not charge any fee for processing a grievance.
