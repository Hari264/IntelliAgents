## Section 1 What Is the Initial Waiting Period Under Health Silver

Q: Is there an initial waiting period when I first buy Health Silver?

A: Yes. Under Health Silver, there is an initial waiting period of 1 month from the policy start date (Clause 4.1.1). During this 1-month period, claims for any illness or disease will not be entertained, regardless of whether the Insured Person was aware of the condition before buying the Policy.

Q: Does the initial waiting period apply to accidents?

A: No. Claims arising directly from an accidental bodily injury are covered from the first day of the Policy Period and are not subject to the 1-month initial waiting period (Clause 4.1.2). To qualify for this exception, the injury must have been caused by an unforeseen, sudden and external event.

Q: How is the date of loss determined for the initial waiting period?

A: The relevant date is when the Insured Person first sought or received medical attention for the condition that led to hospitalisation, not necessarily the date of admission (Clause 4.1.3). If medical records show that the condition was first treated during the 1-month initial period, the claim will not be payable.

Q: Does portability credit reduce the initial waiting period?

A: The initial waiting period is a fresh period that applies from the commencement of each new Policy. Portability credit under Clause 9.2 applies to the specific waiting periods in Clause 4.2 and the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period in Clause 4.3, as described in Section 4 of this document.

## Section 2 Which Specific Conditions Have Waiting Periods Under Health Silver

Q: Are there waiting periods for specific medical conditions or procedures?

A: Yes. Under Health Silver, the following specific waiting periods apply from the policy start date (Clause 4.2):

Joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty): 36 months of Continuous Cover must have elapsed before a joint replacement claim is payable (Clause 4.2.1). If the date of loss falls before 36 months from the policy start date, the claim will not be entertained.

Cataract surgery: 24 months of Continuous Cover must have elapsed (Clause 4.2.2).

Hernia surgery: 24 months of Continuous Cover must have elapsed (Clause 4.2.3).

Bariatric surgery: 36 months of Continuous Cover must have elapsed (Clause 4.2.4).

Q: How do I calculate when I become eligible for joint replacement cover under Health Silver?

A: Add 36 months to the policy start date. For example, if the policy start date is 1 February 2025, the eligible date for joint replacement surgery is 1 February 2028.

Q: Are these specific waiting periods in addition to the initial waiting period?

A: Yes. The specific waiting periods in Clause 4.2 apply in addition to the 1-month initial waiting period and the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period. They are not alternatives to each other (Clause 4.2.6).

## Section 3 What Is the Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period Under Health Silver

Q: What counts as a Pre-Existing Disease under Health Silver?

A: Under Health Silver, a Pre-Existing Disease is any condition, ailment, injury or disease for which the Insured Person received a medical diagnosis or treatment at any time during the 48-month period immediately before the policy start date (Clause 1.2.1). It does not matter whether the Insured Person was aware of the condition at the time the Policy was taken.

Q: How long is the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period under Health Silver?

A: The Pre-Existing Disease waiting period under Health Silver is 48 months of Continuous Cover from the policy start date (Clause 4.3.1). No claim relating to a Pre-Existing Disease or its complications will be payable until 48 months of Continuous Cover have been completed.

Q: What if my condition is related to a Pre-Existing Disease but the immediate cause is a new illness?

A: If the treating physician determines that the primary cause of the claimed condition is a Pre-Existing Disease, the claim will be assessed under Clause 4.3, even if the immediate precipitating event appears to be a new illness or complication (Clause 4.3.3).

Q: Must I declare Pre-Existing Diseases when buying Health Silver?

A: Yes. All Pre-Existing Diseases must be declared on the proposal form with the diagnosis date and details of treatment received. Non-disclosure of a Pre-Existing Disease may result in referral to underwriting and may affect the validity of the Policy.

## Section 4 How Does Portability Apply to Waiting Periods Under Health Silver

Q: If I transfer from another insurer to Health Silver, do my waiting periods start again?

A: Not necessarily. If you have maintained Continuous Cover under a prior registered health insurance policy, you may apply for portability credit at the time of transfer (Clause 9.2.1). The specific waiting periods under Clause 4.2 and the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period under Clause 4.3 will be reduced by the number of months already completed under the prior policy, subject to supporting documentation.

Q: When must I apply for portability?

A: You must submit a portability application to the Company at least 45 days before the renewal date of your prior policy (Clause 9.2.2). Applications submitted less than 45 days before the prior policy's renewal date may be declined.

Q: What documents are needed to claim portability credit?

A: The Company will calculate portability credit based on the prior insurer's renewal certificate, policy documents and information available through the Insurance Information Bureau. The Company may also verify details directly with the prior insurer (Clause 9.2.3).

Q: What happens if my prior cover lapsed before I transferred to Health Silver?

A: If there is a break in Continuous Cover before transfer, the full waiting periods under Clauses 4.2 and 4.3 apply afresh from the commencement of the Health Silver Policy. No portability credit is available for periods after a lapse (Clause 9.2.5).
