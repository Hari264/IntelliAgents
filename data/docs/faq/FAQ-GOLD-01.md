## Section 1 Who Can Buy Health Gold

Q: Who is eligible to purchase Health Gold?

A: Health Gold is available to individuals aged 18 years and above who are residents of India, subject to the Company's underwriting guidelines. Cover is also available on a family floater basis, allowing the Policyholder and eligible family members — including a spouse, dependent children and dependent parents — to be covered under a single policy with a shared sum insured. Child dependants may be included from the age of 91 days, subject to underwriting. Persons with declared Pre-Existing Diseases are not automatically excluded; they are subject to the waiting period under Clause 4.3 of the applicable Policy Wording.

Q: Can I buy Health Gold if I already have another health insurance policy?

A: Yes. Health Gold may be purchased alongside an existing policy. If you have prior continuous cover with another registered insurer, you may apply for portability credit at the time of transfer so that waiting periods already served are credited toward your Health Gold waiting periods, subject to the conditions in Clause 9.2.

## Section 2 What Does Health Gold Cover

Q: What types of hospitalisation are covered under Health Gold?

A: Health Gold covers the following hospitalisation types, subject to all terms, conditions and exclusions of the Policy Wording:

In-patient Hospitalisation (Clause 2.1): admission of at least 24 continuous hours for Medically Necessary Treatment. Covered expenses include room charges, ICU charges, surgeon and anaesthetist fees, nursing, diagnostic tests and medicines during admission.

Pre and Post Hospitalisation (Clause 2.2): medical expenses up to 30 days before admission and up to 60 days after discharge, directly related to the covered condition.

Day Care Procedures (Clause 2.3): procedures on the Company's Day Care list, for which 24-hour admission is not required.

Domiciliary Hospitalisation (Clause 2.4): home-based treatment that continues for more than 3 consecutive days and where admission was medically inadvisable.

Road Ambulance (Clause 2.5): up to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation event.

Q: Are accidental injuries covered from day one?

A: Yes. Claims arising directly from accidental bodily injury are covered from the first day of the Policy Period and are not subject to the initial waiting period.

## Section 3 What Sum Insured Options Are Available Under Health Gold

Q: What sum insured options can I choose?

A: Health Gold is available with the following sum insured options: ₹3,00,000; ₹5,00,000; ₹10,00,000; and ₹25,00,000. The sum insured represents the maximum amount payable across all claims in a Policy Year. It resets at the start of each renewed Policy Year.

Q: Are there monetary sub-limits within the sum insured?

A: Yes. The following sub-limits apply under Health Gold (Clause 3.2): cataract surgery at ₹40,000 per eye per Policy Year; joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty) at ₹3,00,000 per joint per event; and road ambulance at ₹2,000 per hospitalisation. Amounts paid under a sub-limit reduce the available sum insured balance.

Q: Does ICU treatment have a separate limit?

A: ICU and ICCU charges are covered at actuals with no monetary cap per day, provided the admission is Medically Necessary (Clause 8.1).

## Section 4 What Is Not Covered Under Health Gold

Q: What conditions are permanently excluded?

A: Health Gold permanently excludes war and related events, nuclear and chemical contamination, intentional self-inflicted injury and suicide, regardless of how long the policy has been in force (Clause 5.1). These exclusions cannot be removed by any rider or endorsement.

Q: Are there other specific exclusions?

A: Yes. Specific exclusions under Clause 5.2 include substance abuse and related treatment, non-surgical obesity treatment, and rest cures. Experimental or unproven treatments are excluded under Clause 5.3.

Q: What are the waiting periods that apply?

A: An initial waiting period of 1 month applies to all illnesses from the policy start date (Clause 4.1). Specific conditions are subject to additional waiting periods, including cataract surgery (24 months) and bariatric surgery (36 months) (Clause 4.2). Joint replacement surgery and Pre-Existing Diseases are subject to waiting periods as per the policy wording version applicable (Clause 4.2 and Clause 4.3). For maternity and certain dental provisions, refer to the applicable policy wording version (Clauses 2.6 and 5.4).
