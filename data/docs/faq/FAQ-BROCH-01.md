## Section 1 About Health Gold

Health Gold is a comprehensive individual and family health insurance product from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. Designed to protect you and your family against the financial impact of hospitalisation, Health Gold offers wide coverage, a large network of cashless hospitals and straightforward claim processes — so you can focus on recovery, not paperwork.

Whether you need planned surgery, emergency care or treatment for a long-term condition, Health Gold is built to be there when it matters most.

Health Gold is available on an individual basis or as a family floater covering your spouse, dependent children and dependent parents under a single sum insured. With four sum insured options ranging from ₹3,00,000 to ₹25,00,000, you choose the level of protection that fits your family's needs.

Health Gold is renewed annually. Your cover builds over time: the longer you hold the policy with Continuous Cover, the sooner certain waiting periods are completed, and the more conditions become covered.

For full details of all terms, conditions, waiting periods and exclusions, please refer to the Health Gold Policy Wording applicable to your policy. The key highlights in this brochure are a summary only and do not constitute a binding description of cover.

## Section 2 Core Coverage Benefits

Health Gold covers a broad range of hospitalisation scenarios, so you are protected whether the need arises suddenly or is planned.

In-patient Hospitalisation: cover for admissions of at least 24 hours for Medically Necessary Treatment, including room charges, surgeon fees, nursing, operation theatre, diagnostic tests and medicines during the admitted period.

Day Care Procedures: modern medicine means many procedures no longer require overnight stays. Health Gold covers procedures on our Day Care list even when hospitalisation lasts less than 24 hours.

Pre and Post Hospitalisation: expenses in the run-up to admission and during recovery after discharge are part of your cover — up to 30 days before and 60 days after a covered hospitalisation.

Domiciliary Hospitalisation: when the treating doctor certifies that hospitalisation is not possible or medically inadvisable, Health Gold covers home-based treatment that continues for more than 3 consecutive days.

Road Ambulance: up to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation event for a registered road ambulance.

ICU Cover: Intensive Care Unit and ICCU charges are covered at actuals, with no daily monetary cap, subject to medical necessity.

For waiting periods applicable to specific conditions and for maternity terms, refer to the policy wording version applicable to your policy.

## Section 3 Sum Insured Options and Benefits at a Glance

Choose the right level of protection for your family with four sum insured options.

₹3,00,000 — Ideal for individuals seeking essential coverage.
₹5,00,000 — A popular choice for individuals and couples.
₹10,00,000 — Comprehensive cover for families.
₹25,00,000 — Maximum protection for those seeking the highest level of cover.

Sub-limits: within the sum insured, specific procedures are subject to monetary caps. Cataract surgery is covered up to ₹40,000 per eye, and joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty) up to ₹3,00,000 per joint. These sub-limits apply within, not in addition to, the sum insured.

Room Rent: a room rent limit applies per day of hospitalisation, as set out in the Policy Wording applicable to your policy. ICU charges are covered at actuals without a daily cap.

Optional Riders: Health Gold can be enhanced with optional rider benefits including the Waiting Period Waiver Rider, the Room Rent Waiver Rider, the Outpatient Care Rider and the Critical Illness Lump Sum Rider. Rider availability is subject to underwriting and eligibility criteria.

For the specific room rent limit, waiting periods and all other terms and limits applicable to your policy, refer to the Health Gold Policy Wording applicable to your policy version.

## Section 4 How to Make a Claim and Reach Us

Making a claim with Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. is designed to be simple. Here is what you need to know.

Cashless Claims: if you are admitted to one of our Network Hospitals, you may use our cashless facility. For planned admissions, notify us at least 48 hours in advance. For emergency admissions, notify us within 24 hours of admission. The hospital will coordinate the pre-authorisation with us on your behalf.

Reimbursement Claims: if you are treated at a non-network hospital, pay the hospital directly and submit your original bills, discharge summary and other required documents to us within 30 days of discharge. We will assess your claim and pay the eligible amount directly to your bank account.

Grievances: if you are not satisfied with a claim decision, you may raise a grievance with our Grievance Redressal Officer. Details are provided in your Policy Schedule and in our written communications.

Contact Us: our helpline is available to assist with network hospital queries, pre-authorisation requests, claim submissions and general policy enquiries. Contact details are provided in your Policy Schedule.
