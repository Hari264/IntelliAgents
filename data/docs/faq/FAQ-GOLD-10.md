## Section 1 What Are Pre and Post Hospitalisation Expenses

Q: What are pre-hospitalisation expenses?

A: Pre-hospitalisation expenses are medical costs incurred by the Insured Person before being admitted to hospital that are directly related to the condition for which the admission subsequently takes place. Under the applicable Policy Wording (Clause 2.2), these may include outpatient consultations, diagnostic investigations and prescribed medicines obtained in the period before admission.

Q: What are post-hospitalisation expenses?

A: Post-hospitalisation expenses are medical costs incurred by the Insured Person after discharge from hospital that are directly related to the same condition for which the admission took place. These may include follow-up consultations, diagnostic tests, medicines and dressings forming part of the ongoing treatment of the condition.

Q: Are pre and post-hospitalisation expenses a separate benefit?

A: Pre and post-hospitalisation expenses are covered as part of the main hospitalisation claim under Clause 2.2 of the applicable Policy Wording. They are not a standalone benefit and must be claimed together with the main hospitalisation claim. They draw from the same sum insured.

Q: Do pre and post-hospitalisation expenses attract their own sub-limits?

A: Pre and post-hospitalisation expenses do not have a separate monetary sub-limit under the base Policy Wording. However, they are subject to the overall sum insured and to any sub-limits that may apply to the underlying procedure. For specific sub-limit details, refer to Clause 3.2 of the applicable Policy Wording.

## Section 2 What Is the Coverage Window for These Expenses

Q: How many days before admission are pre-hospitalisation expenses covered?

A: Under Clause 2.2.1 of the applicable Policy Wording, pre-hospitalisation expenses are covered for up to 30 days before the date of admission to the Hospital.

Q: How many days after discharge are post-hospitalisation expenses covered?

A: Under Clause 2.2.2, post-hospitalisation expenses are covered for up to 60 days from the date of discharge from the Hospital.

Q: Does the coverage window extend if treatment takes longer than expected?

A: No. The 30-day pre-hospitalisation window and the 60-day post-hospitalisation window are fixed periods under the Policy Wording. Expenses falling outside these windows are not covered under Clause 2.2, regardless of whether they are related to the same condition.

## Section 3 Which Pre and Post Hospitalisation Expenses Are Covered

Q: What types of expenses are covered in the pre and post-hospitalisation period?

A: Covered expenses in both periods include outpatient consultations with the treating physician, diagnostic tests and investigations ordered by the treating physician, medicines prescribed for the condition being treated, and other expenses that are directly related to the condition for which the Insured Person was admitted.

Q: Are all expenses incurred in the window automatically covered?

A: No. The expenses must be directly related to the condition for which the hospitalisation took place. Routine medical check-ups, treatment of unrelated conditions and over-the-counter supplements not prescribed for the specific condition are not covered under this benefit.

Q: Can pre and post-hospitalisation expenses be claimed for a Day Care Procedure?

A: Yes. The pre and post-hospitalisation benefit applies to covered Day Care Procedure claims as well as to in-patient hospitalisation claims, subject to the same 30-day and 60-day windows and the same requirement that expenses are directly related to the condition treated.

## Section 4 How to Claim Pre and Post Hospitalisation Expenses

Q: How do I submit pre and post-hospitalisation expenses?

A: Pre and post-hospitalisation expenses must be submitted together with the main hospitalisation or Day Care claim documents. All original bills, receipts, prescriptions and diagnostic reports relating to the pre and post periods must be included. Under Clause 6.2.1, all reimbursement documents must be submitted within 30 days of the date of discharge.

Q: Can I claim pre and post-hospitalisation expenses separately after the main claim is closed?

A: Standalone claims for pre or post-hospitalisation expenses that are submitted independently of the main hospitalisation claim will not be entertained, as stated in Clause 2.2.3 of the applicable Policy Wording. All expenses should be consolidated into a single submission.

Q: What if post-hospitalisation treatment is still ongoing when I submit the claim?

A: Submit the main hospitalisation claim promptly once you have been discharged and all mandatory documents are available. Post-hospitalisation expenses that are incurred after the initial submission but within the 60-day window may be submitted as a supplementary claim, referencing the original claim number. Consult the Company's helpline for guidance on the submission process for supplementary items.
