## Section 1 Is the Cashless Facility Available Under Health Silver

Q: Can I use the cashless facility with my Health Silver policy?

A: Yes. The cashless facility is available to Health Silver policyholders at Network Hospitals — hospitals that have a valid cashless arrangement with the Company or its designated third-party administrator. The cashless facility under Health Silver operates in the same way as under Health Gold. When you are admitted to a Network Hospital, the hospital arranges pre-authorisation with the Company and the eligible portion of your bill is settled directly between the hospital and the Company.

Q: How do I find out which hospitals are on the network?

A: The Network Hospital list is available on the Company's website and can be accessed through the customer helpline. You should check whether your preferred hospital is on the network before a planned admission. Network membership can change, so always verify the current status rather than relying on a previous confirmation.

Q: Is the cashless facility available at all hospitals?

A: No. Cashless treatment is available only at hospitals that are on the Company's current Network Hospital list. If you receive treatment at a hospital that is not on the network, the cashless facility does not apply, and you will need to pay the hospital directly and submit a reimbursement claim under Clause 7.2 of the Health Silver Policy Wording.

## Section 2 How Do I Use the Cashless Facility Under Health Silver

Q: What do I need to do to use cashless at a Network Hospital?

A: For a planned admission: notify the Company or its third-party administrator at least 48 hours before the date of admission and ask the hospital to submit the pre-authorisation request on your behalf. For an emergency admission: notify the Company within 24 hours of admission. The hospital will then coordinate the pre-authorisation process.

Q: How quickly will the pre-authorisation decision be issued?

A: The Company must issue a pre-authorisation decision within 1 hour of receiving the complete pre-authorisation request from the hospital. For the final discharge authorisation, the decision must be issued within 3 hours of receiving the hospital's final bill.

Q: Does a pre-authorisation approval guarantee the full bill will be paid?

A: Pre-authorisation is an in-principle approval based on the information available at the time of the request. The final settlement is subject to the actual treatment rendered, the submission of complete documents, and verification of the terms of the Health Silver Policy Wording. Amounts that are not covered — for example, those subject to exclusions, waiting periods or sub-limits — will not be included in the cashless settlement.

## Section 3 What Happens If I Am Admitted to a Non-Network Hospital Under Health Silver

Q: What should I do if I am admitted to a non-network hospital?

A: If the hospital is not on the Company's network, you should pay the hospital bill directly and then submit a reimbursement claim under Clause 7.2 of the Health Silver Policy Wording. The eligible amount will be reimbursed directly to your bank account after the assessment is completed.

Q: Does using a non-network hospital reduce my cover?

A: The same policy terms, sub-limits and applicable deductions apply whether you use a network or a non-network hospital. Using a non-network hospital means you must pay upfront and claim reimbursement, but the amount ultimately reimbursable is calculated on the same basis as a cashless claim, subject to the terms of the Policy Wording.

Q: What documents must I submit for a reimbursement claim after using a non-network hospital?

A: All mandatory documents set out in Clause 6.2 of the Health Silver Policy Wording must be submitted within 30 days of discharge. These include the original discharge summary, the original itemised final bill, all original receipts, diagnostic reports, prescriptions, a valid identity document and bank account details for the electronic fund transfer.

## Section 4 What Should I Know Before a Planned Admission Under Health Silver

Q: Are waiting periods checked at the cashless pre-authorisation stage?

A: Yes. When the pre-authorisation request is received, the claims team verifies whether any applicable waiting period under the Health Silver Policy Wording has been completed as at the date of admission. If a waiting period has not been completed, the cashless request may not be approved. For the specific waiting periods under Health Silver, refer to the Health Silver Policy Wording.

Q: Does the room rent limit affect cashless claims?

A: Yes. The Health Silver Policy Wording applies a room rent limit per day of hospitalisation for standard in-patient rooms, as defined in Clause 8.1. If the room category chosen is above the eligible limit, a proportionate deduction will apply to associated medical expenses in accordance with Clause 8.2. For the specific room rent limit applicable to your policy, refer to the Health Silver Policy Wording.

Q: What should I bring to the hospital for a cashless admission?

A: Bring your Health Silver policy details or e-card, a valid photo identity document, and the Company's customer helpline number. Presenting your policy details at admission helps the hospital to initiate the cashless pre-authorisation process promptly.
