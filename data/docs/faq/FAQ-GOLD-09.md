## Section 1 What Are Riders and How Do They Work Under Health Gold

Q: What is a rider?

A: A rider is an optional add-on benefit that can be attached to the base Health Gold policy at inception or, in some cases, at renewal. Each rider modifies or supplements the cover provided by the base policy in a specific way. Riders are shown in the Policy Schedule and are subject to their own terms and conditions, which are set out in the applicable rider document. All riders operate alongside the base policy; the base policy terms continue to apply except where a rider expressly modifies them.

Q: Does a rider increase the sum insured?

A: Not automatically. Some riders — such as the Critical Illness Lump Sum Rider and the Personal Accident Rider — provide a separate benefit that is paid in addition to any amount settled under the base policy. Other riders, such as the Room Rent Waiver Rider, modify the terms under which claims are assessed without increasing the sum insured itself. Refer to the specific rider document for details.

Q: Are rider benefits payable in addition to the base policy settlement?

A: This depends on the rider. Where a rider provides a lump sum on a specific event, the payment is made in addition to any in-patient settlement under the base policy. Where a rider modifies an assessment rule — such as waiving the room rent limit — the effect is applied to the existing claim rather than generating a separate payment. Each rider document defines how the benefit interacts with the base policy.

## Section 2 What Riders Are Available Under Health Gold

Q: Which riders can be added to a Health Gold policy?

A: The following riders are available under Health Gold. Eligibility conditions, benefit amounts and premium for each rider are as specified in the Policy Schedule.

Waiting Period Waiver Rider (RID-WPW-01): modifies the waiting period applicable to joint replacement surgery for eligible Insured Persons; available only at inception under Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 for Insured Persons aged 45 or below. Refer to RID-WPW-01 for eligibility and conditions.

Room Rent Waiver Rider (RID-RRW-01): removes the room rent limit and the proportionate deduction that would otherwise apply under Clause 8.1 and 8.2 of the base Policy Wording. The benefit applies per hospitalisation, with no annual cap beyond the sum insured.

Outpatient Care Rider (RID-OPD-01): extends cover to outpatient consultations, diagnostic tests and prescribed medicines outside a hospitalisation event, up to the annual limit stated in the Policy Schedule.

Critical Illness Lump Sum Rider (RID-CI-01): pays a lump sum on the first diagnosis of a covered critical illness, subject to a survival condition. The list of covered conditions and the benefit amount are stated in the Policy Schedule.

Hospital Cash Rider (RID-HC-01): pays a daily cash amount for each day of qualifying hospitalisation. The daily amount and maximum period are as specified in the Policy Schedule.

Alternative Medicine Treatment Rider (RID-AYUSH-01): covers treatment under recognised alternative medicine systems for qualifying conditions. Benefit details are as specified in the Policy Schedule.

Personal Accident Rider (RID-PA-01): provides benefit on accidental injury, including specified categories of disability or accidental death. Benefit as specified in the Policy Schedule.

## Section 3 When and How Can I Add a Rider

Q: Can I add a rider at any time?

A: Most riders are available at inception or at annual renewal. Mid-term addition of a rider is not ordinarily permitted, except in limited circumstances specified in the rider terms. The best time to add or review riders is at renewal.

Q: Is an additional premium charged for each rider?

A: Yes. Each rider attracts a separate additional premium, which is stated in the Policy Schedule at the time the rider is added. Premium for a rider may change at renewal in line with the Company's rating.

Q: Does adding a rider require fresh underwriting?

A: Some riders require a health declaration or medical evidence before they can be added, particularly those whose benefit is linked to health status. For example, the Critical Illness Lump Sum Rider and the Waiting Period Waiver Rider have specific eligibility criteria. Refer to the respective rider documents and the Company's underwriting guidelines for details.

## Section 4 Common Questions About Rider Benefits

Q: If I have both the Room Rent Waiver Rider and other riders, how do they interact?

A: Each rider operates independently of others. The Room Rent Waiver Rider removes the room rent assessment, which means the proportionate deduction does not apply to the base claim. Other riders — such as Hospital Cash — are assessed separately and are not affected by the room rent waiver.

Q: If the base policy claim is declined, are rider benefits still paid?

A: It depends on the rider. Benefits that are specifically triggered by an event distinct from in-patient hospitalisation — such as a Critical Illness lump sum or a Personal Accident benefit — may still be payable on their own terms. Benefits that depend on a covered hospitalisation — such as Hospital Cash — would not be payable if the hospitalisation itself is not covered.

Q: Where can I find the full terms of each rider?

A: Each rider has its own document in the data/docs/riders folder. These are RID-WPW-01, RID-OPD-01, RID-CI-01, RID-RRW-01, RID-HC-01, RID-AYUSH-01 and RID-PA-01. The applicable rider documents are provided to the Policyholder when a rider is added to the policy.
