## Section 1 About Health Silver

Health Silver is an affordable health insurance product from Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. designed to give individuals and families essential protection against the financial impact of hospitalisation. With straightforward cover, a wide network of cashless hospitals and a simple claims process, Health Silver is built for those who want reliable protection without complexity.

Health Silver is available on an individual basis or as a family floater, covering your spouse, dependent children and dependent parents under a single shared sum insured. With three sum insured options — ₹2,00,000; ₹3,00,000; and ₹5,00,000 — you can choose the level of protection that fits your needs and your budget.

Health Silver is renewed annually. As your policy matures with Continuous Cover, you build toward completing the waiting periods that unlock cover for a broader range of conditions.

This brochure is a marketing summary. For complete details of waiting periods, sub-limits, exclusions and all other terms applicable to your Health Silver policy, refer to the Health Silver Policy Wording.

## Section 2 What Does Health Silver Cover

Health Silver provides a comprehensive package of core hospitalisation benefits.

In-patient Hospitalisation: cover for admissions of at least 24 hours for Medically Necessary Treatment. Covered expenses include room charges (subject to the room rent limit in the Policy Wording), ICU charges at actuals, surgeon and anaesthetist fees, operation theatre charges, nursing, diagnostic tests and medicines during the admission.

Day Care Procedures: many surgical procedures can now be completed in less than 24 hours. Health Silver covers Day Care Procedures on the Company's approved list, so you are not penalised for technology-driven shorter stays.

Pre and Post Hospitalisation: Health Silver covers eligible expenses up to 30 days before a covered admission and up to 60 days after discharge, ensuring that the costs of preparing for and recovering from hospitalisation are included in your cover.

Domiciliary Hospitalisation: when treatment at home is medically required and continues for more than 3 consecutive days, Health Silver covers those expenses.

Road Ambulance: up to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation event for a registered road ambulance journey to the Hospital.

ICU Cover: Intensive Care Unit and ICCU charges are covered at actuals with no monetary cap per day, subject to medical necessity.

For waiting periods applicable to specific conditions, refer to the Health Silver Policy Wording.

## Section 3 Sum Insured Options and Key Benefits

Health Silver gives you three clear sum insured choices:

₹2,00,000: ideal for individuals seeking essential, affordable cover.
₹3,00,000: a popular option for individuals and couples.
₹5,00,000: comprehensive cover for families.

The sum insured is the maximum the Company pays across all claims in a Policy Year. It resets fully at each renewal.

Sub-limits apply to certain procedures under Health Silver, as defined in Clause 3.2 of the Health Silver Policy Wording. For the specific sub-limit amounts applicable to your policy, refer to the Policy Wording rather than to this brochure.

Room rent is covered subject to a per-day limit for standard rooms, as stated in Clause 8.1 of the Health Silver Policy Wording. ICU charges are covered at actuals. For the specific room rent limit, refer to the Policy Wording.

For complete details of waiting periods, sub-limits, exclusions and all other terms and limits applicable to your Health Silver policy, refer to the Health Silver Policy Wording.

## Section 4 How to Buy and How to Claim

**Buying Health Silver:** Contact the Company's sales helpline, visit a branch or apply through an authorised intermediary. You will need to complete a proposal form and declare any existing health conditions. Medical tests may be required for Insured Persons aged 46 and above.

**Cashless claims:** Health Silver policyholders may use the cashless facility at any Network Hospital by notifying the Company before a planned admission and presenting their policy details at the hospital. For planned admissions, notify us at least 48 hours in advance; for emergencies, within 24 hours.

**Reimbursement claims:** If you are treated at a non-network hospital, pay the hospital directly and submit your claim documents to us within 30 days of discharge.

**Grievances:** If you are dissatisfied with any claim decision or service experience, you may raise a grievance with our Grievance Redressal Officer. Contact details are in the Policy Schedule. We will acknowledge your grievance within 3 working days and resolve it within 14 days.

**Contact us:** Our customer helpline and website provide network hospital details, claim status updates and all the information you need to get the most from your Health Silver policy.
