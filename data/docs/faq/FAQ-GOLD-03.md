## Section 1 What Is the Cashless Facility

Q: What is the cashless facility under Health Gold?

A: The cashless facility allows an Insured Person to receive covered in-patient treatment at a Network Hospital without making an upfront payment for eligible expenses. Instead of paying the hospital and then claiming reimbursement, the Company settles the eligible portion of the bill directly with the hospital, subject to pre-authorisation and the applicable terms of the Policy Wording (Clause 7.1).

Q: Which hospitals offer cashless treatment?

A: Cashless treatment is available only at hospitals that are listed on the Company's current Network Hospital list at the time of admission. A Network Hospital is one that has a valid cashless arrangement with the Company or its designated third-party administrator (Clause 1.1.4). The network list can be checked through the Company's helpline or website. Policyholders should confirm network status before elective admissions.

Q: Is cashless available for day care procedures?

A: Yes, the cashless facility may be used for Day Care Procedures performed at a Network Hospital, provided the procedure is on the Company's Day Care Procedure list and pre-authorisation is obtained.

## Section 2 How Do I Use Cashless Treatment at a Network Hospital

Q: What is the process for using cashless at a planned admission?

A: For a planned hospitalisation, the Insured Person or the Network Hospital must submit a cashless pre-authorisation request to the Company or its third-party administrator at least 48 hours before the planned admission date (Clause 6.1.1). The request must include the provisional diagnosis, proposed treatment and estimated cost. The Company must issue a pre-authorisation decision within 1 hour of receiving the complete request.

Q: What is the process for a cashless emergency admission?

A: For an emergency admission, the Network Hospital or a family member should notify the Company and submit the pre-authorisation request within 24 hours of admission (Clause 6.1.2). The Company will process the pre-authorisation as quickly as possible to enable seamless cashless settlement at discharge.

Q: What should the Insured Person carry to a Network Hospital?

A: The Insured Person should carry the original health insurance policy or e-card, a valid photo identity document, and the Company's helpline number. Presenting the policy or e-card at the time of admission helps the hospital to initiate the cashless process promptly.

## Section 3 What Happens If I Am Admitted to a Non-Network Hospital

Q: Can I still claim if I am treated at a non-network hospital?

A: Yes. If the Insured Person receives treatment at a hospital that is not on the Company's network, the cashless facility is not available. However, the Insured Person may pay the hospital directly and submit a reimbursement claim under Clause 7.2. The reimbursement process requires submission of all original bills, discharge summary and other mandatory documents within 30 days of discharge.

Q: Will I receive the same amount as under cashless?

A: The reimbursement amount will be the lesser of the actual expenses incurred or the amount that would have been payable had treatment been received at a Network Hospital, subject to all applicable limits and deductions (Clause 7.2.3). Choosing a non-network hospital does not reduce the cover available, but the same policy terms, sub-limits and deductions apply.

## Section 4 Can the Cashless Facility Be Withdrawn After Pre-authorisation

Q: Is pre-authorisation a guarantee of full payment?

A: No. Pre-authorisation approval is an in-principle confirmation based on the information available at the time of the request. Final settlement is subject to the actual expenses incurred, submission of complete documents and verification of the terms of the Policy Wording (Clause 7.1.4). If the actual treatment differs from what was pre-authorised, or if documents reveal a policy exclusion or waiting period, the settlement may differ from the pre-authorised amount.

Q: What if the cashless facility is declined at the hospital?

A: If the cashless facility is declined — for example, because the pre-authorisation was not approved or the hospital is not on the network — the Insured Person may pay the hospital and submit a reimbursement claim under Clause 7.2. If the Insured Person believes the decline was incorrect, they may raise a grievance with the Grievance Redressal Officer after receiving the written rejection. The grievance process is described in Clause 10.1 of the Policy Wording.
