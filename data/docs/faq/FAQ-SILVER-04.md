## Section 1 How Do I Renew My Health Silver Policy

Q: How do I renew my Health Silver policy?

A: Health Silver is renewed annually. The Company will issue a renewal notice to the Policyholder before the policy expiry date, as required by Clause 9.1.1 of the Health Silver Policy Wording. The Policyholder is responsible for ensuring that the renewal premium is paid on time. If you wish to change the sum insured option or add a rider at renewal, please contact the Company before the expiry date, as changes are subject to underwriting review.

Q: When will I receive my renewal notice?

A: The renewal notice will be issued before the policy expiry date as specified in the applicable Policy Wording. It will state the renewal premium, the expiry date, the grace period available and any changes to the premium or terms at renewal. If you have not received a renewal notice when you expect it, please contact the Company helpline and quote your policy number.

Q: Can I change the sum insured at renewal?

A: Yes. You may apply to change the sum insured option at renewal. The available options under Health Silver are ₹2,00,000; ₹3,00,000; and ₹5,00,000. Any increase in sum insured is subject to underwriting review in accordance with UW-SUMINS-01 and may require a fresh health declaration and medical tests for Insured Persons aged 46 and above.

## Section 2 What Is the Grace Period Under Health Silver

Q: Is there a grace period for paying the renewal premium?

A: Yes. A grace period of 30 days is available after the policy expiry date for payment of the renewal premium, as stated in Clause 9.1.2 of the Health Silver Policy Wording. If you pay within the grace period, your Continuous Cover is preserved.

Q: Am I covered during the grace period?

A: No. During the 30-day grace period, no cover is available for new losses. If you are admitted to hospital during the grace period, that hospitalisation will not be covered under the policy. It is important to renew before the expiry date wherever possible.

Q: What happens if I do not renew within the grace period?

A: If the renewal premium is not received within the 30-day grace period, the policy lapses. A lapsed policy may be reinstated at the Company's discretion, subject to payment of the outstanding premium and a satisfactory declaration of continued good health. All waiting periods restart from the reinstatement date.

## Section 3 Can I Transfer My Health Silver Policy with Portability

Q: What is portability?

A: Portability is the right of an Insured Person to transfer their health insurance policy from one registered insurer to another and retain credit for the waiting periods already served, without having to restart waiting periods from scratch. Portability is available under Health Silver in accordance with Clause 9.2 of the Health Silver Policy Wording and the NIR's portability guidelines.

Q: How do I apply for portability when transferring to Health Silver?

A: To apply for portability, you must submit a portability application to Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. at least 45 days before the renewal date of your existing policy with the other insurer. Applications received less than 45 days before the renewal date may not be processed in time and may be declined.

Q: What documents do I need for a portability application?

A: You will need to provide: your existing policy documents and renewal certificates showing uninterrupted cover; and a completed Aarogya Shield proposal form. Our underwriting team will verify your prior cover history and confirm the portability credit applicable to your waiting periods.

## Section 4 How Does Portability Credit Work Under Health Silver

Q: Which waiting periods are reduced through portability?

A: Portability credit applies to the specific waiting periods and the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period under the Health Silver Policy Wording. The credit equals the number of months of Continuous Cover already completed under the prior policy, up to the applicable waiting period. The specific waiting periods under Health Silver are stated in the Policy Wording; for existing waiting period details refer to the Health Silver Policy Wording or to the Health Silver FAQ on waiting periods.

Q: Does portability credit affect the sum insured or premium?

A: No. Portability credit applies to waiting period credits only. The sum insured, premium and all other policy terms under Health Silver are set fresh under the new policy. All benefits are governed solely by the Health Silver Policy Wording.

Q: What happens if there is a gap in my cover before transferring to Health Silver?

A: If your prior policy lapses before you transfer to Health Silver, Continuous Cover is broken. Full waiting periods apply afresh under the new Health Silver policy from the inception date, as stated in Clause 9.2.5 of the Health Silver Policy Wording.
