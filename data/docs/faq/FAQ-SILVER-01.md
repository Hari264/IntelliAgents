## Section 1 Who Can Buy Health Silver

Q: Who is eligible to purchase Health Silver?

A: Health Silver is available to individuals between 18 and 65 years of age who are residents of India. Cover is also available on a family floater basis, allowing the Policyholder and eligible family members to be covered under a single Policy with a shared sum insured. Children may typically be included from 91 days of age up to the maximum age specified at the time of purchase, subject to the Company's underwriting guidelines. Existing health conditions do not automatically disqualify a proposer, but they must be declared on the proposal form and will be subject to the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period under Clause 4.3.

Q: Can I buy Health Silver if I already have another health policy?

A: Yes. Health Silver can be purchased as a standalone policy or alongside an existing policy. If you have a prior health insurance policy with Continuous Cover and wish to transfer to Health Silver, you may apply for portability credit under Clause 9.2 so that waiting periods already served under the prior policy are credited.

Q: Is Health Silver the same as Health Gold?

A: No. Health Silver and Health Gold are separate products with different sum insured options, sub-limits and waiting periods. Customers who require higher sum insured options or different terms should enquire about Health Gold. The applicable terms are always those stated in the Policy Schedule and the relevant policy wording.

## Section 2 What Does Health Silver Cover

Q: What types of hospitalisation are covered under Health Silver?

A: Health Silver covers the following:

In-patient Hospitalisation (Clause 2.1): admission to a Hospital for at least 24 continuous hours for Medically Necessary Treatment. Covered expenses include room charges (subject to the room rent limit in Clause 8.1), ICU charges, surgeon and anaesthetist fees, nursing charges, operation theatre charges, diagnostic tests ordered during admission, medicines and internal prosthetic devices used during surgery.

Pre and Post Hospitalisation (Clause 2.2): medical expenses incurred up to 30 days before and up to 60 days after a covered hospitalisation, provided the expenses relate to the same condition.

Day Care Procedures (Clause 2.3): procedures listed in the Company's Day Care list, which do not require 24 hours of hospitalisation due to advances in medical technology.

Domiciliary Hospitalisation (Clause 2.4): treatment at home that continues for more than 3 consecutive days, where hospitalisation was medically inadvisable.

Road Ambulance (Clause 2.5): up to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation event for a registered road ambulance.

Q: Are accidental injuries covered from day one?

A: Yes. Claims arising directly from an accidental bodily injury are covered from the first day of the Policy Period and are not subject to the initial waiting period.

## Section 3 What Sum Insured Options Are Available Under Health Silver

Q: What sum insured options can I choose?

A: Under Health Silver, the available sum insured options are ₹2,00,000; ₹3,00,000; and ₹5,00,000. The sum insured selected is stated in the Policy Schedule and represents the maximum amount the Company will pay across all covered claims in a Policy Year.

Q: Are there any monetary sub-limits within the sum insured?

A: Yes. The following sub-limits apply within the overall sum insured under Health Silver:

Cataract surgery: ₹25,000 per eye per Policy Year (Clause 3.2.1).
Joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty): ₹1,50,000 per joint per event (Clause 3.2.2).
Road ambulance: ₹2,000 per hospitalisation event (Clause 3.2.3).

Amounts paid under a sub-limit reduce the available balance of the sum insured.

Q: Does the sum insured reset each year?

A: Yes. The sum insured resets to its full value at the start of each new Policy Year upon timely renewal.

## Section 4 What Is Excluded Under Health Silver

Q: Is maternity covered under Health Silver?

A: No. Maternity benefits, including expenses related to childbirth, delivery, Caesarean section, miscarriage and pre and post-natal care, are not covered under Health Silver (Clause 2.6).

Q: Are there permanent exclusions that can never be claimed?

A: Yes. Permanent exclusions under Clause 5.1 include: war and related events, nuclear and chemical contamination, intentional self-inflicted injury and suicide. These cannot be claimed regardless of any waiting periods served.

Q: What other conditions are specifically excluded?

A: Specific exclusions under Clause 5.2 include treatment arising from substance abuse, non-surgical obesity treatment, spectacles and hearing aids (unless needed as a direct consequence of a covered procedure), and experimental or unproven treatments under Clause 5.3. Dental treatment is covered only when it arises directly from an accidental injury requiring hospitalisation (Clause 5.4).

Q: Does Health Silver cover waiting periods that apply to certain conditions?

A: Yes. Certain conditions are subject to waiting periods. These are set out in Clauses 4.1, 4.2 and 4.3 of the Policy Wording. For detailed waiting period information, please refer to FAQ-SILVER-02.
