## Section 1 What Sum Insured Options Are Available Under Health Gold

Q: What are the available sum insured options under Health Gold?

A: Under Health Gold, the following sum insured options are available: ₹3,00,000; ₹5,00,000; ₹10,00,000; and ₹25,00,000. The sum insured selected by the Policyholder is stated in the Policy Schedule. For Health Silver, different options are available; refer to the Health Silver Policy Wording.

Q: What does the sum insured represent?

A: The sum insured is the maximum aggregate amount the Company will pay for all covered claims made by or on behalf of an Insured Person in a Policy Year (Clause 3.1 of the applicable Policy Wording). Once the sum insured is exhausted by claims in a Policy Year, no further claims are payable until the sum insured resets at the start of the next Policy Year upon renewal.

Q: Does the sum insured reset each year?

A: Yes. The sum insured resets to its full value at the start of each new Policy Year when the policy is renewed with the premium paid on time. It does not carry forward unused amounts from one year to the next.

Q: Does a family floater work differently?

A: Yes. For a family floater policy, the sum insured is shared among all Insured Persons named on the Policy Schedule. Claims made by any covered family member draw from the same shared pool. The sum insured is not split equally among family members; any member may use all or part of the shared amount, subject to the total not being exceeded in the Policy Year.

## Section 2 How Does the Sum Insured Work in Practice

Q: If a family member uses most of the sum insured, is the rest of the family covered?

A: Under a family floater, once the shared sum insured is exhausted by any combination of claims in a Policy Year, no further claims are payable until the sum insured resets at renewal. The Policyholder should choose a sum insured option that is adequate for the size and health profile of the family.

Q: Does the road ambulance benefit reduce the sum insured?

A: Yes. The road ambulance reimbursement of up to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation event (Clause 2.5) is included within and reduces the available sum insured balance for that Policy Year.

Q: Can the sum insured be exhausted without a hospitalisation claim?

A: The sum insured can only be reduced by claims that are covered and payable under the applicable Policy Wording. Administrative requests, pre-authorisation approvals and rejected claims do not reduce the sum insured.

## Section 3 Are There Sub-limits Within the Sum Insured

Q: What is a sub-limit?

A: A sub-limit is a monetary cap that applies to a specific procedure or benefit within the overall sum insured (Clause 3.2 of the applicable Policy Wording). A sub-limit is not additional to the sum insured; it operates within it. If the actual cost of a sub-limited procedure exceeds the sub-limit, the balance above the sub-limit is not payable from any other benefit under the policy.

Q: Which procedures are subject to sub-limits under Health Gold?

A: Certain procedures are subject to sub-limits as defined in Clause 3.2 of the applicable Policy Wording. The specific procedures and the applicable amounts are stated in the Policy Wording and the Policy Schedule. For the sub-limits applicable to your policy, refer to the applicable Policy Wording rather than to this FAQ, as the amounts are defined there. The road ambulance benefit is also subject to a per-event limit as stated in Clause 2.5.

Q: Does a sub-limit apply per claim or per year?

A: Sub-limits are typically stated per event (such as per eye for certain procedures) or per hospitalisation event. Refer to Clause 3.2 of the applicable Policy Wording for the specific basis on which each sub-limit is stated.

## Section 4 Can I Change My Sum Insured

Q: Can I increase my sum insured?

A: Yes. A Policyholder may apply for a sum insured enhancement at renewal, subject to the conditions in UW-SUMINS-01 and the Company's underwriting review. Mid-term enhancements are not ordinarily available. Fresh medical tests and a declaration of good health may be required.

Q: Can I decrease my sum insured at renewal?

A: A reduction in sum insured is permissible at renewal and is treated as an administrative change rather than a fresh underwriting decision. The Policyholder must inform the Company of the change before the renewal date.

Q: If I increase my sum insured, do my waiting periods restart?

A: Waiting periods already served under the existing sum insured option continue. The enhanced portion of the cover may be subject to fresh waiting periods applicable to the increment, as determined by the underwriting review. The specific treatment of waiting periods on a sum insured enhancement is confirmed in writing in the revised Policy Schedule.
