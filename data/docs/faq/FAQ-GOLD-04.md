## Section 1 How and When Do I Renew My Health Gold Policy

Q: How do I renew Health Gold?

A: Health Gold is renewable annually. The Company will issue a renewal notice to the Policyholder at least 30 days before the Policy expiry date. However, the Policyholder bears primary responsibility for ensuring timely renewal. The renewal premium must be paid before the policy expiry date to ensure uninterrupted cover. The Policyholder's right to renew is subject to the Company's underwriting guidelines and Clause 9.1 of the Policy Wording.

Q: Can I change my sum insured or add riders at renewal?

A: Yes. At renewal, the Policyholder may apply to change the sum insured option or to add or remove riders, subject to the Company's underwriting requirements. Increases in sum insured may be subject to fresh underwriting, including medical tests for Insured Persons in the applicable age bands.

Q: Is renewal guaranteed?

A: Health Gold is a renewable product and renewal will generally not be declined unless there are specific underwriting grounds to do so. The Company's approach to renewal follows the guidelines of the National Insurance Regulator.

## Section 2 What Is the Grace Period and What Does It Mean

Q: What is the grace period for Health Gold?

A: A grace period of 30 days is available after the Policy expiry date for payment of the renewal premium (Clause 9.1.2). The policy is considered to be in force during the grace period for the purpose of Continuous Cover continuity.

Q: Is cover available during the grace period?

A: No. Although the policy is considered in force for continuity purposes during the grace period, no claim arising from a loss that occurs during the grace period is payable by the Company (Clause 9.1.2). The Insured Person is not covered for new hospitalisation events during the grace period.

Q: What happens if the policy lapses?

A: If the renewal premium is not paid within the 30-day grace period, the policy lapses. A lapsed policy may be reinstated at the Company's discretion, subject to payment of arrears and a satisfactory declaration of good health. Waiting periods recommence from the date of reinstatement, and Continuous Cover is broken (Clause 9.1.3).

## Section 3 Can I Transfer My Policy from Another Insurer

Q: What is portability?

A: Portability is the right of an Insured Person to transfer their health insurance policy from one registered insurer to another without losing credit for the waiting periods already completed under their prior policy. This right is governed by the National Insurance Regulator's portability guidelines, as reflected in Clause 9.2 of the Health Gold Policy Wording.

Q: How do I apply for portability?

A: A portability application must be submitted to the Company at least 45 days before the renewal date of the prior policy (Clause 9.2.2). Applications submitted less than 45 days before the prior policy's renewal may be declined at the Company's discretion. The application must be supported by the prior insurer's renewal certificate and policy documents.

Q: What if I have a break in cover between policies?

A: If there is a break in Continuous Cover between the expiry of the prior policy and the commencement of Health Gold, the portability credit is not available and the full waiting periods under Clauses 4.2 and 4.3 apply afresh from the Health Gold policy start date (Clause 9.2.5).

## Section 4 How Does Portability Affect My Waiting Periods

Q: Which waiting periods are reduced through portability?

A: Portability credit applies to the specific waiting periods under Clause 4.2 (including cataract, hernia, bariatric surgery and joint replacement) and the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period under Clause 4.3. The credit is equal to the number of months of Continuous Cover already completed under the prior policy, up to the applicable waiting period for each condition.

Q: How is portability credit calculated?

A: The Company calculates portability credit based on the prior insurer's renewal certificate and, where needed, information from the Insurance Information Bureau (Clause 9.2.3). The credit is reflected in the Health Gold policy file and reduces the effective waiting period for the relevant conditions. Waiting periods that have already been fully served under the prior policy are treated as fully served under Health Gold as well.

Q: Does portability affect the sum insured or riders?

A: No. Portability credit applies only to waiting period credits. It does not transfer the sum insured, premium, riders or any other benefit from the prior insurer. All benefits under Health Gold are governed solely by the Health Gold Policy Wording (Clause 9.2.4).
