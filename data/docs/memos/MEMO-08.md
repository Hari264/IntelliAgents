## Section 1 Purpose

This memo informs service staff of the ongoing Customer Call Quality Review programme and what it means for day-to-day service interactions. The programme exists to ensure that customers receive accurate, respectful and compliant service on every call. High-quality calls reduce complaints, support grievance prevention and protect the Company from regulatory risk.

All calls handled by service staff are subject to review under this programme, whether inbound or outbound. Calls are selected for review on a sample basis by the Quality Assurance function in coordination with service team supervisors. The review criteria are set out in Section 2. Feedback is provided to individual agents and their supervisors as described in Section 3.

## Section 2 Review Criteria

Customer calls are reviewed against the following criteria. Agents should consider each criterion before and during every call.

2.1 Identity verification: the agent must confirm the customer's identity — including their name and date of birth — before discussing any claim or policy details. Skipping this step is a critical failure in a call review.

2.2 Accuracy of information: all information communicated to the customer — claim status, settlement amounts, document requirements, timelines — must match the records in the claims management system at the time of the call. Agents must check the system before and during the call and must not quote information from memory.

2.3 Use of approved scripts: where an approved customer script from the SCR series applies to the call scenario, the agent must use it. Scripts for common scenarios — including claim status enquiries, grievance registration, settlement confirmations and document follow-ups — are available in the claims management system. Deviating from the script without supervisor approval is a quality failure.

2.4 Compliance with communication standards: agents must comply with all standards in SOP-COMMS-01. In particular, they must not make promises about claim outcomes, must not share information from one Policyholder's record with another caller, and must follow the specific communication restrictions that apply to certain claim statuses. Any call in which restricted information is disclosed to an unauthorised party is a critical quality failure.

2.5 Tone and empathy: agents must be polite, calm and empathetic throughout the call, including when delivering unwelcome decisions. The agent must acknowledge the customer's situation before delivering a decision.

## Section 3 Feedback and Improvement

3.1 Each reviewed call is scored against the criteria in Section 2. The scoring framework and the threshold for satisfactory performance are maintained by the Quality Assurance function and communicated to supervisors at the start of each review cycle.

3.2 Feedback for individual agents is shared with the agent's direct supervisor following each review cycle. The supervisor must discuss the feedback with the agent within a reasonable period and agree on specific actions where the call did not meet the required standard.

3.3 Agents who consistently score below the satisfactory threshold will be placed in a structured improvement programme, involving supervised calls, targeted coaching and more frequent review.

3.4 Positive examples — calls that demonstrate excellent accuracy, empathy and script adherence — will be shared with the broader service team (with the caller's details removed) as models of good practice. Agents whose calls are selected as positive examples will be recognised in the team's performance records.
