## Section 1 Purpose

This memo informs claims adjusters and service staff of an update to the Company's network hospital onboarding process. The Provider Management team has completed a review of the empanelment criteria and the documentation requirements for new hospitals seeking to join the Company's Network Hospital list. The updated criteria are reflected in SOP-NETWORK-01 and are consistent with the NIR's requirements summarised in CIR-04.

The purpose of this memo is to ensure that all claims and service staff are aware of what has changed, so that any queries from hospitals or policyholders about the network or its composition are handled accurately and consistently.

## Section 2 Key Updates to the Onboarding Process

The following changes have been made to the network hospital empanelment process and should be noted by all relevant staff.

2.1 Updated documentation checklist: hospitals applying for network empanelment are now required to submit an expanded set of documents, including an up-to-date fire safety certificate from the relevant local authority and confirmation of their daily patient record management system. These additions bring the documentation checklist into alignment with the NIR's standards in CIR-04.

2.2 Site visit requirement: a site visit is now mandatory for all new hospital empanelment applications, without exception. Previously, site visits were conducted at the Provider Management team's discretion. The mandatory site visit ensures that the hospital physically meets the facilities criteria in Clause 1.1.1 of the applicable Policy Wording before the cashless arrangement is activated.

2.3 Provider agreement renewal: existing Network Hospitals whose provider agreements have not been renewed within the past cycle will be contacted by the Provider Management team to sign updated agreements. Until a renewed agreement is in place, existing cashless arrangements remain in force, but no new high-value pre-authorisations will be issued for the hospital in question until the renewal is confirmed.

2.4 Communication to policyholders: the network list on the Company's website will be updated to reflect any additions or removals resulting from the new criteria. Service staff must direct all network-related enquiries to the website or helpline rather than confirming network status from their own records.

## Section 3 Staff Action Required

3.1 Claims adjusters must familiarise themselves with the updated SOP-NETWORK-01 to understand how empanelment decisions and removal decisions are made. This is relevant to pre-authorisation assessments where the hospital's network status may be in question.

3.2 Service staff should be prepared for questions from policyholders about network changes. The correct response is to direct the customer to the Company's website or helpline for the current network list. Do not confirm whether a specific hospital is on or off the network without first checking the current system record.

3.3 If a policyholder or hospital contacts you about the empanelment process, refer them to the Provider Management team. Claims and service staff do not make empanelment decisions and must not give any indication that an application will or will not be approved.
