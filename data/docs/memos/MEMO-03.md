## Section 1 Background

This memo is addressed to all claims adjusters and service staff. It sets out the management focus on claim turnaround time (TAT) for the current quarter (Q3) and describes the specific actions the team must take to ensure compliance with regulatory and internal TAT targets.

TAT compliance is a regulatory obligation under NIR guidelines (summarised in CIR-01). Delays in settlement or cashless authorisation attract interest liability, expose the Company to regulatory findings and harm policyholder trust. Our data for Q1 and Q2 indicates that cashless discharge authorisation timelines and reimbursement settlement timelines are the two areas requiring the most immediate attention in Q3.

Team supervisors will monitor TAT performance daily through the claims management system and will escalate cases at risk of breach before the deadline is reached, not after. Every adjuster is responsible for updating claim milestones in real time to support accurate monitoring.

## Section 2 Target Timelines

All staff must be familiar with the following mandatory timelines and must not treat them as aspirational targets. They are regulatory obligations.

Cashless pre-authorisation decision: 1 hour from receipt of the complete pre-authorisation request. Where clinical information is missing, the adjuster must contact the hospital immediately and log the information request in the system. The 1-hour clock is paused while the information request is outstanding.

Cashless discharge authorisation: 3 hours from receipt of the hospital's final bill. Supervisors must be notified immediately if this deadline is at risk.

Reimbursement settlement: 30 days from receipt of the last necessary document. Where an investigation is required, 45 days. The date of last necessary document must be recorded in the system on the day the final document is received.

Rejection communication: 15 days from receipt of the last necessary document. Rejection letters must be drafted and issued within this period; late rejection communications are a regulatory breach.

Document deficiency notice: 7 working days from initial claim receipt. Do not allow incomplete claim files to sit without a deficiency notice being issued.

## Section 3 Actions Required

The following actions are required of all claims and service staff with immediate effect for Q3.

Update claim milestones in the claims management system on the same day the event occurs. A cashless request received today must be logged today; a document received today must be recorded as such. Retrospective updates are not acceptable.

Review your open claim queue each morning before taking any other action. Identify any claim approaching a TAT deadline and escalate to your supervisor before the deadline is missed, not after. The TAT breach alert report from the system is a guide, not a substitute for your own judgment.

For any cashless claim where the hospital has submitted a final bill but the discharge authorisation has not been issued, notify your supervisor immediately. No final bill should remain unactioned for more than 1 hour.

TAT performance data will be reviewed by the Head of Claims at the weekly Q3 review meeting. Team-level and individual-level data will be shared. Supervisors are asked to identify any process or system issue contributing to TAT breaches and to report them to the Head of Claims by end of the first week of each month.
