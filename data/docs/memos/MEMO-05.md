## Section 1 Purpose and Scope

This memo summarises the changes to the Health Gold product that take effect from 1 January 2025. These changes are reflected in the Health Gold Policy Wording 2025 (HG-WORD-2025), which applies to all Health Gold policies with a date of loss on or after 1 January 2025.

Adjusters must use HG-WORD-2025 for all claims where the date of loss falls on or after 1 January 2025. For claims with a date of loss before 1 January 2025, HG-WORD-2024 continues to apply. The effective date of the Policy Wording is determined by the date of loss, not the policy inception date or the renewal date.

Underwriters should note the changes to waiting periods and maternity cover when advising proposers or processing renewals from 1 January 2025. All new policies and renewals with an inception date on or after 1 January 2025 will be issued under HG-WORD-2025.

## Section 2 Product Changes Summary

The following changes apply under HG-WORD-2025 compared to HG-WORD-2024. All clause references are to HG-WORD-2025.

2.1 Clause 2.6 — Maternity Cover (new benefit):
Under HG-WORD-2024, maternity was not covered. Under HG-WORD-2025, maternity is covered. The conditions are: (a) a waiting period of 24 months of Continuous Cover must be completed before a maternity claim is payable; (b) the benefit is limited to ₹50,000 per delivery; and (c) a maximum of 2 deliveries are covered during the lifetime of the policy. Policyholders with an existing policy who renew into HG-WORD-2025 will begin accruing the 24-month maternity waiting period from 1 January 2025, subject to Clause 2.6.1.

2.2 Clause 4.2.1 — Joint Replacement Waiting Period (reduced):
Under HG-WORD-2024, the waiting period for joint replacement surgery (Arthroplasty) was 24 months. Under HG-WORD-2025, the waiting period for joint replacement surgery is reduced to 12 months. Portability credit continues to apply to this waiting period under Clause 9.2.

2.3 Clause 4.3.1 — Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period (reduced):
Under HG-WORD-2024, the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period was 48 months. Under HG-WORD-2025, the Pre-Existing Disease waiting period is reduced to 36 months. The definition of Pre-Existing Disease under Clause 1.2.1 is unchanged: the lookback period for what constitutes a PED remains 48 months before the policy start date.

2.4 Clause 8.1 — Room Rent Limit (increased):
Under HG-WORD-2024, the room rent limit for standard in-patient rooms was 1% of the sum insured per day. Under HG-WORD-2025, the room rent limit is increased to 2% of the sum insured per day. ICU and ICCU charges continue to be covered at actuals with no cap in both versions. The proportionate deduction under Clause 8.2 applies in the same manner as before, using the new 2% limit for assessments under HG-WORD-2025.

2.5 Clause 5.4 — Dental and Cosmetic Treatment (new sub-clause):
Under HG-WORD-2024, Clause 5.4 covered dental treatment only when required as a direct consequence of an accidental injury requiring hospitalisation. Under HG-WORD-2025, this provision is unchanged, but a new Clause 5.4.2 is added: "Cosmetic dental treatment, including teeth whitening, veneers and aligners, is excluded." This new sub-clause makes explicit the exclusion of cosmetic dental procedures that was previously implicit under the general cosmetic exclusion in Clause 5.4.

## Section 3 Effective Date and Implementation

All five changes described in Section 2 take effect simultaneously for claims with a date of loss on or after 1 January 2025. There is no phased implementation.

Adjusters should update their assessment approach immediately for any claim with a date of loss on or after 1 January 2025. In particular, note the following when assessing claims: always confirm the date of loss before applying any waiting period or room rent rule; check whether the date of loss is pre-2025 (HG-WORD-2024 applies) or 2025 onwards (HG-WORD-2025 applies); for maternity claims with HG-WORD-2025, verify both that 24 months of Continuous Cover have elapsed and that the ₹50,000 delivery limit applies.

Underwriters processing renewals and new applications from 1 January 2025 must use HG-WORD-2025 terms when explaining cover to proposers and when assessing risk. The reduced joint replacement and PED waiting periods under HG-WORD-2025 may affect loading decisions; refer any unusual case to the Senior Underwriter.

Questions about the application of these changes to specific claims or proposals should be directed to the team supervisor or, for underwriting questions, to the Senior Underwriter.
