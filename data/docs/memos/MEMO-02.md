## Section 1 Welcome and Purpose

Welcome to the Claims team at Aarogya Shield General Insurance Ltd. This training note is intended to orient new claims adjusters to the key documents, systems and processes you will use from your first day.

As a claims adjuster, your primary responsibilities are to assess health insurance claims accurately and fairly, to apply the terms of the applicable Policy Wording correctly, and to communicate decisions clearly to policyholders and service colleagues. Everything you need to perform these duties is governed by the policy wording documents, the Claims SOPs, the regulator circular summaries and the reference tables available in the Company's document repository.

Before handling any live claim independently, you must complete the structured onboarding programme with your team supervisor. This note supplements, but does not replace, that programme. If anything in this note conflicts with guidance from your supervisor, please raise it immediately.

## Section 2 Key Documents and Systems

The documents you will use most frequently are listed below. All are available in the Internal document repository.

Policy Wordings: for Health Gold, the applicable wording is determined by the date of loss. Claims with a date of loss in 2024 use HG-WORD-2024; claims from 1 January 2025 onwards use HG-WORD-2025. Health Silver claims use HS-WORD-2025. Always identify the correct wording version before assessing any waiting period or benefit.

Claims SOPs: the standard operating procedures govern every major claim process. Start with SOP-CASHLESS-01 (cashless pre-authorisation), SOP-REIMB-01 (reimbursement processing) and SOP-DOCS-01 (document requirements). SOP-JOINT-01 applies to all joint replacement claims and must be followed carefully.

Rejection Codes: the rejection codes reference table lists every rejection code, its category, the applicable policy clause and the corresponding customer script. Always assign the correct code before issuing a rejection or placing a claim on hold.

Customer Scripts: when calling customers about a decision, use the applicable script from the SCR series. Never deviate from the CL-601 script for claims under additional review.

Claims Management System: all claims must be registered, assessed and closed in the system. Your team supervisor will provide system access and a walkthrough in your first week.

## Section 3 Your First Two Weeks

During your first two weeks, your supervisor will pair you with a buddy adjuster who will review your initial assessments before they are finalised. Use this time to ask questions freely.

Key activities in your first two weeks: read HG-WORD-2025 in full, paying particular attention to Clauses 4, 5, 8 and 10; read SOP-CASHLESS-01, SOP-REIMB-01 and SOP-DOCS-01; shadow your buddy on at least five live cashless pre-authorisation calls and five reimbursement assessments; complete the system access training with the IT team; and attend the new joiner briefing on fraud awareness conducted by the SIU team.

Your buddy adjuster and your supervisor are your first points of contact for any question. Do not hesitate to ask — asking before acting is always the right approach in claims, particularly when working with unfamiliar wording versions, waiting period calculations or unusual claim types.
