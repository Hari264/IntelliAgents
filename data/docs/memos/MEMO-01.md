## Section 1 Scope and Effective Period

This memo applies to policies with losses up to 31 December 2023. It is superseded by the applicable Policy Wording for all losses from 1 January 2024 onwards. Adjusters must not apply the room rent rule described in Section 2 to any claim whose date of loss falls on or after 1 January 2024.

For claims with a date of loss on or after 1 January 2024, the room rent limit is governed by Clause 8.1 of the applicable Health Gold Policy Wording (HG-WORD-2024 for losses in 2024, and HG-WORD-2025 for losses from 1 January 2025 onwards). The room rent limit applicable under those wording versions differs from the rule described in this memo.

This memo was issued to ensure consistent adjudication of room rent claims during the period when the Health Gold product operated under a fixed daily cap. It is retained in the document repository for historical reference only.

## Section 2 Room Rent Cap Under the Prior Rule

Under the product terms in force for the period covered by this memo, the room rent cap applicable to all Health Gold policies was a fixed amount of ₹3,000 per day, regardless of the sum insured selected by the Policyholder.

This fixed cap of ₹3,000 per day applied uniformly to all sum insured options: ₹3,00,000; ₹5,00,000; ₹10,00,000; and ₹25,00,000. Unlike the subsequent sum-insured-linked approach introduced in the Policy Wordings effective from 1 January 2024, this rule did not vary by sum insured level.

The proportionate deduction under the room rent excess principle applied in the same manner as described in the Policy Wording: where the Insured Person occupied a room category costing more than ₹3,000 per day, the associated medical expenses (excluding pharmacy, implants and consumables) were reduced proportionately. ICU and ICCU charges were covered at actuals without any daily cap, consistent with the approach in subsequent Policy Wording versions.

## Section 3 Application Instructions

Adjusters handling claims with a date of loss on or before 31 December 2023 must apply the ₹3,000 per day room rent cap described in Section 2, not the room rent provisions of the HG-WORD-2024 or HG-WORD-2025 wording.

To determine the correct rule, check the date of loss on the claim, not the policy start date or renewal date. The date of loss governs which room rent rule applies.

Example: a policyholder with a policy start date of 1 June 2022 who was hospitalised on 10 November 2023 is subject to the ₹3,000 per day cap in this memo. The same policyholder hospitalised on 15 February 2024 would be subject to the room rent limit in the applicable Policy Wording effective from 1 January 2024.

If there is any doubt about the applicable room rent rule for a specific claim, raise the query with the team supervisor before finalising the assessment. Do not apply the HG-WORD-2024 or HG-WORD-2025 room rent provisions to a loss date falling in 2023 or earlier.
