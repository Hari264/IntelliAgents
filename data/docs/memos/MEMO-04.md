## Section 1 Purpose

This memo informs claims and service staff of updates to the Company's Network Hospital list and provides guidance on how to handle claims and cashless requests affected by network changes. The Network Hospital list is reviewed and updated periodically by the Provider Management team in consultation with the third-party administrator.

Policyholders must be directed to the current network list at the time of enquiry, as network membership can change. A hospital's historic status on the network is not determinative; what matters is whether the hospital is a Network Hospital at the time of the Insured Person's admission, as defined in Clause 1.1.4 of the applicable Policy Wording.

All claims processing staff must use the current network list available in the claims management system when assessing cashless pre-authorisation requests or advising policyholders about network availability. The list in the system is updated by the Provider Management team and is the single authoritative source.

## Section 2 Network Changes

The Provider Management team has completed the most recent periodic review of the Network Hospital list. As a result of this review, a number of hospitals have been added to the network and a number have been removed.

Newly added hospitals: hospitals added to the network are eligible for cashless claims from the date their addition is reflected in the claims management system. For Insured Persons who were admitted to a newly listed hospital before the hospital was added to the network, the cashless facility cannot be retrospectively applied; such cases must be processed as reimbursement claims under Clause 7.2.

Removed hospitals: hospitals removed from the network are no longer eligible for the cashless facility from the date of removal. For Insured Persons admitted to a removed hospital before the removal date, cashless claims must be honoured if a valid pre-authorisation was issued before the removal date, in accordance with the pre-authorisation terms already communicated to the hospital.

Hospitals under review: some hospitals are currently under review by the Provider Management team. Cashless requests for hospitals under review should be escalated to the supervisor before a decision is issued.

## Section 3 Claims Handling at Affected Hospitals

When handling a claim or pre-authorisation request, the adjuster must check the network list at the date of the Insured Person's admission, not the date the claim is received.

For cashless pre-authorisation requests: verify network status at the date of admission before issuing any pre-authorisation. If the hospital is not listed as a Network Hospital at the admission date, advise the Insured Person to pay directly and submit a reimbursement claim under Clause 7.2 of the applicable Policy Wording. Log the response in the claims management system.

For reimbursement claims from non-network hospitals: the reimbursement route is available regardless of whether the hospital is on the network. The amount payable will be assessed under Clause 7.2 on the same basis as if the Insured Person had been treated at a Network Hospital — subject to all applicable limits, sub-limits and deductions. The cashless facility not being available does not reduce the reimbursement entitlement.

If a policyholder enquires whether a specific hospital is on the network, always direct them to the Company's helpline or website to check the current network list, rather than confirming from memory. Network status can change and any incorrect advice may create a complaint.
