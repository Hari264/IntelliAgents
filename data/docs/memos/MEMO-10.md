## Section 1 Scope and Effective Period

This memo applies to forms used up to 31 December 2023. It is superseded by the current pre-authorisation process for all requests submitted from 1 January 2024 onwards. For the current pre-authorisation process, refer to SOP-CASHLESS-01 and SOP-PREAUTH-02. This memo is retained in the document repository solely for historical reference in the event that a query arises about a cashless claim processed before 1 January 2024.

The instructions in this memo must not be applied to any pre-authorisation request received after 31 December 2023. If there is any uncertainty about the applicable process for a specific pre-authorisation, the team supervisor must be consulted before proceeding.

## Section 2 Old Form Layout

The pre-authorisation form in use up to 31 December 2023 was a paper-based form issued by the Company to its Network Hospitals. The form had five labelled sections, each of which was completed by the hospital before submission to the Company.

Section A — Patient and Policy Details: this section captured the patient's name, date of birth, the policy number, the name of the Policyholder and the hospital's name and registration code. The hospital was required to attach a copy of the Insured Person's identity document to the form.

Section B — Admission Details: this section recorded the proposed admission date, whether the admission was planned or an emergency, and the name of the treating physician.

Section C — Clinical Information: this section required the hospital to state the provisional diagnosis, the proposed treatment or surgical procedure, and the anticipated duration of the in-patient stay. A brief clinical history field was included.

Section D — Cost Estimate: this section required the hospital to provide an estimated breakdown of the expected charges, categorised by type. No pre-set tariff values or reference amounts appeared on the form itself; the fields were free-text entry for the hospital to complete.

Section E — Hospital Declaration: this section contained a declaration to be signed by the hospital's authorised representative confirming the accuracy of the information provided.

## Section 3 Transition to Current Process

The paper-based form described in Section 2 was replaced from 1 January 2024 with a digital pre-authorisation submission process, through the Company's designated third-party administrator portal and the claims management system.

3.1 The digital process provides structured fields that guide the hospital through the same information categories as the old form, but with enhanced validation and faster routing to the adjuster team.

3.2 Any historical claim from the period covered by this memo that is revisited — for example, as part of a reopening under SOP-REOPEN-01 or a quality audit under SOP-QUALITY-01 — should be assessed using the terms and processes that were applicable at the time of the original submission.

3.3 Adjusters who have questions about the content of a pre-authorisation form submitted before 31 December 2023 should contact the team supervisor, who can access the archived form records if required.
