## 5.1 Health Gold — Facts Common to v2024 and v2025

### Definitions (Clauses 1.1 and 1.2)

- **Hospital**: a registered institution with in-patient facilities, a qualified medical officer on duty round the clock, and a minimum of ten in-patient beds.
- **Insured Person**: any individual named in the Policy Schedule and covered under this Policy.
- **Policy Schedule**: the schedule issued by the Company forming part of the Policy, setting out the name of the insured, sum insured, cover period, and any riders or endorsements.
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED)**: any condition diagnosed or treated within 48 months before the policy start date.
- **Day Care**: a surgical procedure or treatment listed on the Company's Day Care list that is completed within less than 24 hours of hospitalisation due to technological advances.
- **Network Hospital**: a hospital that has a current cashless arrangement with the Company.
- **Continuous Cover**: uninterrupted health insurance cover, including portability credit from a prior insurer.
- **Arthroplasty**: surgical replacement of a joint with a prosthetic implant; also known as joint replacement surgery.

### Coverage

- **In-patient Hospitalisation (Clause 2.1)**: admission for at least 24 continuous hours; treatment must be medically necessary.
- **Pre-hospitalisation (Clause 2.2)**: expenses incurred up to 30 days before the date of admission.
- **Post-hospitalisation (Clause 2.2)**: expenses incurred up to 60 days after the date of discharge.
- **Day Care Procedures (Clause 2.3)**: procedures on the Company's Day Care list; the 24-hour minimum does not apply.
- **Domiciliary Hospitalisation (Clause 2.4)**: covered if treatment at home lasts more than 3 consecutive days and hospitalisation would otherwise have been required.
- **Road Ambulance (Clause 2.5)**: up to ₹2,000 per hospitalisation.

### Sum Insured Options (Clause 3.1)

₹3,00,000 | ₹5,00,000 | ₹10,00,000 | ₹25,00,000

### Sub-limits (Clause 3.2)

- Cataract: ₹40,000 per eye
- Joint replacement (arthroplasty): ₹3,00,000 per joint

### Waiting Periods — Common to Both Versions

- **Initial Waiting Period (Clause 4.1.1)**: 1 month from the policy start date for all illnesses; accidental injuries are covered from day one.
- **Cataract (Clause 4.2.2)**: 24 months.
- **Hernia (Clause 4.2.3)**: 24 months.
- **Bariatric Surgery (Clause 4.2.4)**: 36 months.

### Exclusions

- **Permanent Exclusions (Clause 5.1)**: war, nuclear contamination, intentional self-injury, and related events.
- **Specific Exclusions (Clause 5.2)**: substance abuse, hazardous sports, unproven obesity treatment outside Clause 4.2.4.
- **Experimental and Unproven Treatment (Clause 5.3)**: excluded.

### Claim Process

- **Claim Notification (Clause 6.1)**: planned hospitalisation — at least 48 hours before admission; emergency hospitalisation — within 24 hours of admission.
- **Reimbursement Documents (Clause 6.2)**: to be submitted within 30 days of discharge.
- **Cashless Facility (Clause 7.1)**: available only at Network Hospitals, subject to pre-authorisation.
- **Reimbursement Claims (Clause 7.2)**: for treatment at non-network hospitals.

### Room Rent

- **ICU (Clause 8.1)**: actuals with no cap in both v2024 and v2025.
- **Proportionate Deduction (Clause 8.2)**: if the room category taken exceeds the eligible limit, associated medical expenses (excluding pharmacy, implants and consumables) are reduced in the same proportion as the room rent excess.

### Renewal and Portability

- **Grace Period (Clause 9.1)**: 30 days; no cover for losses incurred during the grace period.
- **Portability (Clause 9.2)**: credit for waiting periods already completed under prior continuous cover with another insurer; portability request must be made at least 45 days before renewal.

### Grievance Redressal (Clause 10.1)

- Grievance acknowledged within 3 working days of receipt; resolved within 14 days.
- Escalation available to the Insurance Ombudsman if unresolved.

## 5.4 Regulator Facts (National Insurance Regulator — NIR)

- **Claim settlement**: within 30 days of receipt of the last necessary document. Where investigation is required, within 45 days.
- **Cashless pre-authorisation**: decision within 1 hour of receipt of the request; final discharge authorisation within 3 hours of receipt of the final bill.
- **Rejection communication**: reasons must be communicated in writing within 15 days of receipt of the last necessary document, citing the specific policy clause.
- **Interest on delayed settlement**: 2% above the prevailing bank rate for the period of delay.
- **Grievance timelines**: acknowledged within 3 working days; resolved within 14 days. Escalation to the Insurance Ombudsman is available thereafter.

## 5.5 Fraud Facts (RESTRICTED — Appears Only in FRD-01, FRD-02, FRD-03)

These facts must not appear in any other indexed document. They are reproduced here for team orientation only.

- **FT-01**: Claimed amount above ₹5,00,000 AND date of loss within 90 days of policy start → mandatory SIU referral.
- **FT-02**: 3 or more claims by the same insured person in 12 months → enhanced review.
- **FT-03**: Billed amount above 150% of the applicable network tariff for the procedure → billing audit.
- **FT-04**: Hospital appearing on the internal provider watchlist → field visit required before cashless approval.
- **FT-05**: Red-flag score 70 or above → SIU referral; 40–69 → senior adjuster review; below 40 → normal processing.
- The customer must never be told that a fraud review is in progress (see SCR-GEN-01 for CL-601 script).

## 5.6 Underwriting Facts

- **Age bands**: 18–35 | 36–45 | 46–55 | 56–65 | 66 and above.
- **Pre-policy medical tests**: mandatory from age 46 onwards; test results are valid for 6 months from the date of the test.
- **PED declaration**: the proposer must declare all pre-existing diseases on the proposal form, including the diagnosis date and details of treatment.
- **Non-disclosure consequence**: referral to the underwriting team; may result in cancellation of the policy, as per NIR Circular summary CIR-03.
- **Occupational classes for Group Health (UW-OCC-01)**:
  - Class 1 — office, clerical, administrative: no loading.
  - Class 2 — light warehousing, retail, logistics: 10% loading.
  - Class 3 — heavy manufacturing, chemical handling, fireworks, fuel depots: 25% loading.
  - Class 4 — mining, explosives: refer to the Chief Underwriter; decline by default.
- Any occupation not explicitly listed must be referred to the Chief Underwriter and must not be assigned a class by analogy.
